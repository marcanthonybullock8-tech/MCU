## THE PHASE 5 ARC: "TIME RUNS OUT" (Hickman, 2014–15)
- **Cap remembers** the Illuminati's mind wipe (the end of Phase 4), and the heroes **split** again.
- **The Cabal**: **Namor** leads a group that **destroys other Earths without hesitation.** On 616 its members were **Thanos**, Black Swan, Maximus, Terrax, Proxima Midnight and Corvus Glaive.
- **The Beyonders** are **killing the gods of the multiverse**: the Celestials, the Living Tribunal.
- **Doom and the Molecule Man** go after the Beyonders.
- **The final shot of Phase 5:** everything goes **white**, then **"SECRET WARS."**

## PHASE 5 FILM #1: ANT-MAN AND THE WASP: RAGE OF ULTRON
- **Release date: Friday, February 14, 2025.** Valentine's Day, for the franchise's married couple.
- **Comics:** ***Avengers: Rage of Ultron*** (2015 original graphic novel): **Ultron merges with Hank Pym.** Plus ***Marvel Premiere* #47** (1979): **Scott Lang becomes Ant-Man.**
