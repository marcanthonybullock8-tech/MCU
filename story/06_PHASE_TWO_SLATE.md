## PHASE 2 FILM #1: IRON MAN 3
- **Release date: Friday, May 3, 2013.** The first Friday in May, Marvel's summer-opening slot for the fifth year in a row. It's one year after *The Avengers*, and it comes two weeks ahead of *Star Trek Into Darkness* (May 17). It's also Disney's first solo Marvel release after *The Avengers*.
- **Why Phase 2 opens with Iron Man:** Tony started everything, and after *The Avengers* he pays for everything. Phase 2 opens by asking what the team's founder does after saving the world, and gives Tony his biggest power upgrade from 616: **Extremis.**

## PHASE 2 FILM #2: THOR: STORMBREAKER
- **Release date: Friday, July 26, 2013.** It's the heart of summer. In the real world *The Wolverine* held this slot, but that film doesn't exist in our reality, so the weekend is open. It comes twelve weeks after *Iron Man 3* and a week after *Pacific Rim* (July 12) and *R.I.P.D.* (July 19).
- **Comics:** Walter Simonson's *Thor* #337–349: **Beta Ray Bill** and **Malekith**.

## PHASE 2 FILM #3: FANTASTIC FOUR
- **Character(s):** **The Fantastic Four**: Mr. Fantastic, the Invisible Woman, the Human Torch and the Thing. First appearance: ***Fantastic Four* #1** (November 1961), Stan Lee & Jack Kirby.
- **Why third:**
  1. **They're Marvel's first family.** *FF* #1 started the Marvel Age of Comics, two years before the Avengers and the X-Men. We held them back on purpose, and now they arrive.
  2. **They bring the cosmos.** Through the FF, 616 introduces **the Watcher, the Silver Surfer and Galactus.** On 616, **the Silver Surfer is a key player in *The Infinity Gauntlet***. Phase 2 needs him on the board.
  3. **They bring Doctor Doom**, the greatest villain Marvel has.
  4. **They balance the X-Men.** The FF are the heroes the public **loves**. The X-Men are the ones it fears.
- **Release date: Friday, November 8, 2013.** Marvel's fall slot, **52 years almost to the month** after *FF* #1 (November 1961), in the slot *Thor: The Dark World* held in the real world (a film that doesn't exist in ours), two weeks ahead of *The Hunger Games: Catching Fire* (November 22).
- **⏳ Casting:** the FF are played by real actors, and the showrunner picks. The recommendations are in the dossier.

## PHASE 2 FILM #4: CAPTAIN AMERICA: THE WINTER SOLDIER
- **Release date: Friday, April 4, 2014.** Early spring, with a clear runway before the summer season. It's Cap's first film set in the present day.
- **Comics:** Ed Brubaker's "Winter Soldier," *Captain America* vol. 5 #1–14.
