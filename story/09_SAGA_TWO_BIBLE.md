## 2. THE BIG BAD: **VICTOR VON DOOM**
**First appearance:** *Fantastic Four* #5 (July 1962), Stan Lee & Jack Kirby.
**Played by:** **Robert Downey Jr.** (locked in *Fantastic Four*, 2013). In our reality Doom is his signature Marvel role.

### Why Doom
1. **He's Marvel's greatest villain.** He's a genius equal to Reed Richards, a **master sorcerer**, **the absolute monarch of Latveria**, and a man who has **stolen the Power Cosmic** and **taken godhood.** No other 616 villain has done as much.
2. **He's already on the board.** We met him in *Fantastic Four* (2013), when he beat all four of them in round one and **escaped by sorcery.** His mid-credit scene set up his **time platform.** In *Avengers Forever* (2019) he **went for the Infinity Gauntlet himself.** Doom has been watching the heroes lose since 2013.
3. **He's the opposite of Thanos.** Thanos was a nihilist who wanted to be loved by Death. **Doom is a man who believes he should rule everything, and he might be right.** He wants to **save the universe**, and to be the one who owns it.
4. **The 616 story is already written.** Jonathan Hickman's run (*Avengers* / *New Avengers*, 2012–15) leads to ***Secret Wars*** (2015–16), where **the multiverse dies** and **Doom rebuilds what's left as its God.** It's the definitive modern 616 epic, and it grows out of **the Illuminati**, whom we already set up in *Planet Hulk* (2018).
5. **Nobody is nerfed, and that includes the villain.** At the saga's height, **Doom takes the power of the Beyonders** and becomes **God Emperor Doom.** The heroes have to beat something **even bigger than the Infinity Gauntlet.**

### Comic-accurate canon (locked)
- **Origin:** a Romani boy from **Latveria**. His mother **Cynthia** made a pact with **Mephisto** and died for it. **Mephisto already exists in our MCU** (Ciarán Hinds), and **Doom's lifelong goal is to free his mother's soul from Mephisto.**
- **At Empire State University** his forbidden machine exploded after **Reed warned him.** He trained with **Tibetan monks**, put on **the mask while it was still red-hot**, and took **Latveria.**
- **Science and sorcery:** Doom is both. He's **Sorcerer Supreme-class** (on 616 he took the title in 2015), and his technology rivals Reed's.
- **His code:** Doom **keeps his word**, protects **Latveria's people**, and **never lets anyone else kill Reed Richards.**
- **Doom isn't evil in his own eyes.** He's **the only one competent enough to save everything.**

### The cosmic threat behind him: **the Beyonders and the Incursions**
- **Incursions**: two universes' Earths **collide**, and **both universes die** unless one of the two Earths is destroyed first (Hickman's *New Avengers*, 2013).
- **The Beyonders**, beings from beyond the multiverse, are **deliberately destroying it** (*Avengers*/*New Avengers*, 2015).
- **The Molecule Man** (Owen Reece) is **the bomb they planted in every universe**, and **Doom's key to beating them** (*Secret Wars*, 2015).
- **The Illuminati have to decide which worlds die**, and that drives them apart. **Black Panther** vs. **Namor.** **Cap** vs. **Tony.**

## 3. THE SAGA NAME: **THE SECRET WARS SAGA**

**Why this name:**
- It's taken straight from 616: ***Secret Wars*** (1984–85, Jim Shooter, **the Beyonder**, **Battleworld**, **Doom steals the Beyonder's power**) and ***Secret Wars*** (2015–16, Jonathan Hickman, **the multiverse ends**, **God Emperor Doom**). **Both Secret Wars climax with Doom becoming a god.**
- Like "the Infinity Saga," it's **the name of a story, not a franchise.** The public won't hear it until the right moment.
- It fits the secrecy that runs through the whole plan: **the Illuminati's secret war** to save reality, fought behind the backs of the heroes who trust them.

### The three-phase arc (following Hickman's structure)
| Phase | Hickman arc | Where the saga is |
|---|---|---|
| **Phase 4 (2021–24)** | ***"Everything Dies"*** (*Avengers*/*New Avengers* 2012–14) | **The first Incursion.** The **Illuminati** re-form in secret. **The multiverse is revealed.** Doom **watches, and prepares.** |
| **Phase 5 (2025–27)** | ***"Time Runs Out"*** (2015) | **The Illuminati and the Avengers go to war over which worlds die.** Doom **hunts the Beyonders.** The universes run out. |
| **Phase 6 (2028–31)** | ***"Secret Wars"*** (2015–16) and ***Secret Wars*** (1984) | **The multiverse ends.** **Battleworld.** **God Emperor Doom.** The heroes' final war against a god who **did** save everything. The ending: **Reed rebuilds the multiverse, and Doom's face is healed.** |

### Seeding plan
- **Phase 4:** a **second Earth appears in the sky** over Wakanda (the first Incursion, *New Avengers* #1, 2013). T'Challa calls the Illuminati. **The Black Swan.** Doom's **time platform** comes back. Mephisto and Doom **start their war over his mother's soul.**
- **Phase 5:** "Time Runs Out." The **Cabal** (Namor's Earth-destroyers) splits from the heroes. **Doom meets the Beyonders.** **The last Incursion begins.**
- **Final shot of Phase 5:** everything goes **white**, then a single title card: ***"SECRET WARS."***
- **Phase 6:** Battleworld. God Emperor Doom. The saga's two-part finale.
