## PHASE 3 FILM #1: CAPTAIN AMERICA: CIVIL WAR
- **Release date: Friday, May 6, 2016.** The first Friday in May, Marvel's summer-opening slot for the ninth year in a row.
- **Comics:** Mark Millar & Steve McNiven's ***Civil War* #1–7** (2006–07), the biggest 616 event of the modern era.
- **Why Phase 3 opens with Cap:** Thanos has the Gauntlet. Before the universe's greatest threat arrives, **Earth's heroes break apart.** When the Gauntlet comes, the Avengers aren't a team anymore.
