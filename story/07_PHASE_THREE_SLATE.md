## PHASE 3 FILM #1: CAPTAIN AMERICA: CIVIL WAR
- **Release date: Friday, May 6, 2016.** The first Friday in May, Marvel's summer-opening slot for the ninth year in a row.
- **Comics:** Mark Millar & Steve McNiven's ***Civil War* #1–7** (2006–07), the biggest 616 event of the modern era.
- **Why Phase 3 opens with Cap:** Thanos has the Gauntlet. Before the universe's greatest threat arrives, **Earth's heroes break apart.** When the Gauntlet comes, the Avengers aren't a team anymore.

## PHASE 3 FILM #2: BLACK PANTHER
- **Character:** T'Challa / Black Panther. First appearance: ***Fantastic Four* #52** (July 1966), Stan Lee & Jack Kirby.
- **Why second:** he was introduced in *Ultron Unlimited* and *Civil War*, and **Wakanda** has to be established before *The Infinity Gauntlet*. **"Panther's Rage"** was **Marvel's first graphic novel-length story**, and it's a king's epic.
- **Release date: Friday, July 8, 2016.** Mid-summer, nine weeks after *Civil War*.

## PHASE 3 FILM #3: DOCTOR STRANGE
- **Character:** Dr. Stephen Strange. First appearance: ***Strange Tales* #110** (July 1963), Stan Lee & Steve Ditko.
- **Why third:** on 616, **Doctor Strange is at the center of *The Infinity Gauntlet***. His Sanctum is where the heroes gather. The MCU also needs **magic** to stand against **Mephisto**, who's whispering to Thanos.
- **Release date: Friday, November 4, 2016.** Marvel's fall slot.

## PHASE 3 FILM #4: GUARDIANS OF THE GALAXY: COSMIC AVENGERS
- **Release date: Friday, May 5, 2017.** The first Friday in May, Marvel's summer opener.
- **Comics:** Bendis's *Guardians* vol. 3 #1–3, "Cosmic Avengers"; Iron Man joins the Guardians.

## PHASE 3 FILM #5: WOLVERINE
- **Character:** Logan / Wolverine. First appearance: ***The Incredible Hulk* #180–181** (1974), Len Wein, Roy Thomas, Herb Trimpe & John Romita Sr.
- **Why fifth:** **he's the most popular X-Man**, and we own him. On 616, he **faces Thanos in *The Infinity Gauntlet***, and his debut **against the Hulk** connects him straight to the Avengers side of the MCU. The Claremont/Miller series is one of the best solo stories in Marvel history.
- **Release date: Friday, July 28, 2017.** Late summer, twelve weeks after *Cosmic Avengers*.
