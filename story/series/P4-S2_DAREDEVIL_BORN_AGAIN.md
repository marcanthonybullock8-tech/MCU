## 4. STORY
| Field | Entry |
|---|---|
| **Logline** | The Kingpin learns who Daredevil really is, and he doesn't kill Matt Murdock. He takes everything from him and waits to see what's left. |
| **Theme** | **Resurrection.** Faith, family, and the man who stands back up with nothing left. |
| **Villain / antagonist** | **Wilson Fisk, the Kingpin** |

### What the season is about
- **Episodes 1–2:** Karen Page, addicted in Mexico, sells a secret, and **Fisk learns Matt Murdock is Daredevil.** Within a week, Matt's **bank accounts are frozen**, he's **disbarred**, and **his brownstone explodes.**
- **Episodes 3–4:** Matt, homeless and paranoid, **goes after Fisk and is beaten**, stabbed, and **dumped in the East River in a cab.** He wakes up in a church, where **Sister Maggie** has saved him. She's **his mother.**
- **Episodes 5–6:** **Born again.** Matt rebuilds his body and his mind, **on nothing.** **Karen** comes home, clean and ashamed. They start over.
- **Episodes 7–9:** Fisk, desperate, sends out **Nuke**. **Captain America** arrives because a super-soldier is involved: *"That's one of ours, son. And he's been poisoned."* Daredevil and Cap stop Nuke. Fisk's ties to Nuke come out, and **the Kingpin's empire begins to crack.**
- **Final scene:** Matt and Karen walk through Hell's Kitchen together, broke and happy.
