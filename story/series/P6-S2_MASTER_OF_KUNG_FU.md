## 4. STORY
| Field | Entry |
|---|---|
| **Logline** | In a domain of Battleworld ruled by the greatest martial-arts tyrant who ever lived, his son enters the tournament that decides who will rule next, to beat his father with his bare hands. |
| **Theme** | **Fathers and sons.** Shang-Chi won't be his father, and he won't be God Doom's either. |
| **Villain / antagonist** | **ZHENG ZU** |

### What the season is about
- **Episodes 1–2:** **K'un-Lun**, a city of **martial-arts schools**, ruled by **Emperor Zheng Zu**. **Shang-Chi**, his estranged son, **teaches the poor to fight.** **Leiko Wu** and the rebels want him to lead them.
- **Episodes 3–4:** **The tournament.** Every school's champion **fights for the throne.** **Shang-Chi enters.**
- **Episodes 5–6:** **Shang-Chi's half-siblings**, each **a champion of a different school**, fight **him** and **each other.** **Zheng Zu's elixir** is **the secret of his power.**
- **Episode 7:** **Father and son**, alone, **in the Emperor's garden.**
- **Episode 8:** **Shang-Chi beats Zheng Zu** without killing him. **K'un-Lun is free.**
