## 4. STORY
| Field | Entry |
|---|---|
| **Logline** | In Doom's Battleworld, every Ghost Rider who ever lived is chained to a racetrack from hell, and the crowd's new favorite is a kid from East L.A. who never wanted to be a hero. |
| **Theme** | **Vengeance vs. freedom.** The Spirits of Vengeance have been used as entertainment. Now they want something else. |
| **Villain / antagonist** | **ARCADE** (main); **God Doom** watching |

### What the season is about
- **Episodes 1–2:** **The Killiseum.** Every week, **Ghost Riders race to the death.** **Robbie Reyes** is the **champion.** In flashback: **Robbie**, his brother **Gabe**, and **the night he died in his Charger.**
- **Episodes 3–4:** **Johnny Blaze**, **the old legend**, is **tired of winning.** His flashback is **Crash Simpson** and **the deal with the devil.** **Danny**, **Alejandra** and **Carter Slade** join them.
- **Episode 5: "Hellfire Night."** **The Halloween race.** Arcade **changes the rules.** The racers **stop racing each other** and **start protecting each other.**
- **Episodes 6–7:** **Murderworld.** Arcade throws **every trap he has** at them. **Johnny unleashes Zarathos.**
- **Episode 8:** **The Ghost Racers rise.** They **turn the Killiseum against Arcade** and **ride out of it together.** **Doom**, from his box, **watches them go.** *"Let them run. There's nowhere to run to."*
