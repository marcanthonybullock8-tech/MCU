### 3A. COMIC ACCURACY REPORT
| Field | Entry |
|---|---|
| **Overall accuracy score** | **97%.** G. Willow Wilson's first year is adapted almost panel for panel. |

**Straight from the page:**
- **Kamala Khan**, 16, a **Pakistani-American Muslim** teen in **Jersey City**, writes **Avengers fan fiction** and worships **Captain Marvel** (#1).
- **The Terrigen cloud** passes over Jersey City. She goes into a cocoon and comes out an **Inhuman** (#1).
- Her powers: **shapeshifting**: **stretching**, **"embiggening"** (growing huge), shrinking, and **a healing factor** that only works when she's in her normal form (#1–5).
- **Bruno Carrelli**, her best friend, is a science genius at the corner store. **Nakia** is her friend. **Zoe** is the popular girl. Her strict, loving family: **Muneeba, Yusuf, Aamir** (#1–5).
- **The Inventor**, a bizarre villain who is **a clone of Thomas Edison with bird DNA**, kidnaps teenagers to power his machines (#8–11).
- **Wolverine** shows up and teams with her, and she's **starstruck** (#6–7).
- **Lockjaw** comes to live with her, sent by **Medusa** (#12–13).

**Changes and why:**
| Change | Why |
|---|---|
| The Terrigen cloud came from **Black Bolt's bomb** in *The Inhumans* (2022) | MCU continuity. On 616 it did too (*Infinity*). |

## 4. STORY
| Field | Entry |
|---|---|
| **Logline** | A Jersey City teenager who writes fan fiction about the Avengers wakes up from a stone cocoon able to change her shape, and has to become a hero while still making it to curfew. |
| **Theme** | **Being yourself.** Kamala tries to be Captain Marvel, then Wolverine, and finally becomes **herself.** |
| **Villain / antagonist** | **The Inventor** |

### What the season is about
- **Episodes 1–2:** Kamala at home, at school, at the mosque, and online writing Avengers fan fiction. **The Terrigen cloud** passes over. **The cocoon.** She wakes up, **stretches and grows**, and saves a classmate from drowning.
- **Episodes 3–4:** She tries to be a hero in a knock-off Captain Marvel costume, and **fails.** **Wolverine** shows up in the sewers, and she nearly faints. They take down a robot nest together. Logan: *"Kid. Be yourself. Nobody else wants the job."*
- **Episodes 5–6:** **The Inventor** is kidnapping Jersey City's teenagers. **Lockjaw** teleports into her bedroom, sent by **Medusa**, and becomes her dog.
- **Episodes 7–8:** Kamala designs **her own costume** (the scarf, the lightning bolt, her mother's help). The final battle against the Inventor's machines, with **Kamala at building size** protecting her city. She names herself: ***"Ms. Marvel."*** Medusa visits to thank her and offers her a place among the Inhumans. Kamala says: *"I'm from Jersey City."*
