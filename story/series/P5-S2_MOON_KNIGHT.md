### 3A. COMIC ACCURACY REPORT
| Field | Entry |
|---|---|
| **Overall accuracy score** | **96%.** Moench's origin, Sienkiewicz's world and Lemire's asylum are all adapted closely. |

**Straight from the page:**
- **Moon Knight hunts the werewolf Jack Russell** for **the Committee**, then **turns on the Committee** (*WbN* #32–33).
- **Marc Spector**, the son of a rabbi, was a **boxer**, then a **Marine**, then a **mercenary** (*MK* #1).
- **In Sudan, Bushman kills Dr. Peter Alraune** at a dig (*MK* #1).
- **Marc tries to stop him**, and is **beaten and left in the desert** (*MK* #1).
- **Marc dies before the statue of Khonshu and rises again** (*MK* #1).
- **Marc protects Marlene**, the doctor's daughter, and **she becomes his great love** (*MK* #1–38).
- **Frenchie** is Marc's partner from the mercenary days (*MK* #1).
- **Steven Grant, the millionaire** and **Jake Lockley, the cab driver** (*MK* #1–38).
- **Crawley and Gena** are Jake's informants (*MK* #1–38).
- **The crescent darts, truncheon, glider cape and white costume** (*MK* #1 on).
- **Marc wakes up in an asylum**, and **nobody believes he's Moon Knight** (Lemire #1–5).
- **Khonshu tells him it's real** (Lemire #1–5).

**Changes and why:**
| Change | Why |
|---|---|
| The *Werewolf by Night* job comes **after** the origin, not before | **The show opens with Moon Knight in action.** The origin comes in Episode 2. |
| **Marc has been Moon Knight since 2017** (MCU time), in secret | MCU continuity. He's worked **only at street level** until now. |
| **Khonshu is afraid of the Beyonders** | It ties the series to *Time Runs Out*. **Gods are dying** all over the multiverse. |

**How the violence is handled:** TV-MA, **true to Moench and Sienkiewicz.** **The asylum episodes** are treated **with respect for mental illness**, and **Marc's dissociative identity disorder** (616) is written **with care.**

## 4. STORY
| Field | Entry |
|---|---|
| **Logline** | Marc Spector died in the Sudanese desert and came back as the fist of an Egyptian moon god. Now the man who killed him is back in New York, and Marc isn't sure which of his three lives is real. |
| **Theme** | **Identity.** Marc, Steven and Jake are all real, and none of them is whole. |
| **Villain / antagonist** | **Raoul Bushman** (main); **the Committee** |

### What the season is about
- **Episodes 1–2:** **Moon Knight is hired to catch a werewolf** in the Adirondacks, **Jack Russell**, and **lets him go.** Marc and Frenchie **smash the Committee.** Episode 2 goes back to **Sudan**: Bushman, Dr. Alraune, **the desert**, **the statue of Khonshu**, and **Marc's death.**
- **Episodes 3–4:** **Steven Grant's** life of millionaire parties, and **Jake Lockley's** cab and diner, **Crawley and Gena.** The three lives start **leaking into each other.** **Marlene** finds out.
- **Episode 5:** **Bushman comes to New York**, and **kills people Marc loves.**
- **Episode 6: "Lunatic."** **Marc wakes in a psychiatric hospital.** Doctors, pills, a white room. **Everyone says he's never been Moon Knight.** Khonshu, at the window: *"Get up, Marc."*
- **Episode 7:** Marc **escapes**, and he **isn't sure whether it happened.**
- **Episode 8:** **Moon Knight vs. Bushman** on a rooftop **under a full moon.** **Marc beats him** and **doesn't kill him.** Khonshu is furious.
