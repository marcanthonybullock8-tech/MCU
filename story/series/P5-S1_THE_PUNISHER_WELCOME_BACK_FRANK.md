### 3A. COMIC ACCURACY REPORT
| Field | Entry |
|---|---|
| **Overall accuracy score** | **97%.** Ennis and Dillon's run is adapted issue for issue, including its black humor. |

**Straight from the page:**
- **Frank Castle**, a Marine whose **family was murdered in Central Park**, comes back to **wage war on the Gnucci family** (#1).
- **Ma Gnucci** loses her sons, then **her limbs** (Frank feeds her to polar bears at the zoo, and she survives), and **keeps coming** (#1–12).
- **Copycat vigilantes** (a priest, a retired cop, and a stockbroker) try to "be the Punisher" (#4–5).
- **The Russian**, a giant hired killer, **nearly beats Frank** (#6).
- **Detective Martin Soap**, the NYPD's most useless cop, is **put on the Punisher case** (#1–12).
- **Frank's neighbors**, Joan the Mouse, Spacker Dave and Mr. Bumpo, **end up helping him** (#1–12).
- **Daredevil** tries to stop him. **Frank drugs him** (#3).
- **Frank burns down the Gnucci mansion with Ma inside** (#12).

**How the violence is handled:** TV-MA, **true to Ennis's tone**, including his dark comedy. **The violence is never glamorized.** Frank is a **tragic figure**, not a role model.

## 4. STORY
| Field | Entry |
|---|---|
| **Logline** | Frank Castle comes home to New York to kill every member of a crime family, and finds himself with a copycat problem, a useless cop on his tail, and neighbors who won't leave him alone. |
| **Theme** | **War never ends.** Frank's only family now is the one he can't get rid of: the lonely people in his building. |
| **Villain / antagonist** | **Ma Gnucci** (main); **the Russian** |

### What the season is about
- **Episodes 1–3:** Frank's back. The Gnuccis lose three sons in one night. **Ma Gnucci** swears revenge. **Detective Soap** gets the case. **Daredevil** tries to stop him on a rooftop, and Frank drugs him: *"Go home, Red."*
- **Episodes 4–6:** **Copycat vigilantes** show up. Frank shuts them down. Ma Gnucci loses her limbs **at the zoo.** **The Russian** comes for Frank, and **Frank loses.**
- **Episodes 7–9:** Frank recovers **in his neighbor Joan's apartment.** He has something like a home now. The rematch with the Russian ends with Frank winning.
- **Episode 10:** **The Gnucci mansion burns** with Ma inside. Frank leaves the city at dawn. Joan leaves a light on for him.
