## REAL-WORLD CONTEXT (locked)
- **The COVID-19 pandemic** (real history) closed U.S. theaters in **March 2020**. There's **no MCU film in 2020**, which lines up with the gap between the two sagas.
- In 2021 theaters are **reopening slowly.** Disney releases films **in theaters and on Disney+ Premier Access** on the same day (as it did in the real world in 2021).
- **In the MCU's own world there is no pandemic**, as in the real Marvel films. The pandemic affects **the business, not the story.**

## PHASE 4 FILM #1: X-MEN: SECOND GENESIS
- **Release date: Friday, July 9, 2021.** **Why not the first Friday in May:** in 2021 theaters are still reopening, and in the real world Marvel **moved its first post-pandemic film to July 9, 2021** for the same reason. It's the realistic call, and it gives the saga's first film a proper summer opening.
- **Comics:** ***Giant-Size X-Men* #1** (1975), "Second Genesis!", the **all-new, all-different X-Men.**

## PHASE 4 FILM #2: NAMOR THE SUB-MARINER
- **Character:** Namor McKenzie, the Sub-Mariner. First appearance: ***Marvel Comics* #1** (October 1939), Bill Everett. He's **the first Marvel superhero ever published**, older than Captain America.
- **Why second:** **Namor is at the center of Hickman's saga.** He's the Illuminati member who **agrees to destroy other Earths** and **founds the Cabal** with Doom's knowledge. His grief and pride have to be established **before** the Incursions start. His film ends with **the first Incursion over Wakanda.**
- **Release date: Friday, November 5, 2021.** Marvel's fall slot. Theaters have mostly recovered by now, so this is a **theatrical-exclusive release** with a 45-day window before Disney+.
- **⚠️ Real history:** Chadwick Boseman died on August 28, 2020. ✅ **Locked:** T'Challa died in the MCU in 2020, off-screen, of illness. **Shuri (Letitia Wright) is Black Panther**, as on 616, and holds Wakanda's seat among the Illuminati.

## PHASE 4 FILM #3: DOCTOR STRANGE: THE BLACK PRIESTS
- **Character:** **Doctor Strange**, as a **member of the Illuminati**.
- **Why third:** Hickman's *New Avengers* **is** the Illuminati story. Strange is the one who **pays the highest price**: he makes a dark bargain for power and **destroys an entire Earth.** His film is where *Everything Dies* gets real, including **Captain America's mind wipe.**
- **Release date: Friday, May 6, 2022.** The first Friday in May, back in its traditional slot now that theaters have recovered.

## PHASE 4 FILM #4: THE INHUMANS
- **Characters:** **Black Bolt** and the **Inhuman Royal Family**. First appearance: ***Fantastic Four* #45** (December 1965), Lee & Kirby.
- **Why fourth:** **Black Bolt is an Illuminati member**, and Hickman's **Terrigen Bomb** (*Infinity*, 2013) **puts Inhumans all over the planet**, changing the MCU's world permanently. It also sets up **Kamala Khan** (the Jersey City cocoon).
- **Release date: Friday, July 8, 2022.** Mid-summer.

## PHASE 4 FILM #5: SPIDER-MAN: KRAVEN'S LAST HUNT
- **Release date: Friday, December 16, 2022.** A holiday event release, the same slot the real *Spider-Man: No Way Home* used in 2021.
- **Comics:** *ASM Annual* #21 (**the wedding**) and **"Kraven's Last Hunt"** (1987).
