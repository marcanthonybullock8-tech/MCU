## 4. STORY
| Field | Entry |
|---|---|
| **Logline** | When his first students disappear on a living island, Charles Xavier goes around the world to recruit a new team of strangers. They have to become the X-Men in one night, or die. |
| **Theme** | **A second chance.** Every new X-Man was an outcast, and together they're a family. |
| **Villain / antagonist** | **Krakoa** (main); **Count Nefaria** (Act 3) |

### What the movie is about
**ACT ONE: THE LIVING ISLAND**
- **Monday, February 15, 2021.** The **original X-Men** go to a mysterious island in the South Pacific to investigate a mutant signal.
- **They disappear.** Only **Cyclops** makes it back, half-dead.
- **Professor X** knows what he has to do: *"I need a new team. Tonight."*

**ACT TWO: SECOND GENESIS**
- The recruitment montage:
  - **Nightcrawler**: a mob with torches in a Bavarian village. *BAMF.*
  - **Storm**: worshipped as a goddess in the Kenyan highlands (the Stan Lee mural is in the village).
  - **Colossus**: a Russian farm, where he stops a runaway tractor with his chest.
  - **Sunfire**: the Tokyo skyline, and he says no until Xavier mentions Krakoa's threat to Japan.
  - **Thunderbird**: the Arizona desert. *"Why me?"* *"Because you're a warrior."*
  - **Banshee**: an Irish pub, with a smile.
  - **Wolverine**: a Canadian roadhouse (the *Wolverine* post-credit scene, paid off).
- They're strangers, and they fight on the Blackbird on the way there.
- **Krakoa.** The island **comes alive.**

**ACT THREE: THE NEW X-MEN**
- They save the originals. Storm, Banshee and Sunfire **throw Krakoa into orbit.**
- Back at the Mansion, **the originals step away.** **Cyclops stays and leads.** **Sunfire leaves.**
- **Count Nefaria** takes over **NORAD** in Cheyenne Mountain.
- The new team's first mission. Nefaria escapes on a jet. **Thunderbird** grabs on as it takes off and won't let go: *"Not this time."*
- **The jet explodes.** Thunderbird is gone.
- At his grave, Wolverine lights a cigar and says nothing. Storm puts her hand on Cyclops's shoulder: *"We will honor him."*
