### 3A. COMIC ACCURACY REPORT
| Field | Entry |
|---|---|
| **Overall accuracy score** | **94%.** It adapts the Soul World as Starlin wrote it and Warlock's 1990 return. |

**Straight from the page:**
- **Inside the Soul Gem** is **the Soul World**, a **golden paradise** where the captured souls live in peace (*Warlock* #11; *MTIO Annual* #2).
- **Adam Warlock's soul** lives there after the Gem takes it (*MTIO Annual* #2).
- **Gamora's soul** is there too (*Warlock* #15).
- **Pip the Troll**, a cynical, rowdy, lovable troll, is Warlock's friend in the Soul World (*Strange Tales* #179).
- **Warlock, Gamora and Pip** leave the Soul World to fight Thanos (*Silver Surfer* #45–50; *IG* #2).

**Changes and why:**
| Change | Why |
|---|---|
| **The Magus is fought inside the Soul World** | MCU continuity. The Soul Gem took his soul in *Guardians of the Galaxy* (2014). |
| **The Surfer opens the way out** | On 616 the circumstances of their return were different. Here the Power Cosmic connects Earth to the Gem, which ties the Surfer into Warlock's return. |

## 4. STORY
| Field | Entry |
|---|---|
| **Logline** | Trapped inside the Soul Gem on Thanos's hand, a golden god-man, a dead assassin and a foul-mouthed troll have five years to find the one crack in paradise that leads back to a broken universe. |
| **Theme** | **Accepting yourself.** Warlock has to face the Magus, the worst version of who he could be, to become who the universe needs. |
| **Villain / antagonist** | **The Magus** (main); **Thanos** (outside the Gem, beyond reach) |

### What the movie is about
**ACT ONE: PARADISE**
- **The Soul World** is endless, golden and timeless. **Adam Warlock** has watched over its souls since Thanos took his Gem.
- **Gamora** arrives (from the *Infinity Gauntlet* post-credit scene). Then **Pip the Troll**, who has been in here for centuries and will not stop talking.
- Through the Gem, Warlock can **feel the snap**: half the universe, gone.
- He can also see **Thanos** through the Gem, sitting on his throne, with **Death** still not looking at him.

**ACT TWO: THE MAGUS**
- Something is **rotting paradise** from the inside: **the Magus**, whose soul the Gem took in *Guardians of the Galaxy*.
- The Magus wants to **take control of the Soul Gem from within** and use it through Thanos's Gauntlet.
- Warlock, Gamora and Pip are hunted across a Soul World that's falling apart.
- Gamora faces the memory of her father. Pip faces the souls he's wronged.
- Warlock faces **himself.**

**ACT THREE: THE CRACK**
- **Warlock vs. the Magus.** Warlock stops fighting him and **accepts him** as part of himself, and the Magus fades.
- In **Earth time**, **five years** have passed. On Earth, the **Silver Surfer**, grief-stricken, **reaches through the Power Cosmic into the Soul Gem's frequency.**
- There's **a crack** in the golden sky.
- **Warlock, Gamora and Pip** step through it, **out of the Soul Gem and into the ruins of Earth, 2023.**
- The Surfer is waiting, with **Captain America, Iron Man and Thor**, older and grieving.
- **WARLOCK:** *"I know how to beat him. You're not going to like it."*
