## 4. STORY
| Field | Entry |
|---|---|
| **Logline** | At the foot of the World Tree, the man who saved everything and the man who could have saved it better settle the question that started the Secret Wars. |
| **Theme** | **Pride and honesty.** Doom saved the multiverse. He just couldn't admit he wasn't the best man for the job. |
| **Villain / antagonist** | **DOOM**, and **his own pride** |

### What the movie is about
**ACT ONE: THE TREE**
- **The same second** *God Emperor Doom* ended. **Reed Richards and God Doom** face each other **beneath the World Tree.**
- **Reed and Doom fight**, **two old men without masks or powers**, **with their fists.** **Doom is winning.**
- **Sue**, with **Franklin** and **Valeria**, watches **from the throne.**

**ACT TWO: THE QUESTION**
- Reed, bloody, asks: ***"What if I'd had it, Victor? The power. What would I have done?"***
- **A long silence.** ***"...You would have done better."***
- **Owen Reece** looks at Doom. **"Thank you, Victor."** He **takes the power away**, and **gives it to Reed.**
- **Doom** falls to his knees. **The mask** falls off.

**ACT THREE: EVERYTHING LIVES**
- **Reed**, with the Beyonders' power, **rebuilds.** **Franklin** makes worlds, **Valeria** does the math. **The multiverse** comes back **universe by universe.**
- **Reed** looks at **Doom's face** and **heals it.**
- **Reed and Sue** leave **with their children** to **keep building.** **Ben** stays behind: *"Somebody's gotta watch the house."*

**THE EPILOGUE: "EIGHT MONTHS LATER"**
- **Friday, December 19, 2031.** **Earth-616.** **Everybody is back.**
- **Tony Stark** wakes up **under the Helicarrier's wreckage**. **Steve Rogers** is holding out his hand. Tony takes it: *"...Later?"* Steve: *"No. Now."*
- **Peter Parker** walks into **their apartment**, and **MJ** is there.
- **Miles Morales** wakes up **in Brooklyn**, and **his mother is making breakfast.**
- **Old Man Logan** stands **in a New York street**, **looking at a world where nobody lost.**
- **Latveria.** **Victor von Doom**, **his face whole**, looks in a mirror **for the first time since he was a young man.** His mother's voice: *"Victor."* He weeps.
