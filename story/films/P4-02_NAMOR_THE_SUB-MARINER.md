**Straight from the page:**
- **Namor** is the son of **Captain Leonard McKenzie**, a human sea captain, and **Princess Fen** of Atlantis. He's **the first mutant**, with **ankle wings**, and **"Imperius Rex!"** (*Marvel Comics* #1).
- He's **stronger the wetter he is** and weakens on dry land (616).
- **Namorita**, his cousin, **died at Stamford** with the New Warriors (*Civil War* #1).
- **Lady Dorma** is Namor's great love, and she's **murdered by Llyra of Lemuria on their wedding day** (*Sub-Mariner* #37).
- **Attuma**, a barbarian warlord, tries to take Atlantis's throne (*FF* #33).
- **The Illuminati re-form** when **a second Earth appears in the sky over Wakanda** (*New Avengers* vol. 3 #1).
- **Shuri is Black Panther** (*Black Panther* vol. 5).

**Changes and why:**
| Change | Why |

| **Shuri** is at the first Incursion | Real history (Chadwick Boseman's death) and 616 (Shuri as Black Panther). See the note above. |

## 4. STORY
| Field | Entry |
|---|---|
| **Logline** | The first and proudest of Marvel's heroes loses his cousin to the surface world and his bride to a traitor, and becomes the king who will one day agree to destroy entire worlds. |
| **Theme** | **Pride and grief.** Namor has every reason to hate the surface, and this is the film where he starts to. |
| **Villain / antagonist** | **Attuma** (main); **Llyra** (the assassin) |

### What the movie is about
**ACT ONE: IMPERIUS REX**
- **Flashback, 1920:** Captain Leonard McKenzie's Antarctic expedition, Princess Fen, and a baby with wings on his ankles.
- **Monday, June 7, 2021.** Five years after Stamford, **Namor** still grieves **Namorita** in secret, and still blames the surface world.
- He's finally going to marry **Lady Dorma.**
- **Attuma** rises in the deep with a barbarian army and challenges the throne.

**ACT TWO: THE WAR BENEATH**
- **Attuma and Namor** battle across the ocean floor.
- On land, Namor's weakened, and **Attuma nearly kills him.**
- **Charles Xavier** reaches Namor telepathically: *"You're not the only one who's seen strange things in the sky, old friend."* Namor dismisses him.
- Dorma drags Namor into the sea, and he comes back to full strength and **breaks Attuma's army.**

**ACT THREE: THE WEDDING**
- **Saturday, September 18, 2021.** The royal wedding in Atlantis.
- A handmaiden brings Dorma a chalice. **It's Llyra in disguise.** Dorma drinks, and dies in Namor's arms.
- Namor tears through Lemuria to hunt Llyra. **She escapes.**
- At Dorma's funeral, Namor looks up at the surface world with hollow eyes: ***"From this day, Atlantis owes nothing to anyone."***
- **Doctor Doom** comes to Atlantis, alone, to pay his respects: *"We are kings, Namor. We are the only ones who will do what must be done."* Namor doesn't answer, and he doesn't refuse.
