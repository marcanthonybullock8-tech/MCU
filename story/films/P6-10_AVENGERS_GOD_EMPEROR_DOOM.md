## 4. STORY
| Field | Entry |
|---|---|
| **Logline** | Every domain that remembers has risen against God Doom. The dead march, the Thors turn, and a Black Panther with the Infinity Gauntlet climbs the World Tree to strike a god. |
| **Theme** | **You can't make people love you.** Not even if you're God. |
| **Villain / antagonist** | **GOD DOOM** (main); **Thanos**; **the Maker** |

### What the movie is about
**ACT ONE: THE MARCH**
- **Monday, January 6, 2031.** **The rebellion marches** on Doomstadt: **the Thors** in the sky, **the Carol Corps**, **Technopolis's armor**, **Steve's Hulks**, **the X-Men**, **the Ghost Racers.**
- **Namor opens the Shield.** **The dead** walk through.
- **The Maker** makes a deal with **Thanos.**

**ACT TWO: THE FAMILY**
- **Thanos** goes for Doom **himself.** **Doom kills him.**
- **Sue** watches **Doom** and **Reed** fight, and **Franklin says Reed's name.** **She remembers.**
- **Wanda** finally **turns her power on Doom.**
- **The Shield wall**, **Ben Grimm**, **wakes up** and **walks.**

**ACT THREE: THE GAUNTLET**
- **Shuri climbs the World Tree** with **the Gauntlet.**
- **She strikes Doom** with the power of **six Gems.**
- **Doom falls from the throne**, and **stands up.** **He crushes the Gauntlet.**
- **Reed** stands between Doom and everything left. **Owen Reece**, behind Doom, **is watching Reed.** **CUT TO BLACK.** **"CONCLUDES IN *SECRET WARS*."**
