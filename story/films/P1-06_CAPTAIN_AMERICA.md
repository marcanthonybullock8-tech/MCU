### 3A. COMIC ACCURACY REPORT
| Field | Entry |
|---|---|
| **Overall accuracy score** | **97%.** The Golden Age origin is almost exact, joined to the *Avengers* #4 flashback of how Cap was lost. |

**Straight from the page:**
- **Frail Steve Rogers** is rejected by the Army. **Dr. Abraham Erskine** chooses him for **Project: Rebirth.** He's given the **super-soldier serum** and bathed in **Vita-Rays**, and he becomes the **perfect human.**
- **A Nazi spy (Heinz Kruger) assassinates Erskine** straight afterward, and the formula dies with him. **Steve is the only one.**
- **At Camp Lehigh, Virginia,** Steve poses as a **clumsy private.** The camp mascot, **Bucky Barnes**, walks in on him changing into costume and becomes his partner.
- **The first shield is the triangular one** (*CA Comics* #1), and it's later replaced by **the round shield**, a gift from **President Roosevelt** (*CA Comics* #2), made from an alloy **Dr. Myron MacLain** created by accident and could never reproduce.
- **The Red Skull**, Johann Schmidt, is Hitler's right hand. **In WWII he wears a red skull MASK** (Golden Age), so this Skull is a man in a mask. He uses the **Dust of Death.**
- **Cap and Bucky fight beside Sgt. Nick Fury's Howling Commandos** (*Sgt. Fury* #13).
- **The Invaders**: Namor and the android Human Torch appear in a battle montage.
- **April 1945:** Cap and Bucky try to stop **Baron Zemo's** explosive-rigged **drone plane.** Bucky is still on it when it blows up. **Cap falls into the icy North Atlantic** and freezes in suspended animation (*Avengers* #4).

**Changes and why:**
| Change | Why |
|---|---|
| The Red Skull and Zemo both appear in one film | Runtime. They're Cap's two great WWII enemies, and both come straight from the comics. |
| The Invaders are a montage cameo | They're held for a future WWII-era project. Their existence is set up. |
| Steve's age: b. **July 4, 1920** (616) | 616 accurate. Tyrese plays 20–24. |

## 4. STORY
| Field | Entry |
|---|---|
| **Logline** | A frail young man from the Lower East Side becomes the only super-soldier in history. He leads America's fight against the Red Skull and gives everything, including the next sixty-seven years, to win the war. |
| **Theme** | **Courage belongs to the heart, not the body.** Steve was a hero before the serum. The serum just caught up. |
| **Villain / antagonist** | **The Red Skull** (main); **Baron Heinrich Zemo** (the finale) |

### What the movie is about
**ACT ONE: REBIRTH**
- **New York, Monday, December 9, 1940.** **Steve Rogers**, 20, skinny and sick, is rejected for the fourth time. He doesn't give up.
- **Dr. Abraham Erskine** watches him throw himself on a dummy grenade in a training yard and chooses him.
- **Tuesday, March 4, 1941, Project: Rebirth.** The serum and the Vita-Rays turn Steve into **the perfect human.**
- Seconds later a Nazi spy **shoots Erskine dead.** Steve runs him down on foot, but the formula is gone. **There will only be one.**

**ACT TWO: THE SENTINEL OF LIBERTY**
- Steve is secretly stationed at **Camp Lehigh, Virginia**, posing as a clumsy private. The camp mascot, **Bucky Barnes**, catches him changing into his costume, and they become partners.
- The **triangular shield**, and then the **round one**, which **President Roosevelt** gives him in the Oval Office.
- **Pearl Harbor.** Cap and Bucky go to war.
- They team up with **Sgt. Nick Fury's Howling Commandos**, who include Dugan, Gabe Jones and the rest of the 616 Howlers, and they run operations across occupied Europe with **Peggy Carter** of the French Resistance.
- The **Invaders** battle montage: Namor bursts out of the Channel, and a flaming android flies overhead.
- **The Red Skull.** Hitler's terror operative, masked, uses the Dust of Death on his own officers and plans the ruin of the Allies.

**ACT THREE: THE ICE**
- **Normandy, June 6, 1944** (Fury's line from Chapter One). Cap duels the Red Skull in a collapsing bunker. The Skull is caught by his own Dust of Death and **disappears.**
- **Baron Zemo** builds a **drone plane loaded with explosives** aimed at London.
- **Wednesday, April 25, 1945, over the North Atlantic.** Cap and Bucky catch the drone at takeoff. Cap is thrown clear. **Bucky stays on board, trying to disarm it**, and the plane **explodes.**
- Cap falls into the black water and the ice closes over him.
- The last image is **the shield sinking into the dark.**

## 6. WORLD-BUILDING
### The film's own world
- **WWII in the MCU**: Project: Rebirth, **the only super-soldier**, the Howling Commandos, the Invaders.
- **The shield**, and the alloy nobody can reproduce.
- **The Red Skull** and **Zemo**, Cap's permanent enemies.
- **Peggy Carter** and **Bucky**, the two people Steve loses.

### World-building for the wider MCU
| Seed | How it appears | Payoff |
