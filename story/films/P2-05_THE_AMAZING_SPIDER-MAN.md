**Straight from the page:**
- A **radioactive spider** bites Peter at a science exhibition (*AF* #15).
- **Peter builds his own web-shooters and web fluid** (*AF* #15). His webs are **mechanical, not organic.**
- He wins money as a **masked wrestler against "Crusher" Hogan**, becomes a TV star, **lets a burglar escape**, and that same burglar **kills Uncle Ben.** Spider-Man corners him at the **Acme warehouse** (*AF* #15).
- The narration closes with the line: ***"With great power there must also come great responsibility."***
- **J. Jonah Jameson** calls Spider-Man a menace. **Peter sells photos of himself to the *Daily Bugle*** (*ASM* #1–2).
- **Spider-Man tries to join the Fantastic Four**, hoping they pay a salary, and learns they're a **nonprofit** (*ASM* #1).
- **Doctor Octopus**: a lab accident fuses four mechanical arms to Otto Octavius and damages his mind. **He beats Spider-Man in their first fight** (*ASM* #3).
- **Flash Thompson** bullies "Puny Parker." **Liz Allan** is the girl he likes (*ASM* #1). **Betty Brant** works at the *Bugle* (*ASM* #4).

## 4. STORY
| Field | Entry |
|---|---|
| **Logline** | A bullied science nerd from Queens gets the powers of a spider, uses them for himself, and loses the one man who taught him better. |
| **Theme** | ***With great power there must also come great responsibility.*** |
| **Villain / antagonist** | **Doctor Octopus** (main); **the Burglar** (Uncle Ben's killer); **J. Jonah Jameson** (public enemy #1 is *him*, as far as Jameson's concerned) |

### What the movie is about
**ACT ONE: GREAT POWER**
- **Forest Hills, Queens, Monday, February 10, 2014.** **Peter Parker**, 15, a science whiz and a social zero, lives with his elderly **Aunt May** and **Uncle Ben.**
- At a science exhibition on radiation, **a radioactive spider** bites him.
- Wall-crawling, strength, spider-sense. He **builds web-shooters** at his desk.
- He becomes a masked wrestler (he beats **Crusher Hogan**) and then a TV sensation.
- At the studio, **a burglar runs past him. Peter lets him go.** *"Not my job."*
- He comes home to police cars. **Uncle Ben has been shot.**
- Spider-Man hunts the killer to the **Acme warehouse** and unmasks him. It's **the same burglar.**
- Peter walks away into the night. **Narration:** ***"With great power there must also come great responsibility."***

**ACT TWO: THE MENACE**
- Aunt May can't pay the rent. Spider-Man tries to **join the Fantastic Four** for a salary, and Reed tells him they're **a nonprofit.** Johnny laughs so hard he catches fire.
- Peter sells photos of Spider-Man to **J. Jonah Jameson**, who uses them to call Spider-Man **a menace.**
- **Dr. Otto Octavius**'s lab accident leaves four mechanical arms fused to his spine, and the arms change his mind.
- **Round one:** Doctor Octopus **destroys Spider-Man.** Humiliated, Peter nearly quits.

**ACT THREE: RESPONSIBILITY**
- Peter remembers Ben. **He goes back.**
- He designs **a chemical web solution that jams Ock's mechanical arms.**
- **Round two:** a fight up the side of the Oscorp building, then down through the power plant. Spider-Man wins.
- Jameson's front page: ***"SPIDER-MAN: MENACE!"*** Peter looks at it and grins: *"Nice photo."*
- Aunt May, at Ben's grave: *"He was so proud of you."*
