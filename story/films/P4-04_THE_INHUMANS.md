### 3A. COMIC ACCURACY REPORT
| Field | Entry |
|---|---|
| **Overall accuracy score** | **95%.** It combines the Kirby debut, the Jenkins/Lee *Inhumans* and Hickman's Terrigen Bomb. |

**Straight from the page:**
- **The Inhumans** are an ancient offshoot of humanity, **altered by the Kree**, who gain powers through **the Terrigen Mists** (*Thor* #146).
- **Attilan**, their hidden city, and **the royal family**: **Black Bolt** (the silent king), **Medusa** (living hair), **Karnak** (sees the flaw in everything), **Gorgon** (seismic hooves), **Crystal** (elemental control), **Triton** (amphibious), and **Lockjaw** (a teleporting dog).
- **Maximus the Mad**, Black Bolt's brother, **tries to take the throne** (*FF* #45–48).
- **Black Bolt's voice** can **destroy a city.** He doesn't speak (*Thor* #146).
- **Black Bolt joins the Illuminati** (*New Avengers: Illuminati*).
- **Black Bolt detonates the Terrigen Bomb**, and Attilan falls, which **awakens hidden Inhumans all over the Earth** (*Infinity* #4).
- **Crystal and Quicksilver** fall in love (*FF* #131).

**Changes and why:**
| Change | Why |
|---|---|
| Thanos is **not** the reason for the Terrigen Bomb (on 616 his invasion prompted it) | Our Thanos is retired. **Maximus's coup** is what forces Black Bolt's hand. |

## 4. STORY
| Field | Entry |
|---|---|
| **Logline** | The silent king of a hidden race has to stop his mad brother from taking the throne, even if it means speaking the one word that will change the world. |
| **Theme** | **Silence and power.** Black Bolt's greatest strength is not using it. |
| **Villain / antagonist** | **Maximus the Mad** |

### What the movie is about
**ACT ONE: ATTILAN**
- **Saturday, June 11, 2022.** **Attilan**, hidden in the Himalayas. **Black Bolt**, the silent king, and **Medusa**, his queen and voice.
- **Quicksilver**, a guest at the royal court, **falls for Crystal.**
- Black Bolt comes back from a secret meeting (the Illuminati) looking grim. Only Medusa knows where he's been.

**ACT TWO: THE MAD PRINCE**
- **Maximus the Mad** stages a **coup.** He takes the royal guard with mind control, and he's also secretly plotting to **sell Terrigen to the surface world.**
- The royal family escapes on **Lockjaw.** Black Bolt, Medusa, Karnak, Gorgon, Crystal and Triton are scattered **across the world.**
- **Pietro and Wanda** help Crystal get back.

**ACT THREE: THE WORD**
- The royal family **retakes Attilan.** Maximus's final gambit is **a Terrigen Bomb** aimed at the surface world, which will kill any humans it touches.
- **Black Bolt has no choice.** He **flies the bomb high above Attilan**, and **detonates it himself** with **one word.**
- **Attilan falls.** The Terrigen cloud spreads across **the whole planet**, harmless to humans but **awakening latent Inhumans everywhere.**
- Medusa finds Black Bolt in the rubble. He looks at the sky. Then, **softly, for the first time in the film, he speaks**, and nothing breaks: *"Forgive me."*
