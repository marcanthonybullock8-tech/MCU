**Straight from the page:**
- **Shuri faces Bast's trial.** **She's rejected at first**, and then **chosen** after proving her heart (*BP* vol. 5 #1–6).
- **Doom invades Wakanda** with the help of a traitor faction (*Doomwar* #1).
- **Doom takes the vibranium vaults** and begins **fusing vibranium with sorcery** (*Doomwar* #2–4).
- **To stop him, the Black Panther makes all of Wakanda's processed vibranium inert**, which **ruins Wakanda's economy** (*Doomwar* #6).

**Changes and why:**
| Change | Why |
|---|---|
| **Shuri leads the war** instead of T'Challa | On 616, **T'Challa** led *Doomwar* with Shuri beside him. Following real history and the locked showrunner decision, **Shuri is Black Panther.** |
| **Doom's reason** is to **build a machine that can survive the Incursions** | It ties the film into the saga. Doom isn't trying to conquer Wakanda. **He's preparing for the end of everything.** |

## 4. STORY
| Field | Entry |
|---|---|
| **Logline** | A grieving young queen, not yet sure the gods have chosen her, has to protect Wakanda's heart from the most dangerous man alive, even if it means breaking it herself. |
| **Theme** | **Grief and sacrifice.** Shuri loses her brother, and she's willing to give up Wakanda's wealth to save it. |
| **Villain / antagonist** | **DOCTOR DOOM** |

### What the movie is about
**ACT ONE: THE PANTHER'S TRIAL**
- **Monday, June 12, 2023.** A memorial to **T'Challa** at the Warrior Falls. **Shuri** wears the suit, and **Bast still hasn't spoken to her.**
- **She fails Bast's trial.** The Panther god **turns away.**
- **Queen Mother Ramonda**: *"The gods do not choose us for what we can do. They choose us for what we will give."*

**ACT TWO: DOOMWAR**
- **Doctor Doom invades Wakanda.** A traitor faction opens the gates. Doombots are in the Golden City.
- **Doom takes the vibranium vaults.** He begins **fusing vibranium with magic**, building **something enormous.**
- Shuri faces Doom, and **he beats her easily**: *"You are a child playing at kingship. Your brother at least understood what a king must do."*
- The **Illuminati** (Tony and Reed) arrive to help. Doom **drives them back.**

**ACT THREE: WAKANDA FOREVER**
- Shuri understands what Doom is building: **a machine to survive the end of the universe.** He needs **all of Wakanda's vibranium** to do it.
- **She makes her choice.** In the heart of the vault, Shuri sends a single command through Wakanda's network, and **every piece of processed vibranium in the nation goes inert.** **Wakanda's fortune is gone**, and so is Doom's machine.
- Doom stares at the dead metal. Then, for the first time, **he bows his head to her**, one monarch to another: *"A worthy sacrifice. You are a queen after all."* He leaves **undefeated.**
- Shuri kneels in the empty vault. **Bast speaks**, and **chooses her.**
- **The final image:** Shuri at T'Challa's tomb. *"Wakanda forever, brother."*
