### 3A. COMIC ACCURACY REPORT
| Field | Entry |
|---|---|
| **Overall accuracy score** | **95%.** It combines the 2008 team formation with Starlin's 1975 Magus Saga. Every character comes from 616. |

**Straight from the page:**
- **Gamora** is **the last of the Zen-Whoberi**. **Thanos took her as a child** and made her "**the deadliest woman in the galaxy**" (*Strange Tales* #180).
- **Drax the Destroyer**: **Arthur Douglas**, an Earthman killed by Thanos, whose spirit was put into a new body by **Mentor and Kronos** for one purpose: **to kill Thanos** (*Iron Man* #55).
- **Star-Lord**: **Peter Quill**, a half-human boy whose mother was murdered by alien invaders. He grew up to be **a space-faring outlaw** (*Marvel Preview* #4).
- **Rocket** (a gun-toting raccoon from **Halfworld**) and **Groot** (a *Flora colossus*, **"I am Groot"**).
- **Adam Warlock** carries **the Soul Gem on his forehead.** The **Magus** is **his own evil future self**, who rules the **Universal Church of Truth.** **Thanos helps Warlock** against the Magus **for his own reasons** (the Magus Saga).
- **Nebula** claims to be **Thanos's granddaughter** (616) and hates him and Gamora.
- **Mantis** is an empath and martial artist.

**Changes and why:**
| Change | Why |
|---|---|
| The 2008 team forms during the 1975 Magus Saga | It puts the Guardians and Thanos's family in the same story |
| Quill's age (35) | Matches Pratt; the 616 details are the same |

## 4. STORY
| Field | Entry |
|---|---|
| **Logline** | An Earth-born outlaw, a raccoon, a tree, a vengeful brute and the daughter of the Mad Titan have to save the galaxy from a god-man's evil future self, with Thanos watching the whole time. |
| **Theme** | **Family you choose.** Every Guardian has lost a family, and they find one in each other. |
| **Villain / antagonist** | **The Magus** and the **Universal Church of Truth** (main); **Nebula**; **Thanos** (in the shadows) |

### What the movie is about
**ACT ONE: THE BARGAIN**
- **1988, Missouri:** young Peter Quill watches **alien invaders kill his mother.** He's taken into space.
- **Present day (Monday, May 19, 2014):** **Star-Lord**, an outlaw, steals a relic from a temple, and it turns out to be the location of **Adam Warlock's cocoon.**
- Every bounty hunter in the galaxy comes after him: **Rocket and Groot**, and **Gamora**, who has been sent by **Thanos.**
- **Drax** hears the name "Thanos" in a Knowhere bar and charges.
- The four of them, and eventually **Mantis**, end up running for their lives together.

**ACT TWO: WARLOCK**
- The cocoon opens, and **Adam Warlock** comes out with a **green Gem burning on his brow.**
- Warlock warns them about the **Universal Church of Truth**, which is led by **the Magus**, his own future self and a tyrant who worships himself.
- The Church hunts the team. **Nebula**, the Magus's hired pirate, fights Gamora.
- **Thanos appears.** He offers **an alliance** against the Magus. Drax attacks him on sight, and **Thanos swats him aside with one hand.**
- **Mantis**, touching Thanos's mind: *"He's in love. And it's the most frightening thing I've ever felt."*

**ACT THREE: GUARDIANS**
- The final assault on the Church's cathedral world, with Thanos's forces fighting alongside the heroes.
- **Warlock vs. the Magus.** Warlock uses **the Soul Gem on his own future.** The Magus is **drawn into the Gem**, screaming.
- **Thanos** asks for the **Soul Gem.** Warlock refuses. Thanos smiles: *"In time, Warlock."* and leaves.
- Gamora doesn't go with him. **She stays.**
- Quill looks at the six of them: *"What do we call ourselves?"* Rocket: *"Something stupid, probably."* Quill: ***"The Guardians of the Galaxy."***
