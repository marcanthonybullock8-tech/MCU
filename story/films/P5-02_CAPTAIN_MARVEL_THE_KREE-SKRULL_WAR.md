## 4. STORY
| Field | Entry |
|---|---|
| **Logline** | Two alien empires bring their ancient war to Earth, and Captain Marvel, the only person who belongs to both worlds, has to stop it before humanity is caught in the crossfire. |
| **Theme** | **Belonging.** Carol is human and Kree. Kamala is human and Inhuman. Who's their side? **Earth.** |
| **Villain / antagonist** | **Ronan the Accuser** (main); **the Super-Skrull**; **Skrull infiltrators** |

### What the movie is about
**ACT ONE: THE WAR ARRIVES**
- **Monday, January 6, 2025.** A **Kree warship** and a **Skrull armada** meet in orbit around Saturn.
- **Carol** tries to broker peace. **Ronan** won't hear it.
- On Earth, **Skrulls** are **impersonating government officials** and **turning the public against superheroes.** **Hearings.** Arrest warrants.

**ACT TWO: WHO GOES THERE?**
- **Carol teams up with Kamala** (her biggest fan) and **Monica Rambeau** (a lieutenant in the New Orleans Harbor Patrol, as on 616) to root out the Skrulls.
- **The Super-Skrull** attacks Jersey City.
- **Rick Jones**, now in his thirties and still Earth's reluctant cosmic kid, **is taken** by the Supreme Intelligence.

**ACT THREE: THE DESTINY FORCE**
- The fleets clash over Earth.
- **Carol goes Binary.** It still isn't enough.
- **Rick Jones**, in the Supreme Intelligence's chamber, **unleashes the Destiny Force.** Ghostly figures of **Earth's heroes, past and future**, freeze **both fleets.**
- **The Supreme Intelligence**: *"Humanity has a potential the Kree lost. You'll need it soon. Something is killing the universe."*
- **Monica Rambeau**, caught in an energy blast, **becomes light.**
- The war ends. The Skrulls retreat. Kamala shows Carol a selfie of the two of them, and Carol smiles.
