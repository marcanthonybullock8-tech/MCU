**Straight from the page:**
- **Carol Danvers**, a **U.S. Air Force officer**, runs security at a base where **Dr. Walter Lawson** works. He's really **Mar-Vell**, a Kree captain undercover on Earth (*CM* #18).
- **The Kree Psyche-Magnitron explodes** during a fight with **Yon-Rogg**, Mar-Vell's jealous commanding officer. **Carol's DNA fuses with Mar-Vell's Kree genes** (*CM* #18, *Ms. Marvel* #1).
- **Mar-Vell** was exposed to **Nitro's nerve gas** (*CM* #34). It gave him **cancer** (*Death of Captain Marvel*).
- **Rick Jones** is at his side (616).
- **Mar-Vell dies surrounded by friends.** **Thanos**, as Death's champion, **comes to lead him into Death's embrace** (*Death of Captain Marvel*).
- **Carol takes the name Captain Marvel** in his honor (vol. 7 #1).

**Changes and why:**
| Change | Why |
|---|---|
| **Nitro's nerve gas** comes from the **Stamford explosion** (*Civil War*) | MCU continuity. Mar-Vell was there investigating the New Warriors. |
| **Carol's origin and Mar-Vell's death** happen in one film | It links the two, and it connects to the Infinity Saga (Thanos) |
| Mar-Vell is played by a woman | The Casting Rules (flagged) |

## 4. STORY
| Field | Entry |
|---|---|
| **Logline** | An Air Force officer gets the power of the stars from a dying alien hero, and in his last days she learns what it means to carry his name. |
| **Theme** | **Legacy and a good death.** Mar-Vell faces death with dignity, and Carol inherits his light. |
| **Villain / antagonist** | **Yon-Rogg** (main); **cancer** (the one enemy Mar-Vell can't fight) |

### What the movie is about
**ACT ONE: THE MAGNITRON**
- **Monday, January 15, 2018.** **Col. Carol Danvers**, U.S. Air Force, runs security at a desert research base. **Dr. Walter Lawson**, a brilliant, quiet scientist, is hiding something.
- Lawson is **Mar-Vell**, a **Kree captain** undercover on Earth for years, and a friend of **Rick Jones.**
- **Yon-Rogg**, Mar-Vell's jealous Kree commander, comes to bring him home or kill him.
- In the fight, the **Psyche-Magnitron explodes.** **Carol is caught in the blast.**

**ACT TWO: THE DIAGNOSIS**
- Carol wakes up **changed**: flight, strength, **photon blasts.**
- Mar-Vell trains her. He also tells her the truth: **he has cancer**, from the nerve gas he breathed at Stamford. **He's dying.**
- **Yon-Rogg** attacks with a Kree strike force. **Carol takes it apart** and flies into orbit to bring down his flagship.

**ACT THREE: THE DEATH OF CAPTAIN MARVEL**
- **Mar-Vell's last days.** **Rick Jones**, **Carol**, and old allies at his bedside. (Fury drops in for a cameo.)
- Mar-Vell drifts off. In a dream, he stands on a golden plain. **Thanos** walks up to him, **peacefully.**
  > **THANOS:** *"Mar-Vell. You fought me once in a dream. There is no need for fighting now."*
- Beside him is **Death**, and she smiles at Mar-Vell. It's the only time we ever see her smile.
- **Mar-Vell takes her hand** and goes.
- **Friday, April 20, 2018.** Mar-Vell's funeral. **Carol** takes his name: ***"Captain Marvel."***
