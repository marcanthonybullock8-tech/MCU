### 3A. COMIC ACCURACY REPORT
| Field | Entry |

| **Overall accuracy score** | **96%.** *Extremis* is adapted almost beat for beat, joined to the Mandarin's Extremis scheme from "Haunted" (2008). |

**Straight from the page:**
- **Extremis** is a nanotech **super-soldier virus** from **Futurepharm** in **Austin, Texas.** It rewrites the body's "repair center" and needs a **cocoon stage** (*Extremis* #1).
- **Aldrich Killian** leaks Extremis and **takes his own life** (#1). This happens off-screen for PG-13.
- **Mallen**, a domestic extremist, is injected with it and gains **super strength, regeneration, and fire breath.** He massacres an FBI convoy in **Houston** (#1–2).
- **Mallen beats Iron Man badly** (#3).
- **Tony, near death, has Maya Hansen inject him with Extremis.** He goes into a **cocoon.** He comes out able to **store the armor's undersheath inside his hollow bones**, **control the armor with his mind**, and **connect directly to every network and satellite on Earth** (#4–5).
- **The Mandarin coerces Maya into perfecting Extremis** so he can release it worldwide ("Haunted").
- **Tony builds the War Machine armor and Rhodey takes it** (*Iron Man* #281–284).
- **When the Mandarin falls, the rings leave to seek new hosts** (vol. 5 #28).

**Changes and why:**
| Change | Why |
|---|---|
| The Mandarin's Extremis plot (2008) is combined with *Extremis* (2005) | It gives the Iron Man trilogy one villain throughout. The Mandarin carries through all three films. |
| War Machine (1992) arrives in the Extremis-era film | Rhodey has earned his own armor since "Next time, baby" in 2008. |
| Maya Hansen's fate | She survives the film. Her 616 death (2008) is held back, and **she's arrested for her part in the Extremis program.** |

## 4. STORY
| Field | Entry |
|---|---|
| **Logline** | After saving the world, Tony Stark can't stop building armor. Then a virus that makes men into weapons shows up, and it's connected to the Mandarin, and Tony has to put the armor inside himself to survive. |
| **Theme** | **The armor was always inside him.** Iron Man isn't the suit. It's the man. |
| **Villain / antagonist** | **The Mandarin** (main); **Mallen** (his Extremis enforcer); **Aldrich Killian** (the leak that starts it) |

### What the movie is about
**ACT ONE: THE LEAK**
- **Monday, January 7, 2013.** Eight months after *The Avengers*, Tony is paying for the team, running Stark Industries, and not sleeping. He's building armor after armor in the Avengers Mansion basement. He's **stayed sober**, and Pepper sees the building for what it is: a new bottle.
- In **Austin, Texas**, **Dr. Maya Hansen**, an old acquaintance of Tony's, calls in a panic. Her partner **Aldrich Killian** has **leaked Extremis** and **taken his own life.**
- **Houston:** a man named **Mallen**, injected with Extremis, **wipes out an FBI convoy** with fire and bare hands.

**ACT TWO: THE COCOON**
- Iron Man goes after Mallen, and Mallen **almost kills him.** The armor is shredded, Tony's organs are failing, and Rhodey drags him out.
- Maya offers the one thing that could save him: **Extremis.**
- Tony agrees. He goes into the **cocoon.**
- In the dark, his heart stops. Only the audience sees **the hooded woman** standing at the foot of the bed. **She looks at Tony, then turns away.** He wakes.
- **Tony comes out of the cocoon changed.** He can think the armor onto his body and hear every phone in Texas. In the **rematch** in downtown Houston, he takes Mallen apart.
- Maya's funding trail leads to **the Mandarin.** He forced her to make Extremis stable for **mass release.**

**ACT THREE: WAR MACHINE**
- The Mandarin plans to spread an aerosol form of Extremis from his Makluan starship over **Beijing, Mumbai, Lagos and New York**, and whoever survives will be his army.
- Tony gives Rhodey the **Variable Threat Response Battle Suit.** Rhodey: *"Took you long enough."* **War Machine** is born.
- **The Valley of Spirits.** Tony, in the Extremis armor, and War Machine fight through the Mandarin's Extremis soldiers.
- **Iron Man vs. the Mandarin** in his ring-powered armor. All ten rings against a man who runs his armor by thought. Tony uses **Extremis** to **overload the starship's reactor through the Mandarin's own link to it.**
- The Mandarin falls into the reactor core. **His body is never found.**
- Maya is arrested. At Avengers Mansion, Tony stands in the basement full of armors, then shuts the lights off and goes upstairs to Pepper.
- Final line, voiceover: *"They can take the suits. They can't take the armor. **I am Iron Man.**"*
