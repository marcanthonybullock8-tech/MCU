## 4. STORY
| Field | Entry |
|---|---|
| **Logline** | Guided by a dead man's map, the last Black Panther searches a domain overrun by monsters for the six Gems that could bring down a god, with Thanos one step behind. |
| **Theme** | **Inheritance.** Shuri never wanted her brother's crown, or the Gauntlet. |
| **Villain / antagonist** | **THANOS** (main); **the Annihilation swarm** |

### What the movie is about
**ACT ONE: THE MAP**
- **Shuri and Namor** follow **Strange's map** to **a domain overrun by insects the size of trucks.**
- **Anwen Bakian**, 15, and **her family** have been **hiding the Gems** there for generations, or so Doom made them believe.

**ACT TWO: SIX GEMS**
- **The hunt** for **six Gems.** **Thanos** is hunting them too.
- **Namor and Shuri** clash: *"You destroyed worlds." "So did your brother's Illuminati."*
- **Anwen's family** are killed **one by one.** **Anwen** keeps going.

**ACT THREE: THE GAUNTLET**
- **Thanos gets the Gauntlet.** For a moment, **the Mad Titan is God again.**
- **Shuri steals it back**, with Anwen's help. **She puts it on.**
- **Thanos** is beaten, **by a Black Panther with the power of God.**
- **Shuri** stands **at the Shield**, with **Namor** and **Anwen.** **Beyond it** are **the Deadlands**: **an army of the dead.** Namor: *"If we open it, they march with us."* Shuri: ***"Then open it."***
