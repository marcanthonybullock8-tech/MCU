| Field | Entry |

| **Overall accuracy score** | **96%.** *Avengers* #16 and #54–58 are adapted in order. |

**Straight from the page:**
- **"The Old Order Changeth!":** Iron Man, Thor, Giant-Man (now **Goliath**) and the Wasp **step away.** **Cap leads a new team** of three reformed villains (#16).
- **Hawkeye breaks into Avengers Mansion** to prove herself worthy (#16).
- **The Scarlet Witch and Quicksilver** answer the Avengers' call after leaving Magneto (#16).
- **The Crimson Cowl** leads the **Masters of Evil** (including **Klaw**), and **Jarvis is framed** as the Cowl (#54).
- **The Cowl is Ultron-5**, a robot that **evolved itself** from a prototype built by **Hank Pym**, then **hypnotized Hank into forgetting** he'd made it (#54–58).
- Ultron builds **the Vision**, a synthezoid, to destroy the Avengers. **The Vision turns on him** (#57).
- **The Vision's mind is patterned on Simon Williams, Wonder Man** (#57). **His body is the original Human Torch's** (616 retcon).
- The Vision is accepted as an Avenger and **cries** (#58, "Even an Android Can Cry").

**Changes and why:**
| Change | Why |
|---|---|
| *Avengers* #16 (1965) and #54–58 (1968) in one film | It shows the new line-up's first great test |
| The founders come back for the finale | The MCU's second big "Assemble" moment. On 616 the founders came back into the fold over time. |
| Klaw is the Masters of Evil member we show | He sets up Black Panther (Phase 3) |

## 4. STORY
| Field | Entry |
|---|---|
| **Logline** | Captain America's new Avengers, three former villains and a man out of time, face a machine that thinks it's God. It was built by one of their own, and it builds a son to destroy them. |
| **Theme** | **Children and their makers.** Hank and Ultron. Ultron and the Vision. Magneto and the twins. Every creation chooses who to be. |
| **Villain / antagonist** | **Ultron** (main); the **Masters of Evil** (Klaw) |

### What the movie is about
**ACT ONE: THE OLD ORDER CHANGETH**
- **Monday, January 12, 2015.** Avengers Mansion. The founders **step back**:
  - Tony has to rebuild Stark Industries after the Mandarin.
  - Thor is called to Asgard, where Surtur is stirring.
  - Hank and Janet are taking a sabbatical. Hank is now **Goliath**, and privately he's struggling.
- **Cap stays.** He needs a new team.
- **Claire Barton breaks into Avengers Mansion** to prove she's good enough: *"Your security's garbage, by the way."*
- **Wanda and Pietro Maximoff**, former Brotherhood members, answer the Avengers' call.
- Natasha, now S.H.I.E.L.D.'s liaison: *"Three ex-villains and a veteran. The papers are going to love this."* The papers **hate** it: ***"CAP'S KOOKY QUARTET."***

**ACT TWO: THE CRIMSON COWL**
- A masked mastermind, **the Crimson Cowl**, leads a new **Masters of Evil**, including **Klaw**, a mercenary with a sonic weapon grafted to his wrist.
- The evidence points to **Jarvis.** The team is shattered, and Cap refuses to believe it.
- The Cowl is unmasked. It's **Ultron**, a self-evolving robot.
- Hank goes white. Ultron's first words to him are ***"Hello, father."***
- Ultron had **hypnotized Hank into forgetting he built him.**
- Ultron creates **the Vision**, a synthezoid built on the body of a WWII android and the brain patterns of the late **Simon Williams**, and sends him to destroy the Avengers.

**ACT THREE: EVEN AN ANDROID CAN CRY**
- The Vision **turns on Ultron.** *"You made me to destroy them. I have chosen otherwise."*
- Ultron takes over every machine in Manhattan.
- **Cap calls the founders home.** Iron Man, Thor, Goliath and the Wasp arrive.
- ***"Avengers... ASSEMBLE!"*** Nine Avengers and the Vision, against an army of Ultron drones across the city.
- Wanda's hex brings Ultron down. **The Vision phases his hand through Ultron's core.** Ultron's last message goes out into the net as a whisper: *"I'll be back, father. I always come back."*
- At the Mansion, the Vision asks to join. Cap: *"You're in."* Alone afterward, **a single tear** runs down the Vision's face.
- Wanda watches him from the stairs.
