### 3A. COMIC ACCURACY REPORT
| Field | Entry |
|---|---|
| **Overall accuracy score** | **96%.** The origin, armor progression, supporting cast, villains and the Stane arc all come straight from 616. The only changes are sliding-timescale updates and compressing a three-year comic arc into one film. |

**Straight from the page:**
- **The origin:** Tony, a weapons-industry genius, is wounded by a booby trap with **shrapnel lodged near his heart**. He's captured by the warlord **Wong-Chu** and forced to build weapons, and with the Nobel-nominated physicist **Ho Yinsen** he builds the **Mark I** instead. **Yinsen sacrifices himself** to buy Tony time.
- **The chest plate:** the armor's **chest plate is what keeps Tony's heart beating.** It has to be **recharged**, and yes, **Tony plugs himself into a wall socket** in one scene, straight out of the Silver Age.
- **The armor progression, exactly as in the comics:** **gray Mark I** (*ToS* #39) → **gold Mark II** (*ToS* #40, "the Golden Avenger") → **red-and-gold Mark III** (*ToS* #48).
- **Rhodey** is a **Marine Corps aviator** (as in 616) who picks Tony up after the escape (*Iron Man* #144).
- **Happy Hogan** is a **former boxer** turned Tony's driver and bodyguard (*ToS* #45).
- **Pepper Potts** is Tony's sharp, capable executive assistant (*ToS* #45).
- **Obadiah Stane** is bald and **obsessed with chess**. He talks about people as pawns, targets Tony through his company, and builds the **Iron Monger** armor from Tony's stolen designs (*Iron Man* #163–200). **He dies by his own hand after he's beaten** (*Iron Man* #200), handled off-screen for PG-13.
- **The "bodyguard" cover story:** Tony first tells the press that Iron Man is his **bodyguard**, the classic 616 secret-identity ruse. Then, following the modern 616 reveal (vol. 3 #55), he tells the world: ***"I am Iron Man."***
- **The Maria Stark Foundation,** named for Tony's late mother and the Avengers' future funder in 616, holds its charity gala.
- **Tony's home is 890 Fifth Avenue, Manhattan,** the Stark family mansion that becomes **Avengers Mansion** in 616.
- **Stark Industries headquarters is on Long Island, New York,** as in 616.
- **Howard and Maria Stark died in a car crash** (616), which put Tony in charge of the company at 21.

**Changes and why:**
| Change | Why |
|---|---|
| Vietnam → **Afghanistan** | The sliding timescale. Marvel's own *Extremis* (2005) moved the origin to Afghanistan, and we follow the latest 616 version. |
| The Stane arc (1982–85) is compressed into one film | Runtime. The beats (takeover, chess games, stolen armor tech, Iron Monger, suicide) stay the same, but Tony's slide back into alcoholism is **saved for *Iron Man 2*** so it gets the time it needs. |
| Mark I built in a **cave** instead of a jungle hut | Follows the Afghanistan update. |
| Mark I gets an improvised flamethrower and a short rocket hop | Derived from later 616 armor tech so the escape is believable. It's listed here to be transparent. |
| Tony's in-universe age is **26** (b. May 29, 1981) | This matches Marc-Anthony. 616 Tony's age slides; ours follows the actor per the real-time rule. |
| Casting of Pepper, Happy, Yinsen and others | Follows the Casting Rules. The characters' histories and personalities are unchanged. |

## 4. STORY
| Field | Entry |
|---|---|
| **Logline** | A weapons-industry genius is captured by the warlord using his own weapons. He builds a suit of armor to escape, and comes home determined to fix what he helped break, unaware that the most dangerous man in his life is sitting across the chessboard. |
| **Theme** | **Responsibility.** Tony built weapons without caring where they ended up. Iron Man is how he makes up for it. |

### What the movie is about
**ACT ONE: THE CAVE**
- **Kunar Province, Afghanistan.** **Tony Stark** (26), the playboy genius who has run **Stark Industries** since his parents died, is demonstrating a new transistor-guided weapons system for U.S. forces.
- His convoy is ambushed. A booby-trapped Stark shell explodes and **shrapnel lodges near his heart.**
- He wakes up in a cave, prisoner of the warlord **Wong-Chu**. Wong-Chu's men are carrying **Stark weapons.** Somebody sold them.
- **Ho Yinsen**, a Nobel-nominated physicist and fellow prisoner, has kept Tony alive with a **chest plate** that keeps the shrapnel from reaching his heart.
- Wong-Chu orders them to build him a weapon. Instead, they build the **Mark I.**
- The escape. **Yinsen sacrifices himself** to buy Tony time. As he dies, only the audience sees **a pale woman in a dark hooded cloak** standing silently at the cave mouth. Then she's gone.
- Tony tears through the cave and burns the stockpile of Stark weapons. Wong-Chu escapes.
- **Marine aviator James Rhodes** picks him up in the desert, and a friendship begins.

**ACT TWO: THE GOLDEN AVENGER**
- Back in New York, Tony announces that **Stark Industries will stop making weapons.** The stock collapses.
- **Obadiah Stane**, head of **Stane International** and a longtime "family friend," starts moving pieces around the board.
- In the workshop under his **Fifth Avenue mansion**, Tony builds the **gold Mark II** and makes his first flight over Long Island Sound.
- The **Maria Stark Foundation** gala: Tony meets **Pepper** on the dance floor, and Stan Lee is mistaken for Hugh Hefner.
- Tony finds out that **Stark weapons are still reaching Wong-Chu.**
- He builds the **red-and-gold Mark III** and flies to Afghanistan to wipe out the arms stockpile. Wong-Chu's forces are shattered.
- The press notices a "flying metal man." Tony's cover story is that it's **his bodyguard.**
- A S.H.I.E.L.D. agent, **Jasper Sitwell**, keeps trying to book a debriefing. Tony keeps not showing up.

**ACT THREE: CHECKMATE**
- Pepper gets into Stark Industries' servers and finds that **Stane has been selling weapons to Wong-Chu** and **ordered the ambush on Tony's convoy.**
- Stane has launched a **hostile takeover** of Stark Industries and has reverse-engineered the Mark I from the wreckage his contacts recovered.
- He ambushes Tony at home and **rips the chest plate out** ("Every king falls, Tony. It's just a matter of moves"), leaving him to die.
- Tony survives by crawling to the **wall socket** and plugging in the old Mark I chest plate that Pepper had framed.
- The finale runs across Long Island: **Iron Monger vs. Iron Man.** Stane is stronger. **Tony wins by out-thinking him.** He lures Iron Monger into the facility's power grid and overloads it.
- Beaten and unmasked, **Stane takes his own life** (off-screen) rather than be arrested.
- The press conference. Pepper hands Tony the **bodyguard story** on note cards. He looks at them, puts them down, and says:
  > ***"The truth is... I am Iron Man."***

## 6. WORLD-BUILDING

### The film's own world
- **Stark Industries** on Long Island, **Stane International** as its rival, and Tony's **Fifth Avenue mansion** with the workshop underneath.
- **The armor progression** (gray → gold → red and gold), the **chest plate** and its recharging problem, **repulsors** and the **uni-beam.**
- **The supporting cast:** Pepper, Happy, Rhodey, Yinsen's legacy, Stane.
- **The Maria Stark Foundation**, the Stark family's charity.
- **The rules:** Iron Man is **technology, not magic.** Anything the armor does has to be something Tony could build.

### Setting up the Big Bad (Thanos / the Infinity Saga)
The setup is deliberately hidden. A general audience won't catch it, but the comic fans will.

| Seed | How it appears | What it means |
|---|---|---|

## 8. ⭐ FIRST FILM ONLY: HOW IT KICKS EVERYTHING OFF
| Field | Entry |
|---|---|
| **How the film launches the shared universe** | *Iron Man* works entirely as a standalone film, and it quietly **builds the foundation for everything after it.** It establishes **S.H.I.E.L.D.** as the thread that runs through every later film. It establishes **a public superhero** ("I am Iron Man"), which puts heroes in the open for the rest of the saga. It gives the future Avengers a **home** (890 Fifth Avenue) and a **source of money** (Stark and the Maria Stark Foundation). And it opens **a door to other films** through the Desert Base news report and the Pym journal. |
| **The first reveal** | **The post-credit scene.** Most of the audience will have walked out and the ushers will be sweeping popcorn. The people who stay see **Nick Fury** in Tony's living room say the word **"Avengers."** That's the moment the world gets bigger. |
| **What the fans discover, and when** | **Opening night (Thursday May 1 at midnight, and Friday May 2):** shaky camera-phone video of the post-credit scene is on YouTube within hours. By Saturday, message boards are breaking down every Easter egg: Desert Base, the Pym journal, Titan, podium 55. **By Monday, May 5**, AICN and SuperHeroHype have threads titled *"ARE THE MARVEL MOVIES CONNECTED??"* Marvel says nothing. |
| **The unexplained seed** | **The hooded woman at Yinsen's death.** Nobody officially notices her for years. Some fans see her and argue about her on forums, and she's dismissed as an extra, a mistake, or Yinsen's wife. **She's the saga's quietest and most important seed.** |
