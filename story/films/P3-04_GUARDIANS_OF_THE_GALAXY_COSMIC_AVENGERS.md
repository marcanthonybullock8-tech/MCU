**Straight from the page:**
- **J'son**, the emperor of **Spartax**, **is Peter's father.** He left Meredith Quill on Earth (vol. 3 #0.1–1).
- **The Galactic Council** (Spartax, the Kree, the Shi'ar, the Badoon and others) **declares Earth off-limits** (#1).
- **The Badoon attack London** (#2–3). They're the race that killed Peter's mother (*Marvel Preview* #4).
- **Iron Man flies out to join the Guardians** as Earth's representative (#1).
- **The original Guardians of the Galaxy**, including **Yondu**, the blue-skinned archer, and **Starhawk**, protect the galaxy in **the year 3000** (*Marvel Super-Heroes* #18).
- **Thanos keeps Nebula alive in torment**, suspended between life and death (*Infinity Gauntlet* #1).

**Changes and why:**
| Change | Why |
|---|---|
| The Guardians of 3000 time-travel to the present | *Guardians 3000* (2014) did this on 616. Here it's used to warn about the snap. |
| J'son's motive is tied to Thanos (the Council is afraid Earth will draw his attention) | It connects the story to the Infinity Saga |

## 4. STORY
| Field | Entry |
|---|---|
| **Logline** | When the galaxy's rulers declare Earth off-limits, Star-Lord finds out the man behind it is his father, and has to choose between the empire he was born to and the planet his mother died on. |
| **Theme** | **Fathers.** Peter and J'son. Gamora and Nebula and Thanos. Every Guardian has to choose who they belong to. |
| **Villain / antagonist** | **J'son** (main); **the Badoon**; **Nebula** (at first) |

### What the movie is about
**ACT ONE: OFF-LIMITS**
- **Earth date: Monday, February 13, 2017.** The **Galactic Council** meets and **declares Earth off-limits** to every race.
- **Peter Quill** is summoned to the Council and meets its president, **J'son, emperor of Spartax.** J'son is **his father.**
- The Guardians **refuse to stay away from Earth.**

**ACT TWO: LONDON**
- The **Badoon**, the race that killed Peter's mother, **invade London** in defiance of the Council.
- **Iron Man**, now Director of S.H.I.E.L.D., flies into orbit to meet the Guardians. Rocket: *"So he's real."*
- **Nebula** is with the Badoon, hired to kill Gamora. The sisters fight across the Thames, and then **stand together** when the Badoon betray Nebula.
- Peter finds out that **J'son brought the Badoon here** to scare the Council into stripping Earth's protections.

**ACT THREE: GUARDIANS OF 3000**
- The battle over London. Iron Man hacks the Badoon command ship through Extremis, and the Guardians bring it down.
- Peter faces J'son: *"You're not my father. My father is a guy named Yondu who stole me when I was a kid."* J'son loses the Council's support.
- A **time-rift** opens. Out of it come **Yondu** and **Starhawk**, **the Guardians of the Galaxy from the year 3000.**
- **Starhawk:** *"We came back to warn you. In our time, half the stars are dead. Half of everything is gone. It starts soon."*
- The rift closes, and they're gone. The Guardians look at each other. So does Iron Man.
