### 3A. COMIC ACCURACY REPORT
| Field | Entry |
|---|---|
| **Overall accuracy score** | **95%.** *The Infinity Gauntlet* #1 is adapted almost exactly, but its snap is **moved from the first act to the end of the film.** |

**Straight from the page:**
- **Mephisto** convinces Thanos that **only an act on a universal scale** can win Death's love (#1).
- **Thanos builds a temple to Death** and **creates Terraxia**, a woman made to be his "consort" and **to make Death jealous** (#1).
- **Nebula**, alive and in agony, is Thanos's **trophy** (#1).
- **The Silver Surfer bursts into the Sanctum Sanctorum** and warns **Doctor Strange** (#1).
- **The snap.** Thanos **closes his fist**, and **half of all living beings in the universe vanish** (#1).
- **Death doesn't respond** (#1).
- **Gamora dies**, and **her soul is taken into the Soul Gem**, as on 616, where Warlock took her soul as she died (*Warlock* #15).

**Changes and why:**
| Change | Why |
|---|---|
| **The snap moves from page 20 of #1 to the end of the film** | It gives the heroes a full film to fight before it happens |
| **The Hulk arrives from Sakaar** in the middle of the battle | It pays off *Planet Hulk*. His rage at the Illuminati turns on Thanos. |
| **The heroes' pre-snap assault on Thanos** | An MCU addition. On 616 the heroes attack *after* the snap (#4). That happens in *Avengers Forever*. |

## 4. STORY
| Field | Entry |
|---|---|
| **Logline** | The Mad Titan holds the power of a god. To win the love of Death, he'll give her half of everything that lives, and Earth's heroes have to stop him while they're still at war with each other. |
| **Theme** | **Love and loss.** Thanos kills for love. The heroes fight for the people they love, and many of them lose them anyway. |
| **Villain / antagonist** | **THANOS** (main); **Terraxia**; **Mephisto** |

### What the movie is about
**ACT ONE: THE WARNING**
- **Monday, April 16, 2018.** **The Silver Surfer** crashes through the skylight of the **Sanctum Sanctorum.** *"He's going to do it."*
- **Doctor Strange** calls Earth's heroes together. Some come, and many don't.
- **Tony Stark**, Director of S.H.I.E.L.D., gets **Steve Rogers** out of the Raft. They don't talk about Civil War. *"Later."*
- The **Avengers**, the **Fantastic Four**, the **X-Men** (Wolverine), **Black Panther**, **Spider-Man**, and a Guardians ship with **Drax, Gamora and Rocket** aboard.
- **Gamora**: *"I know where he is. And I know he won't stop."*

**ACT TWO: THE TEMPLE**
- On Thanos's dead world, a **temple to Death.** Thanos creates **Terraxia** to make Death jealous. **Death doesn't even look.**
- **Mephisto** whispers: *"Only one gift is great enough..."*
- The heroes **strike first.** The Guardians' ship punches through Thanos's defenses, and the Avengers go in.
- **The battle for the temple.** Thanos is holding everything back. The heroes land real blows.
- **The Hulk** arrives from space in a Sakaaran warship, **crashing into the temple** in his World War rage, ready to kill the Illuminati. He sees Thanos instead, and **punches him hard enough to make him bleed.**
- For the first time, **Thanos stops smiling.** He raises the Gauntlet.

**ACT THREE: THE GIFT**
- **Terraxia kills Gamora.** As she dies, **the Soul Gem glows green**, and her soul is drawn into it. **Thanos watches, and something flickers in his eyes.**
- **Thanos stops holding back.** Thor's lightning becomes bubbles. The Hulk is thrown into the sun and brought back. Tony's armor is turned to glass.
- **Saturday, April 28, 2018, 3:14 PM Eastern.**
- Thanos looks at **Death.**
  > ***"For you, my love."***
- **He closes his fist.**
- **Half of everything that lives is gone.** Across the universe, on Earth, on the battlefield:
  - **Spider-Man**: *"Mr. Stark, I don't feel so good..."*
  - **Black Panther**, **the Falcon**, **the Winter Soldier**
  - **Star-Lord**, **Groot**, **Mantis**
  - **the Human Torch** and **the Invisible Woman**
  - **the Wasp** (Elxa Bullock), crumbling to ash in Hank's arms
  - **the Scarlet Witch** (Grace Bullock), reaching for her brother
- **The last shot:** **Death** turns her head toward Thanos, **and then turns away.**
- On Earth, **Tony Stark** sits on the steps of **Avengers Mansion** among the ashes. **Steve Rogers** sits down beside him.
- **CUT TO BLACK.** No music. No credits music.
