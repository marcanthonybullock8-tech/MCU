| Field | Entry |

| **Overall accuracy score** | **96%.** *ASM* #39–40 and #121–122 are adapted closely. |

**Straight from the page:**
- **Peter at Empire State University**, with **Gwen Stacy**, **Harry Osborn** and **Flash** (#31 on).
- **The Green Goblin**'s identity is a mystery. Then he **unmasks: Norman Osborn**, Harry's father (#39–40).
- **The Goblin kidnaps Gwen** and takes her to the top of **the George Washington Bridge.** He **throws her off.** Spider-Man **catches her with a web**, and there's a ***SNAP.*** **Gwen is dead** (#121).
- Peter asks whether his web killed her. The **Goblin's** answer is cruel.
- **The Goblin's own glider** impales him in the final fight (#122).
- **Mary Jane stays with Peter** when he tells her to leave (#122).

**Changes and why:**
| Change | Why |
|---|---|
| Peter's identity is public (since *Civil War*), so the Goblin **already knows** | MCU continuity |
| Gwen's death is shown **with care** (PG-13): the snap is **heard, not seen** | Rating and respect |

## 4. STORY
| Field | Entry |
|---|---|
| **Logline** | A year after the universe was saved, Peter Parker is in college and in love, and the Green Goblin is coming to take all of it away. |
| **Theme** | **The price of great responsibility.** Peter saved the universe. He can't save the girl he loves. |
| **Villain / antagonist** | **The Green Goblin (Norman Osborn)** |

### What the movie is about
**ACT ONE: SUMMER**
- **Monday, May 20, 2019.** A year after the Gauntlet. **Peter Parker**, 20, at **Empire State University**, is in love with **Gwen Stacy.**
- **Harry Osborn** is his best friend. **Norman Osborn** is Harry's cold, brilliant father.
- **Spider-Man** has been public since *Civil War*, and New York loves him (except Jameson).
- A **Green Goblin** attacks the city.

**ACT TWO: HOW GREEN WAS MY GOBLIN**
- The Goblin **captures Spider-Man**, drags him to a warehouse, and **unmasks.** It's **Norman Osborn.**
- *"I know who you are, Peter. I've always known. Everyone has."*
- Norman is **arrested**, and **he escapes.**

**ACT THREE: THE NIGHT GWEN STACY DIED**
- **Saturday, June 22, 2019.** The Goblin kidnaps **Gwen** and takes her to the top of **the George Washington Bridge.**
- He throws her off.
- Spider-Man shoots a web and **catches her ankle.** ***snap.***
- He pulls her up. *"Gwen... I saved you."* **She isn't breathing.**
- Behind them on the bridge tower, **the hooded woman** stands. **This time, Death doesn't turn away.** She holds out her hand, and Gwen's spirit takes it.
- **Spider-Man vs. the Goblin** in a warehouse at night. The **glider** comes back at Peter. **He dodges**, and it impales Norman.
- **Gwen's funeral.** Peter goes home and shuts the door. There's a knock. A redhead is standing in the doorway: **Mary Jane Watson.**
  > **PETER:** *"Go away."*
  > **She doesn't. She sits down beside him.**
