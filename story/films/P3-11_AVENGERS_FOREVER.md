## 3. SOURCE MATERIAL (EARTH-616)
| Field | Entry |
|---|---|
| **Primary comic(s) adapted** | ***The Infinity Gauntlet* #2–6** (1991), Jim Starlin, George Pérez & Ron Lim: **Warlock gathers Earth's heroes and the cosmic entities**, **Thanos rises beyond his body**, **the heroes' doomed assault** and **Captain America's last stand**, **Nebula steals the Gauntlet**, **Thanos helps the heroes**, **Warlock takes the Gauntlet and undoes it all**, and **Thanos becomes a farmer.** |
| **Supporting comics** | ***Avengers Forever* #1–12** (1998–99, Kurt Busiek & Carlos Pacheco): the title, and the theme of **Avengers across time** · ***Warlock and the Infinity Watch* #1** (1992): Warlock **divides the Gems** among trusted guardians |
| **Nerf check** | ✅ **Thanos is omnipotent**, and **every hero who attacks him dies** in the assault, as in *IG* #4. **He isn't beaten.** He **undoes himself**, which is Starlin's thesis: ***"Deep down, Thanos believes he doesn't deserve to win."*** |

### 3A. COMIC ACCURACY REPORT
| Field | Entry |
|---|---|
| **Overall accuracy score** | **94%.** *The Infinity Gauntlet* #2–6 is adapted beat for beat, and the five-year gap is the showrunner's structural choice. |

**Straight from the page:**
- **Adam Warlock returns** and **takes command** of the resistance (#2).
- **The cosmic entities gather**: Galactus, the Stranger, Eternity and more (#3–5).
- Warlock's plan: **a frontal assault that's meant to fail**, to exploit **Thanos's need to win, and his need to lose** (#3–4).
- **The heroes' assault.** **Thanos kills them one by one**: Iron Man, Thor, the Hulk, Wolverine, Namor, the Vision, **Doctor Doom** (who goes for the Gauntlet himself), and more (#4).
- ***Captain America stands alone against Thanos*** (#4):
  > ***"As long as one man stands against you, you'll never win."***
- **The Silver Surfer lunges for the Gauntlet and misses by an inch** (#4).
- **Thanos leaves his body** to become omnipresent, and **Nebula takes the Gauntlet** (#4).
- **Nebula reverses the last twenty-four hours**, bringing the fallen heroes back, then **has to fight the cosmic entities** (#5).
- **Thanos joins the heroes** against his own granddaughter (#5–6).
- **Warlock takes the Gauntlet** and **restores reality** (#6).
- **Thanos fakes his death** and **retires to a farm on a quiet world.** Warlock is the only one who knows (#6).
- **Warlock divides the Gems** among **the Infinity Watch** (*Warlock and the Infinity Watch* #1).

**Changes and why:**
| Change | Why |
|---|---|
| **The five-year jump** | The showrunner's choice. It lets the film show **the cost** of the snap. |
| **Warlock reverses five years, not a day** | On 616, Warlock and Nebula undo **the Gauntlet's events.** Here the Gauntlet's events **span five years**, so the reversal does too. **The people who fought remember.** |

## 4. STORY
| Field | Entry |
|---|---|
| **Logline** | Five years after half of all life disappeared, the survivors are broken, older and grieving. Then a golden stranger steps out of the Soul Gem with a plan that only works if every one of them is willing to die. |
| **Theme** | **Forever.** Loss is permanent until it isn't, and heroes keep fighting anyway. *"As long as one man stands against you, you'll never win."* |
| **Villain / antagonist** | **THANOS**; **Nebula** (with the Gauntlet); **Mephisto** |

### What the movie is about
**PROLOGUE: 3:15 PM**
- **Saturday, April 28, 2018, one minute after the snap.** The ashes haven't settled.
  - **Hank Pym**, on his knees in the rubble, calls **Janet's** name.
  - **Pietro** screams for **Wanda.**
  - **Tony** stares at the dust where **Peter** was standing.
- **Carol Danvers** lands among them: *"How do we fix this?"* Nobody answers.

**TITLE CARD: *FIVE YEARS LATER***

**ACT ONE: 2023**
- **Monday, April 24, 2023.** Half of Earth is empty. Heroes are **older and grieving.**
  - **Cap** runs grief support groups in Brooklyn.
  - **Tony** runs a hollowed-out S.H.I.E.L.D. and doesn't sleep.
  - **Thor** rules a half-empty Asgard.
  - **Hank** is barely functioning.
- **Adam Warlock**, **Gamora** and **Pip** step out of the Soul Gem (the ending of *Warlock*).
- **WARLOCK:** *"Thanos always leaves himself a way to lose. I'm going to give him one."*
- Warlock gathers **every hero still standing** and the **cosmic entities**: **Galactus**, **the Stranger** and **Eternity.**

**ACT TWO: THE LAST STAND**
- **Thanos** has **risen out of his body** to become the universe. He comes back to his temple **to face them himself.** He wants to be seen winning.
- **The assault.** Every hero goes all in:
  - **Captain Marvel goes Binary.**
  - **Thor's God Blast.**
  - **The Hulk at his peak.**
  - **Doom**, who has allied with no one, **goes for the Gauntlet.**
- **Thanos kills them one by one.** Iron Man, Thor, the Hulk, Wolverine, Doom, the Vision, Namor, Carol. Each death is quick.
- The smoke clears. **Captain America stands alone.** His shield is cracked.
  > **CAP:** *"As long as one man stands against you... you'll never win."*
- **Thanos kills him.**
- **The Silver Surfer** dives for the Gauntlet **and misses by an inch.**

**ACT THREE: THE GAUNTLET**
- Thanos, triumphant, **rises out of his body again**, higher than ever.
- **Nebula**, his tortured trophy, **pulls the Gauntlet off his empty hand.**
- **Nebula reverses the last day.** The fallen heroes come back to life on the temple floor.
- Nebula, omnipotent now, **turns on everyone.** **Galactus, Eternity and the Stranger** fight her, and they're losing.
- **Thanos**, stripped of godhood and mortal again, walks up to Warlock: *"She'll destroy everything. Let me help you."*
- Thanos distracts Nebula, and **Warlock takes the Gauntlet.**
- **Warlock**, omnipotent, closes his eyes and **rewinds five years.**

**EPILOGUE: 3:14 PM**
- **Saturday, April 28, 2018, 3:14 PM.** The snap **never happens.** Thanos's fist closes on **nothing.**
- Everyone who vanished is **back**, as if nothing happened:
  - **Janet** is in Hank's arms. **Hank sobs.** She doesn't understand why.
  - **Wanda** turns around to find **Pietro** crushing her in a hug.
  - **Peter Parker** asks Tony why he's crying.
  - **Only the heroes who fought remember the five years.**
- **Thanos** fakes his death in the temple. **Only Warlock sees him leave.**
- **A quiet farm on a distant world.** Thanos, in plain clothes, plants seeds. **Death** stands at the edge of the field. **She watches him for a long time.** Then she leaves, and **he doesn't follow.**
- **May 2019. Avengers Mansion.**
  - **Cap** is pardoned. **Tony** gives the Mansion back to the team.
  - **Every Avenger**, old and new, gathers on the steps.
  - **CAP:** ***"Avengers... ASSEMBLE."***
