### 3A. COMIC ACCURACY REPORT
| Field | Entry |
|---|---|
| **Overall accuracy score** | **96%.** *FF* #1 and Doom's origin are both taken from the page. |

**Straight from the page:**
- **Reed Richards** takes his fiancée **Sue**, her teenage brother **Johnny**, and his best friend, pilot **Ben Grimm**, on an **unauthorized spaceflight.** The ship **isn't shielded against cosmic rays** (Ben warned him), and they crash back to Earth changed (*FF* #1).
- **The powers:** Reed stretches, Sue turns invisible, Johnny **bursts into flame and flies** ("**Flame on!**"), and **Ben turns into the rock-skinned Thing** permanently, and it's a tragedy for him (*FF* #1).
- **Reed names them "the Fantastic Four"**, and they go public. They're **celebrities**, the opposite of the X-Men (*FF* #1–3).
- **The Mole Man** attacks from **Subterranea** with giant monsters (*FF* #1).
- **Victor von Doom**: Reed's **rival at Empire State University**. His forbidden experiment to reach his mother's soul in the underworld **explodes**, and Reed had warned him. He **blames Reed**, goes to Tibet, puts on **the mask while it's still red-hot**, and **rules Latveria** (*FF Annual* #2).
- **Alicia Masters**, a blind sculptor, loves Ben for who he is (*FF* #8).
- **The Baxter Building**, 42nd Street and Madison Avenue, and **Yancy Street**.
- **Uatu the Watcher** on the Moon's **Blue Area**, watching and never interfering (*FF* #13).

**Changes and why:**
| Change | Why |
|---|---|
| The flight is to **study cosmic-ray storms**, not to **beat the Communists to space** | Sliding timescale |
| Doom's first appearance (#5, 1962) is in the origin film | Runtime. He's the FF's greatest enemy. |
| Sue's force fields (1963) from the start | The **latest 616 version**: she's the team's most powerful member |

## 4. STORY
| Field | Entry |
|---|---|
| **Logline** | Four people take a rocket past the edge of the sky and come back as a family of freaks and heroes. The man who blames the smartest of them for his ruined face is coming to take everything. |
| **Theme** | **Family.** Powers aren't what make them fantastic. Coming back to each other is. |
| **Villain / antagonist** | **Doctor Doom** (main); **the Mole Man** (Act One) |

### What the movie is about
**ACT ONE: THE COSMIC STORM**
- **Monday, August 12, 2013.** **Dr. Reed Richards** has built a spacecraft to study the **cosmic-ray storm** forecast to pass Earth. NASA won't clear it.
- Reed goes anyway, with **Sue**, her 17-year-old brother **Johnny**, and his best friend, test pilot **Ben Grimm**, who warns him the shielding isn't good enough.
- The storm hits and they crash in a field in upstate New York.
- One by one, they change: stretching, vanishing, bursting into flame, and **Ben's skin turning to orange rock.** Ben stays that way.
- **The Mole Man** attacks New York from beneath the streets. The four fight side by side for the first time. Reed calls them **the Fantastic Four** on live TV.

**ACT TWO: FIRST FAMILY**
- They move into the **Baxter Building.** Unlike the X-Men, they're adored: talk shows, toy deals, and mail delivered by **Willie Lumpkin.**
- Ben meets **Alicia Masters**, a blind sculptor who sees him the way nobody else does.
- **Victor von Doom**, **the monarch of Latveria**, invites Reed to Doomstadt as an "old friend" from **Empire State University.** Reed tells Sue about the accident years ago: Victor's forbidden experiment, and the warning Victor ignored.
- In Latveria, Doom **reveals what he wants**: to **take the cosmic energy in the Four's bodies** for himself.
- **Round one: Doom beats all four of them.**

**ACT THREE: CLOBBERIN' TIME**
- The Four break out of the Doombots' cells, with Johnny burning through the walls and Ben tearing out the door.
- Doom starts the power transfer. **Sue holds him in a force field** while **Reed out-thinks the machine** and reverses it, and the backlash throws Doom across his own throne room.
- **The Thing** stands over him: ***"It's clobberin' time."***
- Doom escapes by **teleporting away through sorcery.** It's the first time Reed has seen magic, and it shakes him.
- Home at the Baxter Building. Reed proposes to Sue for the second time, **properly this time.** Ben laughs. Johnny flames on and writes a giant **"4"** in the sky over Manhattan.
