### 3A. COMIC ACCURACY REPORT
| Field | Entry |
|---|---|
| **Overall accuracy score** | **95%.** It combines four landmark storylines. The main change is timing: the Widow and Hawkeye's debut (1964) is paired with "Demon in a Bottle" (1979), and the villains' plots are woven together. |

**Straight from the page:**
- **"Demon in a Bottle":** Tony's drinking spirals. **Justin Hammer** remotely takes over the armor, and **it fires on and kills the ambassador of Carnelia** in public. Tony is disgraced, loses control of his company, and hits bottom. He **gets sober by his own choice.**
- **Justin Hammer:** a smooth, jealous rival arms industrialist (*Iron Man* #120).
- **The Mandarin:** a Chinese genius and martial-arts master who found **ten alien rings** in the wreck of a **Makluan starship** in the **Valley of Spirits** (*ToS* #50, #62).
- **The Black Widow:** a Soviet spy sent to steal Stark technology, seductive, deadly and a master of disguise. On 616 she was trained in the **Red Room** (the later 616 version), and she's a ballet dancer.
- **Hawkeye:** **Claire Barton**, a carnival sharpshooter raised in **the circus**, sees Iron Man in action at **Coney Island** and decides to become a hero. On her first night out she's **mistaken for a thief**, and **the Black Widow** takes advantage of that and recruits her against Iron Man (*ToS* #57).
- **Rhodey in the armor:** while Tony is out of action, **Rhodey wears the Iron Man armor** in combat (*Iron Man* #169–170). ("Next time, baby" pays off.)
- **The attaché-case armor:** Tony carries a portable armor in a briefcase (Silver Age).

**Changes and why:**
| Change | Why |
|---|---|
| The Widow and Hawkeye (1964) appear in the same film as "Demon in a Bottle" (1979) | Sliding timescale. It gets both of them established before *The Avengers*, and the Widow's manipulation of Tony adds to his crisis. |
| Hammer and the Mandarin work together | In 616 they were separate threats. Here Hammer is the Mandarin's Western supplier, so the two plots become one. |
| Carnelia's ambassador's death is shown off-screen | PG-13 |
| Hawkeye is Claire Barton | Per the Casting Rules. **She's always been Claire.** Nothing else changes. |

## 4. STORY
| Field | Entry |
|---|---|
| **Logline** | Tony Stark tells the world he's Iron Man, and it nearly kills him. A rival sabotages his armor, a spy works her way into his life, an ancient warlord comes for his technology, and his worst enemy is the bottle. |
| **Theme** | **Addiction and recovery.** The strongest armor in the world can't protect you from yourself. |
| **Villain / antagonist** | **The Mandarin** (main), **Justin Hammer**, and **the Black Widow and Hawkeye** (at first) |

### What the movie is about
**ACT ONE: THE PRICE OF "I AM IRON MAN"**
- **January 2010.** Tony is the most famous man on Earth and falling apart. He's drinking hard and being hauled in front of the Senate, and **Justin Hammer** is testifying against him.
- A striking new executive assistant joins Stark Industries under a cover name. She's **Natasha Romanoff**, the Black Widow, a Soviet-trained spy sent to steal the repulsor designs.
- **Monaco:** Tony suits up from his **attaché-case armor** to stop a Hammer-financed attack at the Grand Prix.

**ACT TWO: DEMON IN A BOTTLE**
- At a United Nations reception, Hammer's hackers **seize control of the armor.** It fires, and **the ambassador of Carnelia is killed.**
- Tony is disgraced. The board forces him out, and the drinking gets much worse.
- **Pepper** tells him the truth. **Rhodey** takes the Mark III: *"Next time, baby."* This is the next time.
- On **Coney Island**, circus sharpshooter **Claire Barton** stops a robbery, is mistaken for the thief, and runs. The **Widow** is waiting for her with an offer and a lie: *Iron Man is the real criminal.*
- The Widow and Hawkeye attack Tony at his lowest point. **Hawkeye's arrows** take the Mark IV apart.
- The Widow reports to her handler, whose voice on the phone is calm and sounds almost amused. It's **the Mandarin.**

**ACT THREE: THE TEN RINGS**
- Hammer has been **supplying the Mandarin**, who wants Tony's armor technology to bring back his ancient Makluan starship.
- Tony wakes up on a hotel bathroom floor. Pepper is sitting next to him, and he **pours the bottle out.** Day one.
- **The Valley of Spirits, China.** Tony (in a new Mark IV), **Rhodey** (in the Mark III) and **Hawkeye**, who has worked out she was lied to, take on **the Mandarin.**
- All ten rings are in use. The Mandarin is winning.
- **The Widow makes her choice:** she sabotages the Makluan starship's power core from inside.
- The Mandarin **retreats into the mountains**, alive, as he always does.
- Hammer is arrested. The Widow and Hawkeye **disappear** together before S.H.I.E.L.D. arrives.
- Tony gets his company back. The last shot is him at an AA meeting in a church basement: *"My name is Tony. It's been thirty-one days."*
