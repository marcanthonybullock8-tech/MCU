**Straight from the page:**
- **Hela takes mortal souls** into **Hel** (#360).
- **Thor** leads a war party into Hel: the **Warriors Three**, **Skurge**, and others (#360–361).

## 4. STORY
| Field | Entry |
|---|---|
| **Logline** | Billions of souls from a murdered world flood the halls of Hel, and its queen wants more. To take back the dead, Thor has to walk into the underworld and pay with her own body. |
| **Theme** | **Sacrifice and redemption.** Skurge was a villain, and he dies a hero. |
| **Villain / antagonist** | **Hela** |

### What the movie is about
**ACT ONE: THE FLOOD OF SOULS**
- **Monday, January 16, 2023.** **Hel is overflowing** with billions of souls from the destroyed Great Society Earth.
- **Hela**, swollen with power, **starts taking mortal souls from Midgard** too, including children in a New York hospital.
- **Doctor Strange** comes to Asgard: *"Those souls in Hel are there because of me. Help me get them back."*

**ACT TWO: INTO HEL**
- **Thor** leads a war party into Hel: **Loki**, **Sif**, **the Warriors Three**, **Beta Ray Bill**, and **Skurge the Executioner.**
- **Hela** faces Thor and **curses her**: *"You shall never die, sister of storms. But your bones will never mend."*
- Thor's bones break with every blow, and she keeps going.

**ACT THREE: GJALLERBRU**
- They free the stolen souls and **run for Gjallerbru**, the only bridge out of Hel.
- The **armies of the dead** are coming.
- **Skurge** stops on the bridge and turns around, alone, with his axe and **two rifles from Midgard.**
  > **SKURGE:** *"Go. I'll hold them."*
- **He stood alone at Gjallerbru.**
- Thor comes back to Asgard **encased in armor** to hold her broken body together. The children in the hospital wake up.
- At Skurge's funeral, **Volstagg** says only: *"Aye. He stood alone."*
