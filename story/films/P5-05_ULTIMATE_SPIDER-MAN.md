## 4. STORY
| Field | Entry |
|---|---|
| **Logline** | In another universe, where Spider-Man has just died and another Earth is filling the sky, a Brooklyn kid gets bitten by a spider and has to become a hero before his whole world ends. |
| **Theme** | **Anyone can wear the mask.** A new hero, in a universe with no time left. |
| **Villain / antagonist** | **Aaron Davis / the Prowler** (main); **the Maker** |

### What the movie is about
- **Earth-1610.** **Its Peter Parker is dead**, and there's a memorial in Queens. **Another Earth** is visible in the sky, getting closer every day: **ours.**
- **Monday, June 1, 2026.** **Miles Morales**, 16, visits **his uncle Aaron**, who's been cagey lately, and **a stolen spider bites him.**
- **Powers.** **Camouflage.** **The venom strike.** **Ganke** figures it out right away.
- Miles puts on a suit. The Ultimate **Nick Fury** watches from a distance.
- **Uncle Aaron is the Prowler**, and **he wants to use his nephew.**
- **The fight.** Miles is losing, until **the venom strike** overloads the Prowler suit. **Aaron dies** in the explosion. Miles holds him. *"You were supposed to be better than me."*
- **The Maker**, Earth-1610's **Reed Richards**, cold and brilliant, is preparing to **destroy the other Earth** to save his own.
- **Fury recruits Miles** for a war he doesn't understand yet.
