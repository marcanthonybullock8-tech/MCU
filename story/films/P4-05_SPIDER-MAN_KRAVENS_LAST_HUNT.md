### 3A. COMIC ACCURACY REPORT
| Field | Entry |
|---|---|
| **Overall accuracy score** | **97%.** The wedding and "Kraven's Last Hunt" are adapted in their 616 order. The wedding came first, and Kraven struck **weeks after it.** |

**Straight from the page:**
- **Peter and Mary Jane marry** (*ASM Annual* #21).
- **Kraven**, aging and haunted, **decides to prove he's better than Spider-Man** (*Web* #31).
- He **shoots Spider-Man with a tranquilizer rifle** and **buries him in a coffin** (*Web* #31).
- **Kraven puts on Spider-Man's costume** and **hunts criminals brutally** (*ASM* #293).
- **Vermin**, a rat-man, is loose in the sewers. **Kraven captures him alone**, which is something Spider-Man never managed (*Spectacular* #131).
- **Two weeks later, Peter claws his way out of the grave** (*Web* #32). **"Spider... spider."**
- **Mary Jane** thinks he's dead, and doesn't give up.
- **Kraven, satisfied that he's won, takes his own life** (*Spectacular* #132). *Off-screen, handled with care (PG-13).*

**Changes and why:**

## 4. STORY
| Field | Entry |
|---|---|
| **Logline** | Peter Parker has just married the love of his life, and a hunter who has spent his life chasing him buries him alive to prove he can be a better Spider-Man. |
| **Theme** | **Identity and resurrection.** Who's the real Spider-Man: the costume, or the man who keeps coming back? |
| **Villain / antagonist** | **Kraven the Hunter** (main); **Vermin** |

### What the movie is about
**ACT ONE: THE WEDDING**
- **Saturday, November 19, 2022.** **Peter Parker and Mary Jane Watson are married** in City Hall. Aunt May cries. Tony Stark is the best man's backup. Jameson grumbles, and cries.
- **Kraven**, older and consumed by his obsession, watches from a rooftop.

**ACT TWO: THE GRAVE**
- **A tranquilizer dart.** Spider-Man falls.
- **Kraven buries him** in a coffin in a country graveyard.
- **Kraven puts on the costume** and **becomes Spider-Man**, brutal and effective. New York is terrified.
- **Mary Jane**, alone in their new apartment, waits. Days pass. **Two weeks.**
- In the sewers, **Vermin** hunts. **Kraven**, as Spider-Man, **captures him alone.**

**ACT THREE: SPIDER**
- **The grave.** Earth, darkness, and a hand **punches up through the dirt.**
- Peter, filthy and furious, walks back into the city.
- **He faces Vermin** in the sewers and wins.
- **Kraven waits for him**: *"I proved it, Parker. I was you. And I was better."*
- Kraven lets Spider-Man go. In the next scene, alone in his mansion, **a single gunshot.** Kraven's peace.
- **Mary Jane** opens the door and holds her husband. **Peter:** *"I'm home."*
