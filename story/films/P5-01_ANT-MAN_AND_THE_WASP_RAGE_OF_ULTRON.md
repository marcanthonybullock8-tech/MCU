### What the movie is about
**ACT ONE: THE NEW ANT-MAN**
- **Monday, September 30, 2024.** **Scott Lang**, an ex-con working Stark security, **steals the Ant-Man suit from Pym Labs** to save his kidnapped daughter **Cassie.**
- Hank catches him, and **lets him keep the suit**: *"You did it for your kid. That's what the suit is for."*
- Hank and Janet are in a quiet stretch of their marriage. **Hank hasn't slept since the Incursions started.**

**ACT TWO: TITAN**
- **Eros and Mentor** crash-land on Earth: **Ultron has taken Titan.**
- The Avengers go: **the Wasp** (leading), **Yellowjacket**, **Ant-Man**, **the Vision**, **Cap**, **Iron Man.**
- **Titan is a machine world.** Its people are **assimilated.** Ultron is in his **adamantium body**, and **nothing hurts him.**
- **Ultron takes over the Vision.**

**ACT THREE: RAGE**
- Hank understands. *"He's my son. He's always been part of me."*
- He **merges with Ultron.** One being, **Hank and Ultron together**, their minds at war.
- **Titan's people are freed.** The hybrid **flies off into space**, and **Hank's voice** comes over the comm: *"Janet. I'm sorry. I have to take him somewhere he can't hurt anyone."*
- Janet, alone on Titan, **watches her husband go.**
- On Earth, **Scott Lang** puts on the helmet. **Ant-Man.** Cassie grins.
