## 4. STORY
| Field | Entry |
|---|---|
| **Logline** | In a city where every citizen has to live inside a suit of armor, somebody is murdering people through their suits, and Baron Tony Stark's brother wants his throne. |
| **Theme** | **Responsibility for what you build.** |
| **Villain / antagonist** | **ARNO STARK** (main); **the Ghost** |

### What the movie is about
**ACT ONE: TECHNOPOLIS**
- **Technopolis**: a shining, crowded city where **nobody takes their armor off.** Outside it, **a plague.**
- **Baron Tony Stark** rules under God Doom. He's rich, adored, and **bored.**
- **A citizen dies inside his armor.** Then another. The suits **were switched off from inside.**

**ACT TWO: THE GHOST**
- **Rhodey** investigates. **The Ghost**, a saboteur who **walks through walls**, is **the killer.**
- The Ghost was **paid by Arno Stark**, Tony's **smarter, colder brother.**
- **Arno's plan:** **make the city afraid of Tony's armor**, and **sell them his.**

**ACT THREE: ARMOR WARS**
- **Tony's choice (1987):** his **stolen designs** are in **every one of Arno's suits.** **He shuts them all down**, and **he's the enemy of the whole city** for one night.
- **Tony vs. Arno**, armor to armor, **over the Shield wall.**
- **Tony wins.** **Arno is banished** into the wastes.
- **Sheriff Strange** arrives, not to arrest Tony but to **look at him for a long time.** *"Do you remember a place called New York, Baron?"* Tony: *"...Should I?"*
