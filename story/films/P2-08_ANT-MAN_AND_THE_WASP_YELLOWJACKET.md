### 3A. COMIC ACCURACY REPORT
| Field | Entry |
|---|---|
| **Overall accuracy score** | **97%.** *Avengers* #59–60 is adapted beat for beat. |

**Straight from the page:**
- Hank, overwhelmed by guilt over Ultron, **inhales chemical fumes in his lab** during an experiment.
- **A new personality appears: Yellowjacket**, brash and arrogant in a yellow-and-black costume, who **claims to have killed Hank Pym** (#59).
- **Janet is the only one who figures out that Yellowjacket *is* Hank.** She **agrees to marry him** anyway, knowing Hank is in there (#59–60).
- **The wedding at Avengers Mansion**, with **the whole team there** (#60).
- **The Circus of Crime** (the Ringmaster, with his hypnotic top hat) **crashes the wedding** to rob the guests (#60).
- In the fight, **Hank's real personality comes back.** Janet and Hank are **married** (#60).

**Changes and why:**
| Change | Why |
|---|---|
| Hank's guilt is tied directly to Ultron | MCU continuity. On 616, Ultron (#54–58) came right before Yellowjacket (#59–60), and we make the cause explicit. |
| Scott Lang's seed (1979) | It sets up the next Ant-Man. On 616, **Scott Lang worked for Stark International.** |

**How the mental-health story is handled:** Hank's identity crisis is treated **seriously and with compassion.** It's a man breaking under guilt, and the people who love him bring him back. It's played as a story about **recovery.**

## 4. STORY
| Field | Entry |
|---|---|
| **Logline** | Guilt over creating Ultron breaks Hank Pym into someone else, and the only person who can bring him back is the woman who's about to marry him. |
| **Theme** | **Love as recovery.** You don't have to be whole to be loved. |
| **Villain / antagonist** | **The Ringmaster and the Circus of Crime**; also **Hank's own guilt** |

### What the movie is about
**ACT ONE: GUILT**
- **Monday, May 18, 2015.** A month after Ultron, **Hank Pym** isn't sleeping. He's in his Cresskill lab around the clock trying to "fix" what he made.
- **Janet** watches him disappear into the work.
- An experiment goes wrong. **Chemical fumes**, a collapse.

**ACT TWO: YELLOWJACKET**
- A **new hero in yellow and black** bursts into Avengers Mansion: brash, cocky and **brilliant**. He calls himself **Yellowjacket** and says he **killed Hank Pym.**
- The Avengers nearly take him down. Janet stops them. She's watching his hands. **It's Hank.**
- Janet: *"Then marry me."* Yellowjacket, stunned: *"...Sure. Why not."*
- Tony, Cap and the rest are horrified. Janet: *"He's in there. I'm not giving up on him."*

**ACT THREE: THE WEDDING**
- **Saturday, June 20, 2015. Avengers Mansion.** The wedding: every Avenger in formal wear, Jarvis serving champagne, and **Stan Lee** officiating.
- **The Circus of Crime** crashes it. The **Ringmaster** hypnotizes the guests and robs them.
- **Janet** isn't hypnotized. She takes on the Circus alone while the Avengers are frozen.
- The Ringmaster aims at Janet. **Yellowjacket** gets between them, and **Hank comes back** in the middle of the fight.
- Hank and Janet, **married**, fight the Circus side by side.
- The Ringmaster is taken down, and the Avengers wake up covered in cake.
- On a rooftop at night, Hank and Janet, both shrunk to half an inch, sit on the edge of a champagne glass: *"Partners?"* *"Husband."*
