**Straight from the page:**
- **The night the heroes lost**: **Mysterio tricks Logan into killing the X-Men**, who he thinks are villains (#72).

- **The Hulk Gang**, **Banner's inbred grandchildren**, **collect the rent** (#66).
- **Blind Hawkeye hires Logan** to drive **a package** to **New Babylon** (#66).
- **The road trip across America** in **the Spider-Mobile**: **Kingpin Territory**, **Hammer Falls** (where Thor's hammer fell), **a symbiote-infected dinosaur**, and **Ashley Barton as Spider-Woman** (#67–70).
- **The Red Skull is President**, in **Captain America's** costume. **Hawkeye is killed** at **New Babylon**, and **Logan kills the Skull with Cap's shield, without popping his claws** (#70–71).
- **The Hulk Gang kills Logan's family** (#71).
- **Logan pops his claws**, and **kills the Hulk Gang** (#71–72).
- **The old Hulk eats him**, and **Logan cuts his way out** (Giant-Size).

## 4. STORY
| Field | Entry |
|---|---|
| **Logline** | Fifty years after the villains won, a gentle old farmer who used to be called Wolverine has to drive across a dead America, and his claws are the one thing he promised never to use again. |
| **Theme** | **Peace has a price.** Logan buried the Wolverine, and the world dug him up. |
| **Villain / antagonist** | **THE HULK GANG** and **the Red Skull**; **God Doom** above them |

### What the movie is about
**ACT ONE: THE FARMER**
- **The Wastelands.** A dusty California farm. **Logan**, gray, gentle, **hasn't popped his claws in 50 years.**
- **The Hulk Gang** takes the rent, and **beats him in front of his son.** He takes it.
- **Claire Barton**, old and **blind**, pulls up in the **Spider-Mobile**: *"I need a driver, Logan. Coast to coast. Pays enough to feed your family for a year."*

**ACT TWO: ROUTE 66**
- **Coast to coast** in the Spider-Mobile, across the wreck of America: **Kingpin Territory**; **Hammer Falls**; a **symbiote dinosaur on the highway**.
- **The flashback**: the night the heroes lost. **Mysterio** makes Logan see villains, and **Logan kills the X-Men.** When the illusion drops, **he's standing in the blood of his friends.**
- **Ashley Barton**, dressed as Spider-Woman, **betrays her mother.**
- **New Babylon.** The Red Skull, President of the Wastelands, **in Captain America's costume.** **Claire is shot down.** Her last words: *"Pop 'em, Logan. Just once."*
- **He doesn't.** Logan **kills the Red Skull with Captain America's shield**, and goes home with the money.

**ACT THREE: THE WOLVERINE**
- Logan comes home: **his family is dead**, killed by **the Hulk Gang.**
- **Snikt.**
- **Logan kills the Hulk Gang.** **Old Bruce Banner** turns into **the biggest Hulk ever**, and **eats him.** **Logan cuts his way out.**
- He takes **the Hulk's infant grandson** in his arms.
- **He rides out of the Wastelands**, **past the edge of the Wastelands**, and runs into **a wall of lights.** **A dozen Thors** land around him. **Doctor Strange**, Sheriff of Battleworld: ***"Logan. You've crossed a border. In the name of God Doom, you're under arrest."*** Logan: ***"...Who?"***
