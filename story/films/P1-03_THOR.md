### 3A. COMIC ACCURACY REPORT
| Field | Entry |
|---|---|
| **Overall accuracy score** | **97%.** This is the Silver Age origin almost exactly, with *Thor* #159's explanation built in from the start. Thor is female, **as she's always been in our universe**, and nothing else about the character changes. |

**Straight from the page:**
- A physician with a lame leg, vacationing in **Norway**, flees the **Stone Men from Saturn** (the Kronans), hides in a cave, and finds a **gnarled walking stick.** When she **strikes it against the ground**, it becomes **Mjolnir** and she becomes **Thor** (*JiM* #83).
- **The inscription is word for word:** *"Whosoever holds this hammer, if he be worthy, shall possess the power of Thor."* The comic's wording is kept exactly.
- **The 60-second rule:** if Thor is separated from Mjolnir for **sixty seconds**, she **turns back into Dr. Blake** (Silver Age rule).
- **Odin created the mortal Dr. Blake** so his proud, reckless heir would learn humility (*Thor* #159).
- **Loki is imprisoned inside a tree** by Odin's magic, and escapes when **a single leaf falls** (*JiM* #85). This is the Silver Age detail, taken straight from the page.
- **Loki is Laufey's son**, a Frost Giant child that Odin adopted (*JiM* #112–113).
- **Jane Foster** is Dr. Blake's devoted nurse (*JiM* #84).
- **Sif, the Warriors Three and Heimdall** guard the **Bifrost**, the Rainbow Bridge.
- At the end, **Loki is exiled to the Isle of Silence**, which is exactly where he's plotting in the first panel of ***Avengers* #1.**

**Changes and why:**
| Change | Why |
|---|---|
| **Dr. Donald Blake** becomes **Dr. Donna Blake** | Thor has always been female in our universe, and the mortal identity follows. It's the only change that requires. |
| Thor's first battle with the Stone Men and her first fight with Loki (#83 and #85) happen in one film | Runtime |
| Loki's heritage reveal (1965) moves into the first film | It gives Loki his motive from the start |

| Field | Entry |

| **Theme** | **Worthiness.** Power doesn't come from blood. You earn it. |
| **Villain / antagonist** | **Loki**, the God of Mischief |

### What the movie is about
**PROLOGUE: ASGARD**
- **Thor** is brilliant and unbeatable, and she's also arrogant. Against Odin's orders she starts a war with the Frost Giants of Jotunheim.
- Odin is furious. He takes her memory and her godhood, and sends her to Midgard as **Dr. Donna Blake**, a gifted physician with a lame leg, who has a full mortal life and no idea who she really is.
- He sends **Mjolnir** too, disguised and hidden.

**ACT ONE: THE CANE**
- **Norway, Monday, February 16, 2009.** Dr. Blake is on a medical conference vacation with her nurse and closest friend, **Jane Foster**.
- The **Stone Men from Saturn**, a Kronan scouting party, land in the fjords.
- Blake is caught alone and hides in a cave, where she finds **a gnarled old walking stick.** Trapped, she strikes it against the rock.
- **THOOM.** Thunder. **Thor** stands where Blake was, holding **Mjolnir.**
- She wipes out the Kronan force in a lightning storm over the fjords. Then she changes back to Blake, **terrified and exhilarated.** Who *is* she?

**ACT TWO: THE TRICKSTER**
- In **Asgard**, **Loki** has been **imprisoned inside a tree** for his part in the Frost Giant war. **A single leaf falls**, and he's free.
- He finds his real parentage in Odin's vault: **Laufey's son**, a Frost Giant child Odin took and raised.
- Loki makes a deal with Laufey: an open path to Midgard, and Thor's life, in exchange for the throne.
- In **New York**, Blake's memories come back in pieces: Asgard, a brother, a hammer.
- Loki attacks in disguise. At one point he **takes Jane's form** and gets Mjolnir away from Thor. When the **sixty seconds** run out, Thor turns back into Blake on the Brooklyn Bridge with Frost Giants closing in.

**ACT THREE: WORTHY**
- Blake, **mortal and limping**, puts herself between the Frost Giants and the civilians on the bridge to protect them.
- In that moment she's **worthy**. Mjolnir tears itself free of Loki's spell and flies back to her hand, and her memory comes back.
- Thor, the Warriors Three and Sif drive the Frost Giants back through the Bifrost and duel Loki above the **Rainbow Bridge.**
- **Odin** sentences Loki to **exile on the Isle of Silence.**
- Thor chooses to **keep her mortal life** as Blake and protect Midgard. Jane is there waiting for her.

## 6. WORLD-BUILDING
### The film's own world
- **The Nine Realms**, **Asgard** and its court, the **Bifrost** and **Heimdall**.
- **Mjolnir's rules:** worthiness, the 60-second rule, the Blake transformation.
- **Loki**, the brother the family never admitted was adopted.
- **Jane Foster** and Blake's mortal life in New York.
