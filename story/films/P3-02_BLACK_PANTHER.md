### 3A. COMIC ACCURACY REPORT
| Field | Entry |
|---|---|
| **Overall accuracy score** | **96%.** "Panther's Rage" is adapted with its structure intact, and *FF* #52–53 supplies the backstory. |

**Straight from the page:**
- **Wakanda**: an isolated, hyper-advanced African nation built on the world's only **vibranium** mound (*FF* #52).
- **Klaw murdered King T'Chaka** when T'Challa was a boy. **Young T'Challa destroyed Klaw's right hand** (*FF* #53).
- **The Heart-Shaped Herb** gives the Black Panther his powers, and **the Panther God Bast** chooses the king (616).
- **Erik Killmonger**, **born N'Jadaka in Wakanda**, was exiled when Klaw took his family. He grew up in the U.S. and came back **to take the throne** (*Jungle Action* #6).
- **Killmonger throws T'Challa over Warrior Falls** (*Jungle Action* #6). **T'Challa survives, and returns** (#6–18).
- **W'Kabi**, T'Challa's security chief, **wavers in his loyalty** (*Jungle Action*).
- **The Dora Milaje**, the king's elite guard (Priest).
- **M'Baku** challenges T'Challa for the throne, and **becomes Man-Ape** (*Avengers* #62).
- **Shuri**, T'Challa's brilliant sister (vol. 4).

**Changes and why:**
| Change | Why |
|---|---|
| Nakia's 616 turn to villainy (as Malice) is held back | It's held for a sequel. She's loyal in this film. |
| Klaw's appearance is a cameo | He was the villain in *Ultron Unlimited*'s mid-credit scene, and this finishes his story |

## 4. STORY
| Field | Entry |
|---|---|
| **Logline** | The new king of the most advanced nation on Earth comes home to take his father's throne, and finds that a Wakandan exile with a claim of his own has come home first. |
| **Theme** | **What does a king owe the world?** Wakanda has hidden itself for centuries. Killmonger asks whether it had the right to. |
| **Villain / antagonist** | **N'Jadaka / Erik Killmonger** (main); **Klaw**; **M'Baku** (a rival who becomes an ally) |

### What the movie is about
**ACT ONE: THE KING**
- **Wakanda, Monday, April 25, 2016.** Two weeks after Civil War, **T'Challa** comes home to be crowned.
- **M'Baku** of the Jabari challenges him at Warrior Falls. T'Challa wins, and **spares him.**
- **Klaw** surfaces in **Busan**, selling stolen vibranium. T'Challa, **Nakia** and **Okoye** go after him in a casino and on a car chase through the city.
- Klaw's buyer shoots him and takes the vibranium. The buyer is **Erik Killmonger.**

**ACT TWO: WARRIOR FALLS**
- Killmonger arrives in Wakanda with **Klaw's body** as proof. **He's Wakandan**: born **N'Jadaka**, exiled as a child, and the son of a Wakandan the royal family abandoned.
- He demands **ritual combat for the throne.**
- **At Warrior Falls, Killmonger beats T'Challa and throws him over the falls.**
- **Killmonger becomes king**, takes the **Heart-Shaped Herb**, and orders that **Wakandan weapons be shipped to the world's oppressed.**
- **W'Kabi** backs him. Okoye stays loyal to the throne, whoever sits on it.

**ACT THREE: PANTHER'S RAGE**
- **M'Baku** has T'Challa's broken body, which the Jabari pulled out of the river.
- **Bast** speaks to T'Challa in the ancestral plane, and **his father** asks him: *"What will you do differently?"*
- T'Challa comes back. **Shuri** gives him a new suit.
- **The battle for Wakanda**: the Jabari, the Dora Milaje and **the whole nation divided.**
- **T'Challa vs. Killmonger** in the vibranium mines. **T'Challa wins.**
- Killmonger, dying, refuses to be healed: *"Bury me in the ocean with my ancestors who jumped from the ships."*
- **T'Challa takes Wakanda into the world**: an outreach center in Oakland, and a seat at the United Nations: *"Wakanda will no longer watch from the shadows."*
