## 4. STORY
| Field | Entry |
|---|---|
| **Logline** | A cosmic herald leads a planet-eating god to Earth, the way he has led him to a thousand other worlds. A blind woman's kindness reminds him what it was like to be a man, and he betrays his master to save a world that isn't his. |
| **Theme** | **Sacrifice.** Norrin gave up his world, his love and his soul to save Zenn-La. On Earth he learns there's still something he can give up. |
| **Villain / antagonist** | **Galactus** (a force of nature, not evil); **Thanos** (post-credit) |

### What the movie is about
**ACT ONE: THE HERALD**
- **Zenn-La, long ago.** **Norrin Radd**, a scientist who longs for the stars, watches **Galactus** arrive to devour his peaceful world. Norrin offers himself **as herald** in exchange for Zenn-La's survival. Galactus accepts, and Norrin is covered in silver and cut off from his heart. **Shalla-Bal** is left behind (flashback).
- **Monday, August 10, 2015.** On the Moon, **Uatu the Watcher** **breaks his oath** and hides Earth behind a false fire in the sky.
- **The Silver Surfer** finds Earth anyway, and **calls his master.**

**ACT TWO: THE DEVOURER**
- **Galactus** arrives over New York and starts building his machine on top of **the Baxter Building.**
- **The Fantastic Four** attack, and **it's like fighting weather.**
- The Surfer fights the FF and wins. He's **hurt**, falls, and crashes through the skylight of **Alicia Masters.**
- Alicia, who is blind, can't see what he is. She sees who he is, and she talks to him about beauty, about life, and about being human.
- **For the first time in centuries, Norrin Radd feels something.**

**ACT THREE: THE ULTIMATE NULLIFIER**
- The Surfer **turns on Galactus.** The Power Cosmic against the Power Cosmic, over Manhattan.
- **Uatu** sends **Johnny Storm** across the universe to Galactus's worldship to get the one weapon Galactus fears: **the Ultimate Nullifier.**
- **Reed** stands on the Baxter Building roof holding the Nullifier. **Galactus backs down.**
- Galactus leaves, and **punishes his herald**: *"You have chosen this world. Then you shall never leave it."* He puts **an invisible barrier** around Earth.
- The last scene is **the Surfer, alone**, flying at the barrier over and over, then turning his face toward the stars he'll never reach again. On a rooftop below, Alicia waits.
