## 4. STORY
| Field | Entry |
|---|---|
| **Logline** | To save our universe, Earth's smartest heroes decide someone else's world has to die. Stephen Strange is the one who pays for it. |
| **Theme** | **The price of power.** Strange gives up his soul to save the world. Was it worth it? |
| **Villain / antagonist** | **The Black Priests** (main); **the Great Society** (heroes on the other side); **the Illuminati's own consciences** |

### What the movie is about
**ACT ONE: EVERYTHING DIES**
- **Monday, October 4, 2021**, the morning after the Namor post-credit scene. **Shuri** summons the **Illuminati** to Wakanda.
- The **Black Swan** explains: **Incursions.** If two Earths meet, both universes die, unless **one Earth is destroyed.**
- **Xavier refuses**: *"I won't be party to genocide."* **Beast** comes in his place.
- **Captain America**, invited by Tony, **refuses.** *"We don't trade lives."*
- **The Illuminati wipe Cap's memory of the meeting.** Strange casts the spell, and his hands are shaking.

**ACT TWO: A PERFECT WORLD**
- **Saturday, March 19, 2022.** A second Incursion. **Another Earth** hangs over the Pacific. Its heroes, **the Great Society**, are coming **to destroy ours.**
- **The Ancient One**, dying, makes Strange **Sorcerer Supreme**: *"Now it is on you, Stephen."* She dies.
- The Illuminati fight the Great Society on the edge of both worlds. **They're losing.**

**ACT THREE: THE BARGAIN**
- Strange goes to a realm he's never been to, and **makes a deal.**
- He **comes back changed**: his eyes are black, and his voice has too many echoes.
- **Strange destroys the Great Society's Earth.** Billions die in an instant. Our universe is saved.
- **The Black Priests** arrive to **collect their price.** Strange fights them off, but they'll be back.
- In the Sanctum, **Wong** watches Strange scrub his hands over and over. **Clea**: *"Stephen. What did you give them?"* He doesn't answer.
