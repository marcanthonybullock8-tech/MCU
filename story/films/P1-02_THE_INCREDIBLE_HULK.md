### 3A. COMIC ACCURACY REPORT
| Field | Entry |
|---|---|
| **Overall accuracy score** | **97%.** The origin is taken almost panel for panel from *Hulk* #1, including the **gray Hulk** and the **night-time transformations**. The changes are sliding-timescale updates. |

**Straight from the page:**
- **The gamma bomb (G-bomb) test** at **Desert Base, New Mexico.** Banner sees a teenager, **Rick Jones**, drive onto the test site on a dare. He runs out to save him and is caught in the blast after a saboteur **delays the countdown**.
- **The first Hulk is GRAY** (*Hulk* #1) and **changes back at sunrise.** The Hulk **turns green** in the middle of the film (*Hulk* #2), and by the end **anger** is what triggers the change (the later 616 rule).
- **Rick Jones** feels responsible, keeps the secret, and tries to take care of the Hulk.
- **General "Thunderbolt" Ross** hunts the Hulk while his daughter **Betty** loves Banner.
- **Emil Blonsky** is a **Soviet-trained Yugoslavian-born spy** who deliberately gives himself a massive gamma dose with Banner's own equipment and becomes the **Abomination** (*TtA* #90).
- The gray Hulk is cunning and talks in full sentences. The green Hulk is simpler and angrier. **"HULK SMASH!"** comes from the page.

**Changes and why:**
| Change | Why |
|---|---|
| The Cold War saboteur Igor Starsky becomes **Blonsky's handler**, an ex-Soviet intelligence network that's still active | Sliding timescale. The Cold War setup is updated, and the roles are the same. |
| The Abomination arrives in the same year as the Hulk (1967 in the comics) | Runtime. He's the Hulk's greatest physical rival, so he headlines the first film. |
| Banner's age is **26** (b. **December 18, 1981**) | Matches Amond per the real-time rule. |

## 4. STORY
| Field | Entry |
|---|---|
| **Logline** | A brilliant scientist saves a boy from his own bomb and becomes the monster the Army built it to fight. |
| **Theme** | **The monster we make of each other.** Banner is hunted by the man who should have protected him. |
| **Villain / antagonist** | **Emil Blonsky / the Abomination** (main); **Gen. Thunderbolt Ross** (the relentless hunter) |

### What the movie is about
**ACT ONE: THE BOMB**
- **Desert Base, New Mexico, Monday, March 3, 2008.** Dr. Bruce Banner's G-bomb is ready to test. At the countdown, a jittery teenager, **Rick Jones**, drives onto the range on a dare.
- Banner orders a hold and runs out to save him. His assistant, secretly an ex-Soviet intelligence asset, **doesn't hold the countdown.** Banner shoves Rick into a trench and takes the full blast.
- He survives without a mark. That night at sunset, **the gray Hulk** tears out of the base hospital.
- Rick sees it happen and understands what he's looking at. By sunrise the monster is Banner again.

**ACT TWO: THE HUNT**
- Ross declares the "gray thing" an enemy of the state and launches the hunt. It's the story Tony Stark's workshop TV reports in *Iron Man*.
- Banner and Rick go on the run across the Southwest while **Betty** hides them from her father.
- Midway through, the Hulk changes **from gray to green**, and the change starts coming **with anger instead of darkness.** Banner can't hide in daylight anymore.
- **Emil Blonsky**, a mercenary who has joined Ross's task force, is actually selling Banner's gamma research to the handlers who arranged the "accident."
- The Hulk wrecks Blonsky's unit at the Hoover Dam, and it's the humiliation of Blonsky's life.

**ACT THREE: THE ABOMINATION**
- Blonsky breaks into Banner's old lab and gives himself **a massive gamma dose**, becoming **the Abomination**, who is permanent and fully aware.
- He tears through Albuquerque to draw the Hulk out.
- **Round one:** the Abomination is stronger, and the Hulk loses.
- **Round two:** after the Abomination nearly kills Rick and Betty, the Hulk's rage **keeps climbing past every limit**, until he's stronger than anything Blonsky can match.
- Ross watches the Hulk save his daughter and his soldiers.
- Banner escapes. The last image is a green giant leaping into the New Mexico night while Rick watches from a hilltop.

## 6. WORLD-BUILDING
### The film's own world
- **Desert Base**, the **G-bomb program**, the **gray, then green** Hulk, and the anger rule.
- **Rick Jones** as the Hulk's conscience, **Betty**, and **Ross**, the father who hunts the man his daughter loves.
- The gamma **"family" starts here**: the Abomination now, and the Leader later.
