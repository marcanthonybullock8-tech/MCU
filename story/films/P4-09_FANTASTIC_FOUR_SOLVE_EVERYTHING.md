| Field | Entry |
|---|---|
| **Overall accuracy score** | **96%.** Hickman's "Solve Everything" and "Three" are adapted closely. |

**Straight from the page:**
- **The Council of Reeds**: Reeds from other universes who **gave up their families** to solve every problem. **Reed joins them, then leaves** to go home (#570–572).
- **Valeria Richards**, a genius at age five, **secretly asks Doom for help** (#579). **Doom's intellect had been damaged**, and Valeria helps restore it.
- **"Three":** **Johnny Storm** holds the **Negative Zone portal** alone against **Annihilus** and his armies, and **dies** (#587).
- **The Fantastic Four becomes the Future Foundation**, and **Doom** joins it (*FF* #1, 2011).

**Changes and why:**
| Change | Why |
|---|---|
| The Council of Reeds is the **first on-screen look at other realities**, which **explains the Incursions** | It ties the film into the saga. Reed learns from other Reeds that **their universes are dying too.** |
| **Cap remembers** in the post-credit scene | It sets up **Phase 5, *Time Runs Out***. On 616, Cap remembers the Illuminati's mind wipe in Hickman's *Avengers* (2014). |

## 4. STORY
| Field | Entry |
|---|---|
| **Logline** | Reed Richards meets a council of himself from a hundred other universes who've solved everything but love, just as the multiverse starts to die and one of the Fantastic Four stays behind so the others can live. |
| **Theme** | **Family is the answer.** Reed can't solve everything. He can only choose who to save. |
| **Villain / antagonist** | **Annihilus** (main); **the Council of Reeds** (Reed's temptation); **Doom** (who, for once, is an ally) |

### What the movie is about
**ACT ONE: THE COUNCIL**
- **Monday, June 3, 2024.** The Baxter Building. Reed, haunted by the Incursions, builds **a bridge into the multiverse.**
- He finds **the Council of Reeds**: **hundreds of Reed Richardses** from other Earths, who gave up their families to "solve everything."
- They show him **the truth**: **their universes are dying too. Every Earth is colliding with another one.**
- Reed stays with them for days, then **goes home** to Sue, Franklin and Valeria.

**ACT TWO: VALERIA'S BARGAIN**
- Five-year-old **Valeria**, a genius, **goes to Latveria alone** and **asks Doom for help**: *"Father can't solve this alone. Neither can you. Together, maybe."*
- **Doom joins the Future Foundation.** Reed and Doom work **side by side** for the first time.
- A breach **opens to the Negative Zone**, and **Annihilus** and his **Annihilation Wave** pour through.

**ACT THREE: THREE**
- The Baxter Building is under siege. The portal has to close from **the other side.**
- **Johnny Storm** goes through. ***"Go. I've got this."***
- He **fires the nova flame** for the first time and holds off millions. **The portal closes.**
- **Johnny is gone.**
- The **funeral**: the FF, **Doom at the edge of the crowd**, Spider-Man in the back row. **The Fantastic Four becomes three.**
- Reed looks up at the sky, where **three other Earths are now faintly visible.** Time is running out.
