## 4. STORY
| Field | Entry |
|---|---|
| **Logline** | In one corner of Doom's Battleworld there's a Peter Parker who chose his family over the fight, and eight years later his daughter is starting to stick to walls. |
| **Theme** | **With great power...** and **great love.** Is it ever wrong to choose the people you love? |
| **Villain / antagonist** | **REGENT** |

### What the movie is about
**ACT ONE: THE CHOICE**
- **The Regency domain.** Peter and MJ, married, with a baby girl. **Venom** attacks, and **Peter can't save his family and his friends at the same time.**
- He chooses **MJ and Annie.**
- **The Regent** beats **the heroes**, and **takes their powers.** Peter watches from a window with his daughter in his arms.

**ACT TWO: EIGHT YEARS LATER**
- **Peter is a science teacher.** **Annie**, eight, **catches a falling glass before it falls.** Peter goes white.
- **The Regent's enforcers** police the city. **Anyone with powers disappears.**
- **Annie's powers** can't be hidden. **The Regent's hunters** find the family.
- **Peter puts the mask back on**, and it's **the most joyful fight in the franchise.**

**ACT THREE: THE PARKERS**
- **The Regent's prison**: **dozens of heroes** plugged into his machine.
- **MJ** in **her own suit**, **Annie in a homemade Spiderling mask.** They fight **as a family.**
- **Peter and Annie** crack the machine. **The heroes wake up.**
- **The Regent falls.** And **Regency is free.**
- **The last shot:** Peter, MJ and Annie on a rooftop at sunset, looking at **Doom's statue** on the horizon. Annie: *"Who's that?"* Peter: *"...The guy who owns everything, sweetheart."*
