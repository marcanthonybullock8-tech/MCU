## 4. STORY
| Field | Entry |
|---|---|
| **Logline** | Across three thousand years, something is butchering the gods. It takes three Thors (young, present, and the last god at the end of time) to stop the God Butcher. |
| **Theme** | **Worth.** What is a god for, if not for the people who pray to her? |
| **Villain / antagonist** | **GORR THE GOD BUTCHER** |

### What the movie is about
**ACT ONE: THREE ERAS**
- **893 A.D., Iceland.** **Young Thor**, a drunken brawler with an axe, finds **a god nailed to a tree.**
- **Monday, May 3, 2027.** **Thor, ruler of Asgard**, answers a prayer from a girl on a world where **the gods have all gone quiet.** Their temple is **full of dead gods.**
- **The end of time.** **Old King Thor**, alone on the ruins of Asgard, **on a dead Earth**, **fights the God Butcher's shadow army.**

**ACT TWO: THE GOD BUTCHER**
- **Gorr's origin**: a starving desert world, his wife and his son dead in the sand, **the gods silent.** He finds **All-Black.**
- **Young Thor fights Gorr in 893**, and **loses badly.**
- **Thor of the present** follows Gorr's trail **through time**, to **the end of the universe.**
- **The Godbomb**: **thousands of enslaved gods** are building it. When it goes off, **every god in every era dies.**

**ACT THREE: GODBOMB**
- **Three Thors in one place.** They **don't like each other.** The young one is a drunk, and the old one is **bitter.** They have to learn **why they're the same person.**
- **The slave-gods rise.** The three Thors fight together.
- **King Thor** takes **the Godbomb's power** and **destroys Gorr.**
- **Agger**, Gorr's son, is **freed.**
- King Thor, to the present Thor: ***"You won't find Gorr's victims in my time. You'll find somebody else's. Something bigger. It's killing gods in every universe. Not one at a time. All of them."***
