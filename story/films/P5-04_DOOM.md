**Straight from the page:**
- **Victor** is born to **Romani** parents in **Latveria**. His mother **Cynthia**, a witch, **makes a pact with Mephisto** for power to protect her people, and **dies for it** (*Books of Doom* #1).
- **At Empire State University** he meets **Reed Richards.** His **machine to contact the dead** **explodes** (#2).
- **In Tibet**, he forges **the mask** and **puts it on while it's still red-hot** (#3).
- **He takes Latveria** (#4–6).
- **Every Midsummer's Eve** Doom duels Mephisto for his mother's soul. **Doctor Strange**, whom he calls "the Sorcerer Supreme," **helps him** (*Triumph and Torment*).
- **Doom frees Cynthia's soul, and she ascends** (*Triumph and Torment*).
- **The Molecule Man**, **Owen Reece**, can **rearrange matter at the atomic level.** On 616 he's the **Beyonders' bomb** in every universe (*Secret Wars*, 2015).

## 4. STORY
| Field | Entry |
|---|---|
| **Logline** | Victor von Doom has spent his life trying to save one soul. With every universe dying, he goes down into hell to take his mother back from the devil before there's nothing left to save. |
| **Theme** | **Love and pride.** Everything Doom does, he does for someone he loves, **and he'll never admit it.** |
| **Villain / antagonist** | **Mephisto** (Doom is the protagonist) |

### What the movie is about
**ACT ONE: THE BOOKS OF DOOM**
- **Monday, October 6, 2025.** Castle Doom. **Doom dictates his life story** to a scribe, and his narration frames the whole film.
- **Flashbacks:** a Romani camp in the Latverian hills. **Cynthia von Doom**, a healer and witch, makes a **pact with Mephisto** to protect her people, and **pays with her soul.** Young Victor watches her die.
- **Empire State University.** **Reed Richards.** The machine explodes. **Tibet.** **The red-hot mask.**

**ACT TWO: MIDSUMMER'S EVE**
- **The present.** Doom has seen the Incursions and the Beyonders. **The universe has a year left, maybe less.** If everything dies, **his mother dies with it, still in Mephisto's hands.**
- Doom goes to the **Sanctum Sanctorum** and **asks Stephen Strange for help.** Doom doesn't ask. He **informs.**
- **Strange**, carrying the guilt of the world he destroyed, **agrees.**

**ACT THREE: TRIUMPH AND TORMENT**
- **Mephisto's realm.** Doom and Strange **fight their way through hell.**
- **Mephisto** offers Doom everything, including his mother's soul **in exchange for his own.**
- **Doom tricks Mephisto** and binds him with his own contract.
- **Cynthia's soul is freed.** She touches Victor's mask. ***"My son."*** **She ascends.**
- Doom, alone, **takes off his mask**, and we see him **only from behind.**
- **He puts it back on.**
