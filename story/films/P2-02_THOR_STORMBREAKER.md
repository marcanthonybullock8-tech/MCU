**Straight from the page:**
- **S.H.I.E.L.D. detects an unknown starship** heading for Earth, and **Nick Fury** calls in Thor (*Thor* #337).
- **Beta Ray Bill**, champion of the **Korbinites**, a race whose home galaxy was destroyed by **Surtur's demons**, fights Thor. When Thor is **separated from Mjolnir for sixty seconds**, she **turns back into Blake.** Bill picks up the cane, strikes it, and **gets the power of Thor** (#337).
- **Odin summons "Thor" to Asgard and Bill arrives.** Odin orders **a trial by combat** on the fiery world of **Skartheim.** **Bill wins** (#338–339).
- Bill **refuses to keep a weapon he didn't earn.** Odin has the dwarves **forge Stormbreaker** for Bill, with the same enchantment as Mjolnir (#339–340).
- **Malekith the Accursed**, lord of the **Dark Elves**, steals **the Casket of Ancient Winters.** Opened on Earth, it would bring **endless winter** (#344–349).
- **Algrim the Strong** is betrayed by Malekith and **turned into Kurse**, a nearly unstoppable warrior sworn to revenge (#347–349).
- **Surtur is the power behind all of it** (#340 on). He's seeded here.

**Changes and why:**
| Change | Why |
|---|---|
| Beta Ray Bill and Malekith (1983–84) in one film | Simonson's first year, told as one film |
| Thor's opponent in the 60-second fight is female | Thor has always been female in our universe. The fight is otherwise the same. |
| The Blake identity continues (on 616 it ended during Simonson's run) | It's held until the Phase 3 Thor film for story reasons |

## 4. STORY
| Field | Entry |
|---|---|
| **Logline** | A stranger with a horse's face and a hero's heart lifts Thor's hammer, and to save both their worlds from the Dark Elves' eternal winter, the god of thunder has to share the storm. |
| **Theme** | **Worthiness isn't one person's property.** Anyone can be worthy, even a monster. |
| **Villain / antagonist** | **Malekith the Accursed** (main); **Kurse** (tragic); **Surtur** (behind it all) |

### What the movie is about
**ACT ONE: THE STRANGER**
- **Monday, May 20, 2013.** **Nick Fury** reaches Thor, now an Avenger, about an **unknown warship** crossing the orbit of Mars.
- Thor boards it. Its pilot, a towering, horse-faced cyborg named **Beta Ray Bill**, fights back. In the fight, Mjolnir is knocked away and **sixty seconds pass.** Thor turns back into **Dr. Blake** in the middle of the battle.
- Bill picks up the cane, **strikes it**, and **becomes a Thor.**

**ACT TWO: THE TRIAL**
- Odin summons "Thor" home, and **Bill arrives.** Asgard is stunned.
- Bill explains that his people, the **Korbinites**, are the last survivors of a galaxy **burned by the demons of Surtur.** He needs Mjolnir to save them.
- Odin orders a **trial by combat** on **Skartheim**, a world of fire. It's **Thor against Bill**, and **Bill wins.**
- Bill **refuses to take the hammer from her.** Odin, moved, has the dwarves forge **Stormbreaker.** Bill has his own hammer now, and Thor has a brother-in-arms.

**ACT THREE: THE CASKET OF ANCIENT WINTERS**
- **Malekith the Accursed** steals the **Casket of Ancient Winters** from Odin's vault and sacrifices his lieutenant **Algrim**, who is reborn as the monster **Kurse.**
- He takes the Casket to **Earth** and opens it over **Manhattan**, and **New York freezes in July.**
- **Thor and Beta Ray Bill** fight side by side through a frozen city, Mjolnir and Stormbreaker, two thunderstorms against the cold.
- **Kurse** is freed from Malekith's control, **turns on his master**, and takes Malekith with him into the void.
- Thor seals the Casket. The ice melts.
- Bill heads back to the Korbinites with Stormbreaker. At the Bifrost, Thor clasps his arm: *"Brother."*
- Thor and **Jane** sit on a Manhattan rooftop in the thaw, their first real vacation.
- Odin, alone, looks into the fire: ***Surtur is awake.***
