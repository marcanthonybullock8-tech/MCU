### 3A. COMIC ACCURACY REPORT
| Field | Entry |
|---|---|
| **Overall accuracy score** | **97%.** The Claremont/Miller series is adapted issue for issue, with Logan's debut as the prologue. |

**Straight from the page:**
- **Prologue:** in the Canadian wilderness, a government agent codenamed **Weapon X / Wolverine** is sent to stop **the Hulk** and **the Wendigo** (*Hulk* #180–181).
- **Mariko Yashida**, the woman Logan loves, has been **forced into an arranged marriage** by her father, the crime lord **Lord Shingen Yashida** (*Wolverine* #1).
- **Logan is drugged, then beaten by Shingen with a wooden bokken** in a duel, and **thrown out** (#1–2).
- **Yukio**, a reckless assassin, is sent to kill him and **falls for him** (#2–3).
- **The Hand**, a clan of ninja assassins, works for Shingen (#3).
- **Logan comes back at full strength, kills the Hand's ninjas, and kills Shingen in a real duel** (#4).
- **Mariko**, freed, **accepts Logan's proposal** (#4).
- **Flashbacks:** **James Howlett**, 1880s Canada (*Origin*); **the Weapon X program** bonding adamantium to his skeleton (*Weapon X*).

**Changes and why:**
| Change | Why |
|---|---|
| His 1974 debut becomes the prologue | It gives Logan an **MCU entrance** against a character audiences already know |
| Shingen's death is **shown carefully** for PG-13 | The rating. The duel is intact. |

## 4. STORY
| Field | Entry |
|---|---|
| **Logline** | The best there is at what he does goes to Japan for the woman he loves, and loses to her father with a stick. He has to become more than an animal to win her back. |
| **Theme** | **The animal and the samurai.** Logan is at war with himself. Honor is the only thing that can make him a man. |
| **Villain / antagonist** | **Lord Shingen Yashida** (main); **the Hand** |

### What the movie is about
**PROLOGUE: THE WENDIGO**
- **The Canadian Rockies, Monday, April 24, 2017.** A government agent in a yellow-and-blue suit, **Wolverine**, parachutes in to stop the **Wendigo**, and finds **the Hulk** fighting it too.
- Three-way fight. The Hulk throws Logan through a forest **six times.** Logan keeps getting up.
- **HULK:** *"Why won't little man stay down?!"*
- It ends in a draw. Logan lights a cigar.

**ACT ONE: JAPAN**
- Logan's letters to **Mariko Yashida** have been coming back unopened. He goes to **Tokyo.**
- Her father, **Lord Shingen**, a crime lord, has **forced her to marry** one of his allies.
- Shingen **drugs Logan** and duels him with **a wooden sword.** **Logan loses** in front of Mariko.
- He's thrown out, humiliated.

**ACT TWO: YUKIO**
- A wild, reckless assassin named **Yukio** is sent to kill him. They end up drinking together.
- Flashbacks: **James Howlett**, a sick boy in 1880s Canada; **the Weapon X program**, the adamantium, the screaming.
- The **Hand**, Shingen's ninja army, comes for him. The berserker rage takes over, and afterward Logan looks at the bodies, ashamed.
- Yukio: *"You're an animal."* Logan: *"I'm trying not to be."*

**ACT THREE: HONOR**
- Logan **goes back**, clear-headed, at full strength, and in control.
- **Logan vs. Shingen**: a real duel with real swords in the Yashida garden. **Logan wins.**
- **Mariko is free.** She accepts his proposal.
- The last line, over a sunset in Kyoto: ***"I'm the best there is at what I do. But what I do best isn't very nice."***
