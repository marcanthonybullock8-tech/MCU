## 4. STORY
| Field | Entry |
|---|---|
| **Logline** | In one corner of Doom's world, Wanda Maximoff gave everyone exactly what they wanted, and one man with a healing factor and a long memory is going to take it all away. |
| **Theme** | **Grief and wishes.** If you could give everyone their heart's desire, would it still be a lie? |
| **Villain / antagonist** | **Magneto**; **Wanda's own grief** |

### What the movie is about
**ACT ONE: THE WISH**
- **Wanda's mind breaks.** Her powers **tear a city apart.** **The heroes meet** at Genosha: **do they kill her?**
- **Pietro** gets to her first: *"Give them what they want, Wanda. Give everyone what they want."*
- **A white flash.**
- **The House of M.** **Mutants rule.** **King Magneto.** **Princess Wanda.** Everyone is **happy.**
- **Logan wakes up** in a world he knows **isn't real.**

**ACT TWO: THE GIRL WHO KNOWS STUFF**
- **Layla Miller**, 12, finds Logan: *"I know stuff."* **Her touch brings memory back.**
- They **wake up the heroes**: **old Steve Rogers**, who **never got frozen** and **grew old with Peggy**; **Peter Parker**, who has **everything** and **begs to forget.**
- The awakened heroes **march on Genosha.**

**ACT THREE: NO MORE MUTANTS**
- **Magneto** learns **what Pietro and Wanda did**, and **kills his son.**
- **Wanda holds Pietro's body.** Then she looks at her father.
- ***"No more mutants."***
- **A second white flash.** **The domain's mutants lose their powers.** Magneto **falls to his knees, just an old man.**
- **Wanda walks off alone** into the rubble of the palace.
