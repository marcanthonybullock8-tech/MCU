### 3A. COMIC ACCURACY REPORT
| Field | Entry |
|---|---|
| **Overall accuracy score** | **96%.** It combines *X-Men* #1, #4 and #11. The costumes, codenames and the era-accurate versions of the powers are exact. |

**Straight from the page:**
- **Xavier's School for Gifted Youngsters, 1407 Graymalkin Lane, Salem Center, Westchester County, NY.**
- The five original X-Men: **Cyclops, the Beast, Iceman, the Angel, and Marvel Girl**, who **arrives as the new student in #1.** They wear **matching blue-and-gold training uniforms.**
- **Magneto attacks the Cape Citadel missile base** in #1.
- **The Brotherhood of Evil Mutants**: Magneto, **Mastermind**, **the Toad**, **Quicksilver** and **the Scarlet Witch**. They take over the South American republic of **San Marco** (#4).
- **The twins serve Magneto out of a debt:** he saved Wanda from a mob in their Eastern European village (*X-Men* #4). **They don't know he's their father** (on 616 they learn this in 1982), and they're reluctant members.
- **Iceman's first form is a SNOWMAN** (#1), and **the Beast is human-looking with enormous hands and feet** (1963). The blue fur comes in 1970.
- **Jean's telepathy has been blocked by Xavier** (616). In this film **she only uses telekinesis**, as in the early comics.
- **The Stranger**, a vast cosmic being, **appears out of nowhere and takes Magneto and the Toad** into space (#11). **With Magneto gone, the twins walk away.**

**Changes and why:**
| Change | Why |
|---|---|
| *X-Men* #1, #4 and #11 (1963–65) are condensed into one film | Runtime. Magneto's arc runs from his first attack to his removal. |
| Bolivar Trask (1965) appears early | It sets up the Sentinels and the anti-mutant climate that defines the franchise. |
| Iceman's actor is older than 616's youngest X-Man | Casting Rules. See Section 2. |

## 4. STORY
| Field | Entry |
|---|---|
| **Logline** | In a world that cheers for gods and armored heroes, five teenagers born with extraordinary powers are feared for them. Their teacher's dream of peace is tested by the one man who knows him best. |
| **Theme** | **Born, not made.** The Avengers chose their powers. Mutants didn't, and the world hates them for it. |
| **Villain / antagonist** | **Magneto** and **the Brotherhood of Evil Mutants** |

### What the movie is about
**ACT ONE: THE NEW STUDENT**
- **Westchester County, Monday, September 12, 2011.** A teenager named **Jean Grey** arrives at **Xavier's School for Gifted Youngsters.** Four students are waiting: **Scott, Hank, Bobby and Warren.**
- The **Danger Room**, the blue-and-gold uniforms, the codenames. **Professor Xavier** tells the class that the world isn't ready for them, so they'll protect it anyway.
- On TV, **Dr. Bolivar Trask** argues on a news panel that heroes like Iron Man are one thing, but **mutants are a different threat altogether.**

**ACT TWO: THE BROTHERHOOD**
- **Magneto** attacks the **Cape Citadel** missile base in Florida and takes control of its missiles.
- The X-Men, on their **first mission**, fight him to a standstill. They save the base, and Magneto escapes.
- The **Brotherhood of Evil Mutants** gathers: **Mastermind**, **the Toad**, and the reluctant twins **Pietro and Wanda Maximoff**, who owe Magneto because he saved Wanda from a mob that wanted to burn her.
- In a quiet scene, Wanda asks Magneto why he protects them. He looks at her far too long before answering: *"Because you are my people."*

**ACT THREE: SAN MARCO**
- The Brotherhood takes over the small South American republic of **San Marco** as a **mutant homeland.**
- The X-Men go in. Mastermind's illusions, Quicksilver's speed and Wanda's hexes nearly beat them.
- Wanda is ordered to kill Cyclops, and she refuses.
- Magneto **raises a destroyer out of the harbor** to crush the X-Men.
- Before it falls, a **towering figure made of starlight** appears in the sky: **the Stranger.** Everything stops. The Stranger looks at Magneto like a specimen, and **takes him and the Toad**, gone in a flash. The destroyer falls back into the sea.
- Xavier and his students are left in the silence. The twins are free.
