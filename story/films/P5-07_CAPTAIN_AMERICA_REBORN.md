### 3A. COMIC ACCURACY REPORT
| Field | Entry |
|---|---|
| **Overall accuracy score** | **96%.** *The Death of Captain America* and *Reborn* are adapted beat for beat, pressed into one film. |

**Straight from the page:**
- **Steve Rogers surrenders** to face federal charges, and **is shot on the courthouse steps** (#25).
- **Crossbones** is the sniper; **Sharon Carter**, under **Doctor Faustus's** conditioning, fires the last shots **without knowing she's doing it** (#25–26).
- **Steve dies** (as far as the world knows). **The funeral**, the mourning, and **Tony at the grave** (*Fallen Son* #5).
- **Steve's letter** to Tony asks him to **keep Bucky safe**. Tony **gives Bucky the shield**, and **Bucky becomes Captain America** (#33–34).
- **The Red Skull** is sharing **Lukin's body**, and uses **Sin, Crossbones, Zola and Faustus** to attack America **from inside** (#25–42).
- **Sharon's gun** was built from **Doom's technology.** Steve was **frozen outside time**, not killed (*Reborn* #1).
- **Steve relives his own life**, is **aware of it**, and **changes nothing**, because he **mustn't** (*Reborn* #2–4).
- **The Skull puts his mind into Steve's body**. **Steve fights him from inside** (*Reborn* #5).
- **The Skull ends up in a Zola robot body**, and **Sharon destroys it** (*Reborn* #6).
- **Steve tells Bucky to keep the shield** (*Reborn* #6).

**Changes and why:**
| Change | Why |
|---|---|
| Steve surrenders over **exposing the Illuminati's classified operations**, not over the Superhuman Registration Act | MCU continuity. **The Registration fight ended in *Civil War* (2016)**; the 2026–27 fight is over the Illuminati. The **courthouse and the shooting** are the same. |
| **Sharon's pregnancy** (616: she's pregnant, and loses the baby in *Reborn*) is **kept, but told quietly**: she tells Steve after he's back. **The baby is lost in the off-screen gap, and the film says so.** | 616 accurate, and handled with care at PG-13 |
| **Doom sells the time technology to the Skull directly**, not through Norman Osborn's Cabal | **Norman Osborn isn't in play** in the MCU. Doom's reason is his own: he wants **data on surviving outside time** (see the post-credit scene). |
| The two comics' story is **pressed into one film** | Runtime. The 616 gap between Steve's death and *Reborn* was **2 years (2007–09)**; in the MCU it's **12 weeks.** |

## 4. STORY
| Field | Entry |
|---|---|
| **Logline** | The man who exposed the Illuminati is shot on the courthouse steps. The world buries Captain America; Bucky Barnes picks up the shield; and Steve Rogers is still alive, trapped somewhere in his own past. |
| **Theme** | **What does Captain America mean without Steve Rogers?** |
| **Villain / antagonist** | **THE RED SKULL** (main); **Sin, Crossbones, Arnim Zola, Doctor Faustus** |

### What the movie is about
**ACT ONE: THE STEPS**
- **Monday, November 16, 2026.** Steve has **exposed the Illuminati**, and **he broke the law to do it.** He **surrenders** to face charges, because **he believes nobody is above the law, including him.**
- **9:42 a.m.**, the steps of the federal courthouse in Lower Manhattan. **A shot from a rooftop** (Crossbones). Steve **pushes Sharon out of the way.** Then **Sharon, eyes blank, fires three more times.**
- **Steve Rogers is pronounced dead at 10:17 a.m.**
- **The funeral at Arlington.** Tony, alone at the grave after everyone's gone: *"It wasn't worth it. None of it was worth it."*
- **Steve's letter**: *"Look after Bucky. And if the shield has to go on... give it to him."*

**ACT TWO: THE NEW CAPTAIN AMERICA**
- **Bucky refuses.** Tony: *"He asked for you. I'm just the mailman."*
- **Bucky's first mission as Captain America**, with **a pistol on his hip** (616): *"I'm not Steve. I never will be."*
- **The Skull's plan**: **Sin** and **Crossbones** create chaos in American cities; **Faustus** conditions agents; **the Kronas Corporation** (Lukin's) buys up America's debt.
- **Sharon**, free of Faustus, finds out **the gun wasn't a gun.** It was **Doom's technology.** ***"He's not dead. He's stuck."***

**ACT THREE: REBORN**
- **Steve, unstuck in time**, relives his life: **Project: Rebirth**, **Bucky in 1944**, **Peggy**, **the Howling Commandos**, and **the ice**, over and over. **He knows it's happening. He can't change anything.**
- **The Skull's real plan** is to **pull Steve back into the present and take his body.**
- **The Skull does it.** For one terrible scene **Steve's face smiles the Skull's smile** in front of the whole nation.
- **Inside Steve's mind**, on a **1944 battlefield**, **Steve beats the Skull** (Hugo Weaving) with his bare hands.
- **The Skull flees into a Zola robot body**, and **Sharon destroys it.**
- **Steve is back.** Bucky holds out the shield. **Steve: *"You keep it, Buck. Right now the country needs you."***
- **The last scene:** Steve, in plain clothes, looks at **the two Earths in the sky** and calls **Maria Hill.** *"The Illuminati. I want every one of them found."*
