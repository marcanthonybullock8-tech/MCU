## 4. STORY
| Field | Entry |
|---|---|
| **Logline** | The last survivors of the old universe have woken up on a planet ruled by God Doom, and the only man who remembers what happened is Doom's own sheriff. |
| **Theme** | **Memory is rebellion.** |
| **Villain / antagonist** | **GOD DOOM** (main); **the Cabal** |

### What the movie is about
**ACT ONE: THE RAFT**
- **Reed, Peter, Carol, Shuri, Namor, Thor and Cyclops** have been **hiding on Battleworld** since the Raft opened.
- **Reed sees Sue** on a giant screen: **Doom's queen**, holding **Doom's children.** **His children.**
- **Strange** finds them. ***"I've been waiting for you."***

**ACT TWO: THE BARONS**
- **The rebellion**: **Baron Tony Stark**, **Steve Rogers**, **Bucky and the Hulk**, **Magneto** and **Bishop**, **Old Man Logan.** **They don't remember.** **The Raft does.**
- **Thor joins the Thor Corps** to **turn it from inside.**
- **Peter** finds **a machine** that makes him **a new black suit**, and **it moves.**
- **Doom attacks.** **Owen Reece drops a mountain range** on the rebels, and **the Hulk holds it up** while **Reed** gets everyone out.

**ACT THREE: THE SHERIFF**
- **Strange** goes to Doom's throne **alone** and tells him **the truth in front of his family.**
- **Doom kills Strange.** **Sue sees it.**
- **Wanda** stands at Doom's side, and **won't look away.**
- **Valeria** watches **her father's face** (the mask), and **asks a question.** *"Who was he talking about, Father? Who's Reed?"*
