## 4. STORY
| Field | Entry |
|---|---|
| **Logline** | Disgraced and hunted, Tony Stark follows the name of a world-destroying god to Latveria, and ends up stranded in Camelot with the one man he hates most. |
| **Theme** | **Pride.** Tony and Doom are the same man, and only one of them can admit it. |
| **Villain / antagonist** | **DOCTOR DOOM** (main); **Morgan le Fay** |

### What the movie is about
**ACT ONE: THE GOD-DOOM**
- **Monday, February 8, 2027.** The Illuminati are **disgraced**, and **Steve Rogers is back and hunting them.** Tony is **living off the grid** in a Stark safehouse in Montana. **Pepper** is running the company without him.
- **Tony has one lead.** Every **Black Priest** captured says the same name before they die: ***"Rabum Alal."*** *The god-doom.* Every trail leads to **Latveria.**
- **Tony goes to Latveria alone.** Doom lets him in. They talk, and it's one of the best scenes in the saga: two geniuses **who have never been in the same room.**
- The talk turns into **a fight**, and the fight **hits the time platform.**

**ACT TWO: CAMELOT**
- **Tony wakes up in the 6th century**, with a broken suit, **at the court of King Arthur.**
- **Doom wakes up beside Morgan le Fay**, and **Morgan knows exactly what he is.**
- Tony **rebuilds a suit out of forge iron**, working with Merlin, and it looks exactly like **medieval Iron Man.**
- **Morgan and Doom raise the dead** and march on Camelot.
- **Merlin**, blind and ancient, tells Tony: *"The masked one isn't of this age, and he isn't of yours. I've seen the end of every world, and his face is at the end of all of them."*

**ACT THREE: THE ROUND TABLE**
- **The siege of Camelot.** Tony and Arthur's knights against the army of the dead.
- **Tony vs. Doom**, single combat, **and Tony wins** (#150).
- **Morgan betrays Doom** for Camelot's throne. **Doom and Tony**, both beaten, **need each other** to get home.
- They **rebuild the time platform**, two geniuses working side by side **for one night.** It's the most honest they'll ever be. Tony: *"We could have been friends."* Doom: *"No. Doom does not have friends. Doom has equals. You are close."*
- **The present.** Doom goes back to Latveria; Tony goes home **with the answer**: in the ruins of Camelot, Tony found **Merlin's last carving**: **the Black Priests' symbol, and a mask.** ***Rabum Alal is Doom.***
