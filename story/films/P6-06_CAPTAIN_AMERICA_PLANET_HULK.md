## 4. STORY
| Field | Entry |
|---|---|
| **Logline** | Doom's greatest gladiator and his dinosaur are sent into a frozen country full of Hulks to rescue the only friend Steve Rogers has left. |
| **Theme** | **Loyalty.** Steve will cross hell for Bucky, even on God Doom's orders. |
| **Villain / antagonist** | **THE RED KING** (main); **God Doom** |

### What the movie is about
**ACT ONE: THE ARENA**
- **The Killiseum.** **Steve Rogers**, Doom's champion, **undefeated**, fights **beside a red T. rex.**
- **Doom** gives him a mission: **Bucky Barnes** is **the Red King's prisoner** in **Greenland.** *"Bring him back, and you'll be free."*

**ACT TWO: GREENLAND**
- **A frozen, green country** full of **Hulk tribes.**
- **Steve and Devil Dinosaur** cross it, and **Devil** becomes **his friend.**
- **The Red King**, **the biggest Hulk** in Greenland, rules from a **throne of ice.**

**ACT THREE: THE RED KING**
- **Steve vs. the Red King.** **Devil Dinosaur** saves Steve's life.
- **Bucky** is **alive**, and **he's the reason** the Red King's people rebelled.
- Steve **refuses Doom's freedom.** He stays with **Bucky**, **Devil**, and **the Hulks** who follow him now.
