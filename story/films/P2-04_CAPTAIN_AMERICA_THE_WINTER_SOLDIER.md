### 3A. COMIC ACCURACY REPORT
| Field | Entry |
|---|---|
| **Overall accuracy score** | **97%.** Brubaker's "Winter Soldier" is adapted almost issue for issue. |

**Straight from the page:**
- **Bucky survived the 1945 explosion.** He lost his left arm and was pulled out of the sea by a **Soviet submarine.** The Soviets gave him a **cybernetic arm**, **brainwashed him**, and **kept him in cryogenic storage between missions** for decades as **the Winter Soldier** (#1–8).
- **General Aleksander Lukin**, ex-Soviet and head of the **Kronas Corporation**, was the Winter Soldier's last handler (#1).
- **Lukin gets the Cosmic Cube** (#1).
- **The Winter Soldier assassinates the Red Skull**, whose consciousness escapes **into the Cube** and then **into Lukin's mind** (#1–7).
- **The Winter Soldier attacks Philadelphia** (#3).
- **Natasha Romanoff trained alongside the Winter Soldier in the Red Room** (Brubaker). They **have a past together** (616).
- **Cap uses the Cosmic Cube to give Bucky his memories back.** Bucky remembers who he is, is horrified by what he's done, and **shatters the Cube** before vanishing (#14).
- **The Falcon**, Sam Wilson, is Cap's partner (#117). **Sharon Carter, Agent 13**, is Peggy's niece (616).

**Changes and why:**
| Change | Why |
|---|---|
| The Red Skull's body comes from the WWII stasis chamber (the *Captain America* mid-credit scene) | MCU continuity. On 616 the Skull had come back decades before 2005. |
| The Cube comes from A.I.M. (the *Captain America* post-credit scene) | MCU continuity. On 616 A.I.M. built the Cube. |
| Crossbones (1989) appears in the Brubaker story | He's part of Brubaker's run (2005–07) |

## 4. STORY
| Field | Entry |
|---|---|
| **Logline** | A Soviet ghost story turns out to be true. The assassin who has shaped the last fifty years of history is the best friend Captain America buried in 1945. |
| **Theme** | **You can't freeze the past.** Steve and Bucky both lost decades. Only one of them remembers. |
| **Villain / antagonist** | **Aleksander Lukin / the Red Skull** (main); **the Winter Soldier** (a victim who's been turned into a weapon); **Crossbones** |

### What the movie is about
**ACT ONE: THE GHOST**
- **Monday, January 13, 2014.** Cap splits his time between the Avengers and S.H.I.E.L.D. strike missions, working with **Agent 13, Sharon Carter**, and with **Natasha Romanoff**, who has **defected to S.H.I.E.L.D.** since the end of *The Avengers*.
- He visits **Peggy Carter**, old and fading, in a D.C. care home. She's Sharon's aunt.
- **The Red Skull's stasis bunker** in Bavaria is raided. The Skull's body is taken, and hours later **he's assassinated** by a masked gunman with a **metal arm.**
- Natasha goes pale. In the Red Room they had a name for him: **the Winter Soldier.**

**ACT TWO: THE WINTER SOLDIER**
- The trail leads to **General Aleksander Lukin** and his **Kronas Corporation**, who bought **the Cosmic Cube** from A.I.M.'s remnants.
- At the moment of the Skull's death, **his mind went into the Cube, and from the Cube into Lukin.** Lukin hears a second voice in his head now.
- Cap meets **Sam Wilson**, a counselor at the VA and a former pararescueman, and **the Falcon** joins him.
- **The Winter Soldier attacks Philadelphia**, a bombing that kills hundreds and is blamed on S.H.I.E.L.D.
- On a burning overpass, **Cap and the Winter Soldier fight.** The mask comes off. **It's Bucky.** *"Who the hell is Bucky?"*
- **Natasha** tells Steve everything. She trained alongside Bucky in the Red Room and loved him, before they wiped his mind again.

**ACT THREE: THE CUBE**
- Lukin, **half Red Skull now**, plans to use the Cube to remake the world.
- Cap, the Widow, the Falcon and Agent 13 **storm Kronas's Siberian facility.**
- **Cap vs. the Winter Soldier, round two.** Cap drops his shield: *"I'm not going to fight you. You're my friend."*
- Cap **takes the Cosmic Cube** and uses it for one thing only: **"Remember."**
- **Bucky remembers.** Every mission, every face, 1945, and Steve. He's horrified.
- He **shatters the Cube** with his metal fist.
- **The Red Skull's voice screams inside Lukin**, then goes quiet. Lukin is taken into custody. Crossbones escapes.
- **Bucky disappears into the Siberian snow.**
- Steve stands at Peggy's bedside. She doesn't know him today. He holds her hand anyway.
