## 4. STORY
| Field | Entry |
|---|---|
| **Logline** | The heroes who exiled the Hulk into space have been destroying whole worlds in secret. He's come back to put them on trial. |
| **Theme** | **Judgment.** Who judges the people who decide which worlds live or die? |
| **Villain / antagonist** | **The Illuminati** (the Hulk's targets); **Miek** (the truth) |

### What the movie is about
**ACT ONE: 24 HOURS**
- **Monday, June 1, 2026.** **Cap has exposed the Illuminati** to the world. They exiled the Hulk, and **they've destroyed other Earths.**
- The Hulk has been hiding in the desert since *Avengers Forever*. He hears the news, and **he comes to New York.**
- **The Warbound** land beside him, with Korg leading.
- ***"You have 24 hours to surrender."***

**ACT TWO: THE SIEGE**
- **Black Bolt** screams, and **the Hulk gets back up.**
- **Iron Man in the Hulkbuster** loses badly.
- **Reed Richards** loses too.
- **Doctor Strange**, desperate, **calls on the demon Zom**, and **loses.**
- **Madison Square Garden:** the Hulk makes the Illuminati **fight each other.**
- **Xavier** stands in front of him: *"I refused to join them, Bruce."* **The Hulk believes him.**

**ACT THREE: THE SENTRY**
- **The Sentry** comes out of the sky, and the two fight **across Manhattan.**
- **Miek** screams the truth: ***"The Red King's loyalists set the bomb. I knew, and I let it happen."***
- **The Hulk stops.** For a long moment, **he doesn't know who to hate.**
- **Stark's satellites** hit him from orbit, and **the Hulk falls.**
- Banner, in a cell, alone. **Rick Jones** comes to visit him.
