**Straight from the page:**
- **The Illuminati** (Iron Man, Mr. Fantastic, Doctor Strange, Namor, Professor X and Black Bolt) **trick the Hulk onto a shuttle** and **launch him toward a peaceful planet.** **Black Bolt votes no. Namor votes no** (*Illuminati* #1).
- The ship **goes off course** and crashes on **Sakaar**, a savage world under **the Red King.**
- The Hulk is made a **gladiator.** He forms the **Warbound**: **Korg** (a Kronan), **Miek** (an insectoid), **Hiroim** (a Shadow priest) and **Elloe Kaifi.**
- **Caiera the Oldstrong**, the Red King's bodyguard, **switches sides.**
- **The Hulk overthrows the Red King**, becomes **king of Sakaar**, and **marries Caiera.** Sakaar calls him **"the Green Scar."**
- **The exile ship's warp core explodes**, killing **Caiera** and **millions** of Sakaarans (#105).
- The Hulk, crazed with grief, **turns toward Earth.** **"World War Hulk"** begins.

**Changes and why:**

## 4. STORY
| Field | Entry |
|---|---|
| **Logline** | Earth's smartest heroes trick the Hulk into a rocket and launch him into space. He crashes on a savage world, becomes its gladiator, then its king, then loses everything, and turns back toward home. |
| **Theme** | **The Hulk finds a home, then loses it.** The monster was never the problem. The people afraid of him were. |
| **Villain / antagonist** | **The Red King** (main); **the Illuminati** (whose choice starts it all) |

### What the movie is about
**ACT ONE: THE EXILE**
- **Monday, August 14, 2017.** The **Illuminati** meet in secret: Tony Stark, Reed Richards, Stephen Strange, Namor, Charles Xavier and Black Bolt. **Four vote to exile the Hulk.** Namor and Black Bolt refuse.
- They trick the Hulk onto a S.H.I.E.L.D. shuttle. Tony's recorded message plays as the shuttle launches: *"I'm sorry, big guy."* The Hulk tears the cockpit apart screaming.
- The ship goes off course and **crashes on Sakaar.**

**ACT TWO: THE GREEN SCAR**
- The **Red King's** gladiator games. The Hulk kills the arena's champions.
- The **Warbound**: **Korg**, **Miek**, **Hiroim**, **Elloe.**
- The Red King sends **Caiera the Oldstrong** to kill him. She's the most dangerous warrior on Sakaar, and she **sees what the Red King is doing to her world.** She switches sides.

**ACT THREE: THE KING**
- **The revolution.** The Hulk overthrows the Red King and becomes **king of Sakaar.** He **marries Caiera.**
- For a moment, the Hulk is **happy.** Banner doesn't come out for months. He doesn't need to.
- **Saturday, April 14, 2018:** the old exile shuttle's **warp core explodes** in the capital. **Caiera** dies in his arms, along with **millions of Sakaarans.**
- The Hulk looks at the wreckage and finds **Stark Industries markings.**
- He boards a warship with the surviving Warbound: ***"Take me to Earth."***
