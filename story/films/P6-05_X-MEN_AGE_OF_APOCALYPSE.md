*Casting notes:*
- ***Oscar Isaac** plays **Apocalypse** and **Moon Knight**, the same precedent as Laurence Fishburne and Brian Tyree Henry. **The two never share a scene.** Apocalypse is a full prosthetic and performance-capture role.*
- ***Ian McKellen** is locked as Magneto. The Age of Apocalypse's Magneto is a man in his prime, so he's **digitally de-aged** (the SLJ precedent). **McKellen performs every dramatic scene**; the action uses doubles and his de-aged face.*

*In memoriam: Stan Lee (1922–2018), who co-created the X-Men (*X-Men *#1, 1963). The X-Men's hidden base, **Wundagore**, has **one wall of names** of the dead. **The first name is "Charles."** The last line on it, by itself, reads **"Excelsior."***

| Change | Why |

| **Gambit and the X-Ternals** are **left out** | Runtime |

## 4. STORY
| Field | Entry |
|---|---|
| **Logline** | In a world where Charles Xavier died before he founded the X-Men, Magneto leads them against Apocalypse, and one soldier who remembers a better world will find out his world was never real at all. |
| **Theme** | **Legacy.** Magneto became the man his friend wanted him to be, in a world that should never have existed. |
| **Villain / antagonist** | **APOCALYPSE** (main); **Mister Sinister**; **Dark Beast** |

### What the movie is about
**ACT ONE: ALPHA**
- **The prologue**: **David Haller** goes back in time to kill Magneto, and **Xavier dies** saving him.
- **The Age of Apocalypse.** North America is **Apocalypse's.** Humans are **culled.**
- **Magneto's X-Men**, **Rogue at his side.** A strange soldier, **Bishop**, keeps **talking about someone called "Xavier."**

**ACT TWO: THE WAR**
- **The X-Men split** into teams: **Weapon X and Jean** go to the **Human High Council**; **Nightcrawler** and **Blink** search for the **M'Kraan Crystal**; **Cyclops** turns against **Sinister.**
- **The Council's nuclear strike** is coming. **Humanity** and **mutants** both **run out of time.**

**ACT THREE: OMEGA**
- **Magneto vs. Apocalypse.** **Apocalypse** breaks Magneto's body, and **Magneto tears Apocalypse in half** (*Omega*).
- **Bishop enters the Crystal**, to go back and **undo this world.** And inside, **there is no past.** **This world began less than two years ago.** **A masked face** looks back at him from **inside the Crystal**: **Doom's.**
- **Bishop comes back.** **The nuclear strike** hits, and **Blink** saves who she can.
- **Bishop tells Magneto the truth.** Magneto, beaten and bleeding, looks up at **the statue of Doom** on the horizon.
