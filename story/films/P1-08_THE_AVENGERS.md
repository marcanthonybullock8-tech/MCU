### 3A. COMIC ACCURACY REPORT
| Field | Entry |
|---|---|
| **Overall accuracy score** | **96%.** It adapts *Avengers* #1, #3 and #4 in order, plot point for plot point. |

**Straight from the page:**
- **Loki, exiled on the Isle of Silence**, uses his **astral form** to get revenge on Thor. He **makes a railroad track vanish** in front of a speeding train. **The Hulk saves the train and is blamed for wrecking it** (#1).
- **Rick Jones** and his ham-radio friends, the **Teen Brigade**, send out a call for help. **Loki reroutes it to Thor**, but **Iron Man, Ant-Man and the Wasp pick it up too** (#1).
- **The Hulk hides in a circus disguised as "Mechano the Robot"** (#1). Yes, really.
- Thor chases Loki to Asgard. **Ant-Man and the Wasp trap Loki** in a **lead-lined container**, and Thor sends him back to exile (#1).
- **The Wasp names the team:** *"It should be something colorful and dramatic... like 'the Avengers'!"* (#1)
- **Hank Pym becomes Giant-Man** (#2).
- **The Hulk quits**, since his teammates don't trust him, and **joins Namor** against the Avengers. **The battle at Gibraltar** (#3).
- **Namor** comes across people **worshipping a figure frozen in ice** and throws the block into the sea. **It drifts into the warmer Gulf Stream and thaws**, and **the Avengers' submarine** picks up **Captain America.** He wakes up shouting ***"Bucky!"*** (#4)
- **Cap joins the Avengers** in the Hulk's place (#4).
- **Tony gives the team 890 Fifth Avenue**, **Avengers Mansion**, with **Edwin Jarvis** running it (616).

**Changes and why:**
| Change | Why |
|---|---|
| Rick's radio call goes out to **"any hero out there"** instead of the Fantastic Four | The FF haven't been introduced in the MCU yet. Their debut is kept for their own film in Phase 2. |
| #1, #3 and #4 (September 1963 to March 1964) happen within about five weeks | The MCU calendar |
| Cap and Namor's WWII history as Invaders is played up | It pays off the *Captain America* montage |

## 4. STORY
| Field | Entry |
|---|---|
| **Logline** | A god's revenge brings Earth's mightiest heroes together by accident. What keeps them together is a choice, and at the bottom of the ocean they find the man who will lead them. |
| **Theme** | **Assembly.** Heroes are strong alone and unstoppable together. The hardest part is **trust.** |
| **Villain / antagonist** | **Loki** (Acts 1 and 2); **Namor**, with **the Hulk** (Act 3) |

### What the movie is about
**ACT ONE: THE COMING OF THE AVENGERS**
- **The Isle of Silence.** Loki's astral form leaves his chained body and searches Midgard for a weapon to use against Thor. He finds **the Hulk** (the *Thor* post-credit scene pays off).
- **The Arizona desert, Monday, March 19, 2012.** A railroad track **disappears.** The Hulk **catches the train**, and the news reports that he wrecked it.
- **Rick Jones** defends him over his ham radio: *"Any hero out there, please..."*
- Loki redirects the call to **Dr. Donna Blake**, and **Iron Man, Ant-Man and the Wasp** also pick up the signal.
- The heroes meet in the desert as strangers. They find the Hulk **hiding in a traveling circus as "Mechano the Robot."** It's the funniest set piece in Phase 1, and it turns into a four-way brawl.
- **Thor sees Loki's trick for what it is** and goes after him to Asgard.

**ACT TWO: ASSEMBLED**
- In Asgard, Loki's trolls and illusions hold off Thor. On Earth, **Ant-Man and the Wasp** work out where Loki's astral form will come back to Earth.
- They lure Loki into a **lead-lined trap** at ant size. Thor sends him back to the Isle of Silence.
- In the desert, five exhausted heroes stand together. Iron Man says they work well as a team. **The Wasp: *"It should be something colorful and dramatic... like 'the Avengers'!"***
- **Avengers Mansion.** Tony gives the team **890 Fifth Avenue.** **Jarvis** serves tea. **Fury** arrives with a charter and a S.H.I.E.L.D. liaison, and **the Avengers Initiative** becomes real.
- **Hank debuts as Giant-Man.**

**ACT THREE: THE MAN IN THE ICE**
- The team doesn't trust the Hulk. Tony wants him monitored and Hank wants a tranquilizer, and **Bruce hears them.**
- **The Hulk quits** and walks out into the night.
- In the North Atlantic, **Namor** has come back from decades of exile at sea. He's angry at the surface world and **recruits the Hulk.**
- **The Battle of Gibraltar:** the Avengers vs. the Hulk and Namor. The Hulk and Thor fight each other **to a standstill**, and the Hulk **leaps away** undefeated and alone.
- Furious, Namor comes across **Inuit hunters on the Greenland ice worshipping a figure frozen in a block of ice.** Disgusted, he **throws it into the sea.**
- The block drifts into the **Gulf Stream** and starts to thaw.
- The Avengers' submarine hauls it aboard. A man in a star-spangled uniform opens his eyes: ***"BUCKY!"***
- **Captain America** wakes up in 2012, 67 years later.
- **Namor leads Atlantis** against **New York Harbor.** Within the hour, **Cap leads the Avengers** into battle.
- On a sinking pier, Cap faces his old Invaders comrade.
  > **CAP:** *"We fought side by side, Namor. Don't make me fight you now."*
- Namor looks at him for a long time. Then he **orders Atlantis to withdraw.**
- **Avengers Mansion, night.** Cap stands before the team. Fury gives him the shield, recovered from the sea floor. **The Avengers ask him to join.**
- The final rallying cry. Cap raises the shield: ***"Avengers... ASSEMBLE!"***

**EPILOGUE**
- A dive bar in Brooklyn. **Claire Barton** and **Natasha Romanoff** watch the Avengers' first press conference on the TV above the bar.
  > **CLAIRE:** *"Think they're hiring?"*
  > **NATASHA** *(sipping)*: *"Everyone's hiring. The question is whether they'll have us."*

## 6. WORLD-BUILDING
### The film's own world
- **The Avengers**: the name, the Mansion, Jarvis, the S.H.I.E.L.D. charter, and **"Avengers Assemble!"**
- **The team's weak point is trust.** The Hulk leaves, and the team doesn't forget it.
- **Cap as leader.** A man out of time leads a team from a century he doesn't recognize.

### World-building for the wider MCU
