### 3A. COMIC ACCURACY REPORT
| Field | Entry |
|---|---|
| **Overall accuracy score** | **96%.** It combines three origin issues. Every major beat comes from the page. |

**Straight from the page:**
- **Hank shrinks himself with his new formula**, is trapped in an **anthill**, fights off soldier ants, and survives with help from a friendly ant (*TtA* #27).
- **The costume and the cybernetic helmet** let him **talk to and command ants** (*TtA* #35).
- **Hank is still grieving his first wife, Maria Trovaya**, a Hungarian scientist killed behind the Iron Curtain (*TtA* #44).
- **Dr. Vernon van Dyne**, a scientist in **Cresskill, New Jersey**, fires a gamma-beam signal into space to contact other worlds. It brings **an alien criminal from the planet Kosmos** to Earth, and it **kills him** (*TtA* #44).
- **Janet**, grieving and furious, asks Hank for help. He **grafts wasp cells** into her so that when she shrinks she **grows wings** (*TtA* #44).
- **Hank notices how much Janet looks like Maria**, and that complicates his feelings from the start (616).
- **Egghead**, the disgraced government scientist **Elihas Starr**, is Hank's **archenemy** (*TtA* #38).

**Changes and why:**
| Change | Why |
|---|---|
| Egghead's role in bringing the Kosmosian to Earth | In 1962–63 they were separate villains. Combining them tightens the film, and each keeps their 616 personality. |
| The Wasp's stings come from bio-electric energy she generates herself | Early on she used a sting gun. We use the **later 616 version** of her powers, per the Bible's rule on using the latest 616 version of a detail. |
| Hank's age is **27** (b. **December 5, 1981**) | Matches Tyler |

## 4. STORY
| Field | Entry |
|---|---|
| **Logline** | A grieving scientist who can shrink to the size of an ant and a fearless heiress who wants revenge for her father become Earth's smallest heroes. |
| **Theme** | **Size isn't power.** Small people and small moments change the world. It's also about grief, and learning to love again. |
| **Villain / antagonist** | **Egghead** (Elihas Starr) and **the Kosmosian** |

### What the movie is about
**ACT ONE: THE MAN IN THE ANT HILL**
- **New York, Monday, June 8, 2009.** **Dr. Hank Pym**, 27, a brilliant biochemist, lives in his lab. He still wears his late wife **Maria's** wedding ring.
- The science world is buzzing about his "Pym Particles" paper, and Tony Stark called it "show-off" work in *Iron Man*.
- Testing the formula on himself, he **shrinks to half an inch**, gets knocked out a window, and ends up **inside an anthill**, where he's hunted by soldier ants and saved by one friendly one.
- He survives and gets obsessed. He builds a **cybernetic helmet** that talks to ants, and **Ant-Man** is born.

**ACT TWO: THE WASP**
- **Dr. Vernon van Dyne**, an astrophysicist in **Cresskill, New Jersey**, brings Hank in to consult on his project: **a gamma-beam signal to other worlds.**
- There Hank meets Vernon's daughter **Janet**, a quick, funny, fearless fashion designer (the woman Tony met at the Maria Stark Foundation gala). Hank is shaken. **She looks like Maria.**
- **Egghead**, Vernon's disgraced ex-partner, has been **boosting the signal** in secret. Something answers.
- **A creature from the planet Kosmos** comes through the beam and **kills Vernon.**
- Janet tells Hank she wants revenge and asks him to help her get it. Hank grafts **wasp cells** into her. She shrinks, **grows wings**, and **flies.**

**ACT THREE: THE HIVE**
- Egghead captures the Kosmosian in a gamma cage, plans to sell it as a weapon, and jams Hank's ant frequency.
- **Ant-Man and the Wasp** team up for the first time. The fight moves up and down in scale across Egghead's Hudson River lab, and the Wasp wins the aerial fight in the rain.
- The creature escapes and is sent back through the beam to Kosmos. Egghead is arrested, and he swears revenge.
- Janet and Hank are sitting on the edge of a dollhouse roof, half an inch tall. She asks what they should call themselves. He says, *"Partners?"* She kisses him.
