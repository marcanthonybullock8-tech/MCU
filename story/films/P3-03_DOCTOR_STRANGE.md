**Straight from the page:**
- **Dr. Stephen Strange** is a **brilliant, arrogant neurosurgeon.** A **car crash** wrecks the nerves in his hands (#115).
- He spends his fortune on cures that fail, and travels to the **Himalayas** to find **the Ancient One** (#115).
- **Baron Mordo**, the Ancient One's student, **is secretly trying to kill his master.** Strange finds out, and Mordo **puts a spell on him to keep him quiet** (#115). *(Mordo is a villain from the start, as on 616.)*
- Strange **gives up his selfishness** and becomes the Ancient One's student to protect him (#115).
- **The Cloak of Levitation**, **the Eye of Agamotto**, **the Sanctum Sanctorum, 177A Bleecker Street**, **Wong.**
- **Dormammu**, lord of the **Dark Dimension**, attacks. **Clea**, Dormammu's niece, helps Strange (#126).
- **Strange beats Dormammu**, not by force but by getting him to **swear** to leave Earth alone (#127).

## 4. STORY
| Field | Entry |
|---|---|
| **Logline** | A genius surgeon loses his hands and his future, and finds the other side of reality instead, where the thing waiting for him is hungrier than any god. |
| **Theme** | **Ego and surrender.** Stephen thinks he can fix everything. Magic teaches him that the world doesn't revolve around him. |
| **Villain / antagonist** | **Dormammu** (main); **Baron Mordo** |

### What the movie is about
**ACT ONE: THE CRASH**
- **Monday, November 9, 2015.** **Dr. Stephen Strange**, the best neurosurgeon in New York, crashes his Lamborghini on a mountain road. **His hands are ruined.**
- Every surgery fails. His money runs out.
- He goes to the Himalayas to find a legend: **the Ancient One.**

**ACT TWO: KAMAR-TAJ**
- **The Ancient One** and **Wong** take him in. Stephen doesn't believe in magic until he's **thrown out of his own body.**
- He learns the mystic arts: the **Cloak of Levitation**, the **Eye of Agamotto**, the astral plane.
- He finds out that **Baron Mordo**, the Ancient One's top student, is **trying to kill him.**
- Mordo **binds Strange with a spell** so he can't warn anyone.
- Strange chooses **to stay and protect his master** anyway. **That's the moment he becomes a hero.**

**ACT THREE: THE DARK DIMENSION**
- Mordo turns to **Dormammu**, lord of the **Dark Dimension**, for power.
- Dormammu attacks Earth through the **Sanctum Sanctorum** on **Bleecker Street.**
- Strange goes **into the Dark Dimension.** A young sorceress, **Clea**, Dormammu's niece, secretly helps him.
- Strange and Dormammu: a hopeless fight. Strange **tricks Dormammu into swearing** by his own power never to harm Earth again.
- Dormammu is **bound by his oath** and leaves, raging.
- Strange comes home to the Sanctum with Wong. He looks at his scarred, trembling hands, which are strong enough now to hold the world together.
