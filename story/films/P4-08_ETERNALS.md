- **A million years ago, the Celestials** experimented on early humans and created **the Eternals** (immortal and powerful) and **the Deviants** (monstrous and unstable) (*Eternals* #1).
- **The Fourth Host**: the Celestials **return to judge Earth**, and **Arishem** stands in judgment for **fifty years** (*Eternals* #1–19).
- **Kro**, the Deviant warlord (#1).
- **Sprite**, eternally a child, **erased the Eternals' memories** so they'd live as ordinary humans (Gaiman #1–7). She resents being a child forever.
- **Thanos** is one of the **Eternals of Titan**, and **Eros/Starfox** is his brother (*Iron Man* #55).

## 4. STORY
| Field | Entry |
|---|---|
| **Logline** | Gods who don't remember they're gods wake up to find that the Celestials who made them have come back to judge the Earth, and something out there is killing the Celestials first. |

| **Villain / antagonist** | **Kro and the Deviants**; **Sprite** (a betrayal); **Arishem** (judgment, not evil) |

### What the movie is about
**ACT ONE: FORGOTTEN GODS**
- **Monday, January 8, 2024.** A London schoolteacher, **Sersi**, turns a coffee cup into glass without meaning to. A man who can **fly** comes to find her: **Ikaris.**
- **Sprite** made them forget everything, **and the spell is breaking.**

**ACT TWO: THE FOURTH HOST**
- The **Celestials** arrive. **Arishem**, a mile tall, stands over the Andes to **judge Earth.**
- **Kro and the Deviants** come out of the ground to wipe out humanity before the judgment ends.
- The Eternals **remember** who they are and **reunite** across the world.

**ACT THREE: JUDGMENT**
- The Eternals beat Kro's army.
- **Sersi** speaks to Arishem **on humanity's behalf.** He **withholds judgment**, and then **looks up toward space** as if he's heard something.
- **A second Celestial falls dead** out of the sky over the ocean, killed by something unseen.
- Arishem **leaves in a hurry**, without a verdict.
- The Eternals look at the corpse of a god.
