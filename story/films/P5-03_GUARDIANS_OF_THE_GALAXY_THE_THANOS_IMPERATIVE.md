## 4. STORY
| Field | Entry |
|---|---|
| **Logline** | A universe where nothing can die is invading ours, and the only one who can stop it is the Mad Titan, who the Guardians have to pull off his farm and trust. |
| **Theme** | **Death gives life meaning.** A universe without death is a cancer. |
| **Villain / antagonist** | **Lord Mar-Vell** and **the Many-Angled Ones**; **Thanos** (their reluctant ally) |

### What the movie is about
**ACT ONE: THE FAULT**
- **Monday, October 7, 2024.** A tear in space, **the Fault**, opens near Kree space. Out of it pour **immortal armies** from **the Cancerverse**, a universe where **nothing can die.**
- **Richard Rider, Nova**, and the Nova Corps can't kill them.
- **Warlock**: *"There's only one being in this universe that Death loves more than any other. We need Thanos."*
- The Guardians go to **Thanos's farm.** **Gamora** stands at the gate. **Thanos puts down his hoe.**

**ACT TWO: THE CANCERVERSE**
- An uneasy alliance: **Thanos and the Guardians** go **through the Fault.**
- **The Cancerverse** is a universe of **endless, rotting life.**
- **Lord Mar-Vell**, a twisted mirror of the hero who died in peace, rules for **the Many-Angled Ones.**
- **Drax** can't stand fighting beside Thanos. He **attacks**, and **Thanos kills him with one blow.**
- **Gamora screams.** Thanos, cold: *"He was made to kill me. He finally tried."*

**ACT THREE: THE IMPERATIVE**
- Thanos, **Death's champion**, faces Lord Mar-Vell and **breaks the Cancerverse's hold.** **The Cancerverse begins to collapse.**
- **Star-Lord and Nova stay behind** to make sure **Thanos can't escape.**
  > **STAR-LORD:** *"Get everybody home, Rocket. We've got this."*
- The Fault closes. **Star-Lord and Nova are gone.**
- On the other side, alone, Thanos **walks out of the rubble**, unhurt. **Death** is waiting. **She touches his face**, for the first time ever, **and then she's gone.**
