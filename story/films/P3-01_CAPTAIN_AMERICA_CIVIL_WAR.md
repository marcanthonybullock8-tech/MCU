### 3A. COMIC ACCURACY REPORT
| Field | Entry |
|---|---|
| **Overall accuracy score** | **95%.** *Civil War* #1–7 is adapted in order. The cast is our MCU's heroes instead of every 616 hero of 2006, but they're on the sides 616 put them on. |

**Straight from the page:**
- **Stamford, Connecticut:** a young team, **the New Warriors**, filming a reality show, attacks the villain **Nitro**. He explodes and **kills more than 600 people, 60 of them children at an elementary school** (#1). *This is shown with restraint, as the aftermath, and not graphically.*
- **The Superhuman Registration Act**: every superhuman must **register their identity with the government** and work under it (#1).
- **Tony Stark leads the pro-registration side.** **Cap refuses**, escapes a S.H.I.E.L.D. strike team, and goes underground with the **Secret Avengers** (#1–2).
- **Spider-Man unmasks on live TV** at Tony's urging (#2).
- **Tony, Reed and Hank clone Thor** from a strand of hair. The clone, **"Ragnarok,"** **kills Bill Foster/Goliath** in the first battle (#4).
- **Sue and Johnny leave Reed** to join Cap (#4–5). **Ben leaves for France** (*FF* #538).
- **Spider-Man switches to Cap's side** after seeing **Prison 42** in the Negative Zone (#5).
- **The Punisher** joins Cap's side and **kills two villains.** **Cap throws him out** (#5–6).
- **Black Panther** joins Cap (#6–7).
- **The final battle in Times Square.** **Cap is beating Tony.** Then first responders tackle him, and **Cap sees the destruction around him**: *"We're not fighting for the people anymore... we're just fighting."* **He surrenders** (#7).
- **Tony becomes Director of S.H.I.E.L.D.** (#7).

**Changes and why:**
| Change | Why |
|---|---|
| The heroes are our MCU cast | On 616 every hero was involved. The film uses ours, on their 616 sides. |
| Thor is off-world, and the clone stands in for her | On 616, Thor was "dead" at the time (after *Ragnarok*, 2004). In our MCU she's in **Asgard fighting Surtur's forces**, which ties to the Thor film. |
| Wanda and Pietro start neutral | On 616 in 2006, Wanda was missing after *House of M*. She's neutral in the film and then **chooses Cap.** |

## 4. STORY
| Field | Entry |
|---|---|
| **Logline** | After a disaster kills 600 people, the government orders every hero to register. Captain America says no, and Iron Man hunts him for it. The Avengers break in two, just as the universe's greatest threat is about to arrive. |
| **Theme** | **Freedom vs. security.** Both sides are right, and that's the tragedy. |
| **Villain / antagonist** | **Nobody, and everybody.** Cap and Iron Man are both right. **Nitro** starts it. **Ragnarok** kills. Behind all of it is **the question itself.** |

### What the movie is about
**ACT ONE: STAMFORD**
- **Monday, January 11, 2016.** Stamford, Connecticut. The young **New Warriors**, filming a reality show, go after the villain **Nitro** near an elementary school. **He explodes.** **600 people are dead, 60 of them children.**
- The nation turns on its heroes. Congress passes the **Superhuman Registration Act.**
- **Tony Stark**, haunted by Stamford, **backs it.** **Cap refuses:** *"It's the government telling heroes who to fight."*
- S.H.I.E.L.D. tries to arrest Cap on a Helicarrier. He jumps out without a parachute and goes underground.

**ACT TWO: WHOSE SIDE ARE YOU ON?**
- **The Secret Avengers:** Cap, the Falcon, Hawkeye, Goliath, and eventually the Scarlet Witch, Black Panther, Sue and Johnny Storm.
- **Tony's side:** Black Widow, Yellowjacket, the Wasp, the Vision, War Machine, Mr. Fantastic and Spider-Man.
- Tony convinces Peter to **unmask on live TV**: *"I'm Peter Parker, and I've been Spider-Man since I was fifteen."*
- **The first battle.** The Secret Avengers walk into a trap, and Tony, Reed and Hank bring out **"Ragnarok," a clone of Thor.** It's Thor's face, and it's empty. **Ragnarok kills Goliath.**
- Horrified, **Sue Storm** leaves Reed and takes Johnny with her. **Ben Grimm** refuses to fight his friends and **leaves for France.**
- Peter sees **Prison 42** in the **Negative Zone**, where registration's resisters are being held without trial, and **switches sides.**
- **The Punisher** joins Cap and **executes two villains.** Cap beats him down and throws him out: *"We're not like you."*
- **Bucky**, back in the shadows, helps Cap in secret.

**ACT THREE: TIMES SQUARE**
- The final battle through **Times Square**, every hero on both sides.
- **Cap vs. Iron Man.** Cap tears the helmet off. **He's winning.**
- A group of **first responders** (firemen, cops and paramedics) tackles Cap to the ground.
- Cap looks around at the wrecked city and **the civilians running.**
  > **CAP:** *"We're not fighting for the people anymore. Look at us. We're just fighting."*
- **He surrenders.**
- **Tony becomes Director of S.H.I.E.L.D.** He won. On a Helicarrier over New York, he's never looked so alone.
- Cap, in handcuffs and a prison jumpsuit, is led into a cell. **Bucky** watches from a rooftop across the street.
