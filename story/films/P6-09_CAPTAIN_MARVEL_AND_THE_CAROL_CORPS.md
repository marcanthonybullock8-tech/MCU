## 4. STORY
| Field | Entry |
|---|---|
| **Logline** | The last Captain Marvel lands in a domain of fighter pilots who worship her legend without knowing she's real, and teaches them to fly past the edge of God's map. |
| **Theme** | **Higher, further, faster.** Question everything, even God. |
| **Villain / antagonist** | **GOD DOOM** and **the Thor Corps** |

### What the movie is about
**ACT ONE: HALA FIELD**
- **Carol**, from the Life Raft, **crash-lands** in **Hala Field**: **a domain of pilots.** **Helen Cobb** pulls her out of the wreck.
- **The Banshee Squadron** has a **legend** they tell recruits: **"Captain Marvel," the pilot who flew past the sky.**

**ACT TWO: THE EDGE OF THE MAP**
- **Doom's law** says **no one flies past the lines.** **The pilots** start asking why.
- **Carol** teaches them. **They fly past the lines**, and **find nothing: no world, no history, just white.**
- **The Thor Corps** comes to **punish them.**

**ACT THREE: THE CAROL CORPS**
- **The pilots vs. the Thor Corps.** **Carol goes Binary.**
- **Thor**, from **inside the Corps**, **turns on her captains.** ***"We were made to serve justice. Not him."***
- **The Thors** turn, one by one.
- **Hala Field** and **the Thor Corps** fly toward **Doomstadt.**
