## 4. STORY
| Field | Entry |
|---|---|
| **Logline** | The Avengers are hunting the Illuminati. The Cabal is destroying worlds. Doom has gone to kill God. And in four months the sky over New York will be another Earth. |
| **Theme** | **Time runs out.** There's no right answer left, only the people you choose to save. |
| **Villain / antagonist** | **THE CABAL** (Namor, Thanos) and **the BEYONDERS**; **Doom** looms over everything |

### What the movie is about
**ACT ONE: THE HUNT**
- **Monday, August 2, 2027.** **Steve Rogers** leads the **Avengers** and **S.H.I.E.L.D.** (Maria Hill) in a **manhunt for the Illuminati.**
- **Earth-1610** fills the sky, **four and a half months from impact.**
- The **Illuminati**, in hiding, are building **a Life Raft**: Reed's lifeboat to **survive the end.**
- **The Cabal** has moved **to another universe's Earth** and is **destroying worlds** to save its own.
- **Tony** tells the Illuminati: *"Rabum Alal is Doom."* Reed goes pale.

**ACT TWO: GODS DIE**
- **The Beyonders** kill **the Celestials**. Their corpses fall **across the sky.**
- **Doom, Strange and the Molecule Man** disappear **beyond the multiverse.** Tony: *"He's going to kill them. And then he's going to take what they had."*
- **Namor**, sick of the Cabal, **traps it on a dying Earth** and **blows it up**, then **comes home.** **Shuri** won't forgive him.
- **The Cabal survives.** **Thanos** fights **Thor and the Hulk** together.
- **Carol** holds back **the other Earth** for **four seconds.**

**ACT THREE: EVERYTHING DIES**
- **Thursday, December 16, 2027.** The last day.
- **Steve and Tony** fight **at last**, in the ruins of the Triskelion. **Steve wins, hand to hand.** Then **the Helicarrier falls on them both.**
- **On 1610**, **Miles Morales** watches **Earth-616 fill the sky.** The Maker smiles.
- **Reed's Life Raft** launches with **a handful of heroes**: **Reed, Shuri, Namor, Carol, Peter Parker, Thor, Cyclops, and the Black Swan.**
- **Sue and the children don't make it.** Reed screams her name.
- **11:59 p.m.** Two Earths **touch.** **Everything goes white.**
- **TITLE CARD: *"SECRET WARS."***
