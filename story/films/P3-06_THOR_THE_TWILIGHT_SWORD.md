## 4. STORY
| Field | Entry |
|---|---|
| **Logline** | A fire demon forges a sword to burn the universe. Thor, her lying brother and her dying father have to stand together, and one of them won't come home. |
| **Theme** | **Legacy.** Odin's love for his children, and what a king owes his kingdom. |
| **Villain / antagonist** | **Surtur** |

### What the movie is about
**ACT ONE: THE BURNING GALAXY**
- **Monday, August 7, 2017.** **Beta Ray Bill** crash-lands in Asgard with the last Korbinite refugees. **Surtur** is coming.
- Odin frees Thor from the mortal Blake identity: *"You are no longer Donna Blake. You are Thor."* **Blake's life ends.** Jane is heartbroken, and she understands.
- **Surtur's demons attack Manhattan.** The **Avengers** (Cap, the Vision, the Wasp, Hawkeye) fight them in the streets, and Thor arrives in a storm.

**ACT TWO: FOR ASGARD**
- **Loki comes to Thor with an offer:** if Surtur burns the universe, Loki burns with it. *"Just this once, sister."*
- Surtur finishes forging **the Twilight Sword** in Muspelheim.
- **Skurge the Executioner** holds the Bifrost gate alone against a demon army until Thor gets there.

**ACT THREE: DOOMSDAY**
- **Surtur** steps onto the Bifrost with the Twilight Sword.
- **Odin, Thor and Loki** stand together: ***"FOR ASGARD!"***
- Thor fires her first **God Blast.** It isn't enough.
- **Odin**, with the full Odinforce blazing around him, **grapples Surtur and pulls him into the abyss.**
- *"My children... be what I could not."*
- **Odin is gone.** Surtur is gone. Asgard stands.
- Thor, now **the ruler of Asgard**, sits on her father's throne. Loki watches from the shadows, **for once not smiling.**
