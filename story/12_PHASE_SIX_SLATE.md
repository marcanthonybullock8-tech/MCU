## THE PHASE 6 ARC: "SECRET WARS" (Hickman, 2015–16; Shooter, 1984–85)
- **The multiverse is dead.** What's left is **Battleworld**: a patchwork of **domains** cut from dead realities, **ruled by barons** and **policed by the Thor Corps**, with **Doctor Strange as Sheriff.**
- **God Emperor Doom** sits on a throne at the top of **the World Tree**, with **Sue Storm as his queen** and **Franklin and Valeria as his children.** Everyone on Battleworld believes **it has always been this way.**
- **The Shield**, a giant wall, keeps out **the Deadlands** (zombies), **Perfection** (Ultrons) and **the Annihilation Wave.**
- **The Life Raft opens** on **Tuesday, August 15, 2028**, eight months after the end (*Secret Wars* #2). **The survivors of Earth-616** are the only people who **remember what came before.**
- **The ending (the saga's two-part finale):** **Reed Richards rebuilds the multiverse**, and **Doom's face is healed.**

## PHASE 6 FILM #1: OLD MAN LOGAN
- **Character:** **Old Man Logan** of the Wastelands domain (Hugh Jackman).
- **Why:** it's **the perfect way into Battleworld.** Millar and McNiven's "Old Man Logan" (2008) became **a Battleworld domain** in *Secret Wars* (2015), and Bendis's tie-in walks Logan **across the borders to God Doom's law.** Jackman is **59**, the right age to play Logan **fifty years after the villains won.**
- **Release date: Friday, March 3, 2028**, eleven years to the day after ***Logan*** (real history).

## PHASE 6 FILM #2: SPIDER-MAN: RENEW YOUR VOWS
- **Character:** **Peter Parker** of the Regency domain, **married to MJ**, with **a daughter, Annie** (Tom Holland's fourth Spider-Man film).
- **Why:** ***Renew Your Vows*** (2015) is **Spider-Man's own *Secret Wars* story**: **the Peter who chose his family.** Our 616 Peter married MJ in 2022 and **lost her when the world ended**, so this is the life he'll never have. The post-credit scene is ***Secret Wars* #2**: **the Life Raft opens.**
- **Release date: Friday, July 7, 2028.** Independence Day week.

## PHASE 6 SERIES #1: GHOST RACERS (Disney+)
- **Character:** **Robbie Reyes / Ghost Rider**, with **Johnny Blaze.** First appearances: ***All-New Ghost Rider* #1** (2014) and ***Marvel Spotlight* #5** (1972).
- **Why:** the MCU's first **Ghost Rider** in a **Battleworld** series. ***Ghost Racers*** (2015) puts **every Ghost Rider** in a **death race** for **Doom's entertainment**, which shows **how God Doom keeps his world in line**: bread and circuses.
- **Schedule: 8 episodes, Tuesdays, October 3 to November 21, 2028**, 44–52 minutes each. **TV-MA.** The Halloween episode airs **October 31.**
