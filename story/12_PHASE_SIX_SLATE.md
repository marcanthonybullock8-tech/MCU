## THE PHASE 6 ARC: "SECRET WARS" (Hickman, 2015–16; Shooter, 1984–85)
- **The multiverse is dead.** What's left is **Battleworld**: a patchwork of **domains** cut from dead realities, **ruled by barons** and **policed by the Thor Corps**, with **Doctor Strange as Sheriff.**
- **God Emperor Doom** sits on a throne at the top of **the World Tree**, with **Sue Storm as his queen** and **Franklin and Valeria as his children.** Everyone on Battleworld believes **it has always been this way.**
- **The Shield**, a giant wall, keeps out **the Deadlands** (zombies), **Perfection** (Ultrons) and **the Annihilation Wave.**
- **The Life Raft opens** on **Tuesday, August 15, 2028**, eight months after the end (*Secret Wars* #2). **The survivors of Earth-616** are the only people who **remember what came before.**
- **The ending (the saga's two-part finale):** **Reed Richards rebuilds the multiverse**, and **Doom's face is healed.**

## PHASE 6 FILM #1: OLD MAN LOGAN
- **Character:** **Old Man Logan** of the Wastelands domain (Hugh Jackman).
- **Why:** it's **the perfect way into Battleworld.** Millar and McNiven's "Old Man Logan" (2008) became **a Battleworld domain** in *Secret Wars* (2015), and Bendis's tie-in walks Logan **across the borders to God Doom's law.** Jackman is **59**, the right age to play Logan **fifty years after the villains won.**
- **Release date: Friday, March 3, 2028**, eleven years to the day after ***Logan*** (real history).

## PHASE 6 FILM #2: SPIDER-MAN: RENEW YOUR VOWS
- **Character:** **Peter Parker** of the Regency domain, **married to MJ**, with **a daughter, Annie** (Tom Holland's fourth Spider-Man film).
- **Why:** ***Renew Your Vows*** (2015) is **Spider-Man's own *Secret Wars* story**: **the Peter who chose his family.** Our 616 Peter married MJ in 2022 and **lost her when the world ended**, so this is the life he'll never have. The post-credit scene is ***Secret Wars* #2**: **the Life Raft opens.**
- **Release date: Friday, July 7, 2028.** Independence Day week.

## PHASE 6 SERIES #1: GHOST RACERS (Disney+)
- **Character:** **Robbie Reyes / Ghost Rider**, with **Johnny Blaze.** First appearances: ***All-New Ghost Rider* #1** (2014) and ***Marvel Spotlight* #5** (1972).
- **Why:** the MCU's first **Ghost Rider** in a **Battleworld** series. ***Ghost Racers*** (2015) puts **every Ghost Rider** in a **death race** for **Doom's entertainment**, which shows **how God Doom keeps his world in line**: bread and circuses.
- **Schedule: 8 episodes, Tuesdays, October 3 to November 21, 2028**, 44–52 minutes each. **TV-MA.** The Halloween episode airs **October 31.**

## PHASE 6 FILM #3: SCARLET WITCH: HOUSE OF M
- **Character:** **Wanda Maximoff**, in **Grace Bullock's first solo film.**
- **Why:** ***House of M*** (2005) is **the definitive Scarlet Witch story**, and a Battleworld domain in 2015. It's a world built out of wishes **where only one man remembers the truth**, which is Battleworld in miniature. **"No more mutants."** The post-credit scene is **Doom and Wanda** (*The Children's Crusade*).
- **Release date: Friday, March 2, 2029.**

## PHASE 6 FILM #4: IRON MAN: ARMOR WARS
- **Character:** **Baron Tony Stark** of **Technopolis**.
- **Why:** 616's Tony died under the Helicarrier, but ***Armor Wars*** (2015) gives Battleworld **its own Tony**: a baron in a city where **everyone lives in armor**, fighting **his brother Arno**. It's joined to the 1987 "Armor Wars": **Tony destroys his own stolen tech.**
- **Release date: Friday, May 4, 2029.** Iron Man's first Friday in May.

## PHASE 6 FILM #5: X-MEN: AGE OF APOCALYPSE
- **Character:** **Magneto's X-Men**, and **Bishop**, the man who remembers a world with Xavier in it.
- **Why:** ***Age of Apocalypse*** (1995) is **the definitive alternate-reality X-Men saga**, and a Battleworld domain in 2015. **A world that shouldn't exist** fits the phase exactly. **Magneto tears Apocalypse in half.**
- **Release date: Friday, November 2, 2029.** Marvel's fall slot.

## PHASE 6 FILM #6: CAPTAIN AMERICA: PLANET HULK
- **Character:** **Steve Rogers**, Doom's gladiator, and **Devil Dinosaur**.
- **Why:** ***Planet Hulk*** (2015) sends **Steve and a T. rex** into **Greenland, the domain of the Hulks**, to rescue **Bucky** from **the Red King** (Amond Baker). It sets up the Hulk army for the war.
- **Release date: Friday, March 1, 2030.**

## PHASE 6 FILM #7: AVENGERS: BATTLEWORLD
- **Characters:** **the Life Raft's survivors** and **the barons' rebellion** vs. **God Doom**.
- **Why:** ***Secret Wars* #2–4** (2015): **Doom is God**, **Sue is his queen**, and **Doom kills Doctor Strange.** Plus the two iconic beats of **the 1984 *Secret Wars***: **the Hulk holds up a mountain range**, and **Spider-Man gets the black costume.**
- **Release date: Friday, May 3, 2030.** The Avengers' first Friday in May.

## PHASE 6 FILM #8: BLACK PANTHER: THE INFINITY GAUNTLET
- **Character:** **Shuri / Black Panther** (Letitia Wright), with **Namor**.
- **Why:** in ***Secret Wars* #5–8**, **Black Panther takes the Infinity Gauntlet** against Doom, and ***Infinity Gauntlet*** (2015) is the Battleworld domain where **the Gems** are. **Thanos** hunts them too.
- **Release date: Friday, November 1, 2030.** Marvel's fall slot.

## PHASE 6 FILM #9: CAPTAIN MARVEL AND THE CAROL CORPS
- **Character:** **Carol Danvers**, and **the pilots of Hala Field**.
- **Why:** ***Captain Marvel and the Carol Corps*** (2015) is the domain where **people start to question God.** **Thor turns the Thor Corps against Doom.** The last film before the finale.
- **Release date: Friday, March 7, 2031.**

## PHASE 6 FILM #10: AVENGERS: GOD EMPEROR DOOM (the finale, Part One)
- **Characters:** **every rebel on Battleworld** vs. **God Doom**.
- **Why:** ***Secret Wars* #5–8**: **the war for Battleworld.** **Doom kills Thanos.** **Shuri** strikes **Doom** with **the Gauntlet**, and **Doom stands up.**
- **Release date: Friday, May 2, 2031.**

## PHASE 6 FILM #11: SECRET WARS (the finale, Part Two)
- **Characters:** **Doctor Doom** and **Reed Richards**.
- **Why:** ***Secret Wars* #9**: ***"You would have done better."*** **Reed rebuilds the multiverse, and heals Doom's face.** Earth-616 returns, with everyone. **The saga ends.**
- **Release date: Friday, December 19, 2031.**

## PHASE 6 SERIES #2: MASTER OF KUNG FU (Disney+)
- **Character:** **Shang-Chi**, the Master of Kung Fu. First appearance: ***Special Marvel Edition* #15** (1973).
- **Why:** **the greatest martial artist in the Marvel Universe** has never appeared in the MCU. His ***Secret Wars*** tie-in, ***Master of Kung Fu*** (2015), puts him in **the K'un-Lun domain**, in a **tournament** against **his tyrant father, Zheng Zu**, a story built for weekly TV. **Simu Liu** is the only real actor to have played him.
- **Schedule: 8 episodes, Tuesdays, August 5 to September 23, 2031**, 45–55 minutes each. **TV-14.** It airs **between the two parts of the finale.**
